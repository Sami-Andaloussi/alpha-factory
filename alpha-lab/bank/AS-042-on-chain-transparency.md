## Mechanism

The mechanism does not say that every on-chain metric is predictive; it says that the crypto asset
class stands apart structurally through a public and granular information regime. Addresses, clusters,
flows, the distribution of holdings, block explorers, and sometimes governance or participation can be
observed almost in real time. This produces a source of repricing that does not exist in this form in
equities: the market can quickly revise its views on adoption, concentration, the health of the
network or custody flows.

The persistence comes from the very design of the public ledger.

## Prediction

The public and granular information regime of crypto produces a source of repricing that does not
exist in this form in equities: from information visible on public ledgers, almost in real time, the
market can quickly revise its views on adoption, concentration, the health of the network or custody
flows.

## What would refute it

- Changes visible on public ledgers in adoption, concentration, network health or custody flows do not
  move prices.
- Once exchanges, custodians and smart contracts are accounted for, on-chain information carries
  nothing about the economic behaviour of holders.
