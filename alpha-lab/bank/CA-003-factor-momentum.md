## Mechanism

The phenomenon is no longer only rotation between assets or countries, but between factors
themselves: value, quality, profitability, investment, low risk and others. Factors show a
persistence of performance that reduces neither to the momentum of individual stocks nor to industry
momentum, which suggests that the true economic units through which pricing propagates can be common
exposures rather than only individual securities.

The proposed economic mechanism is the slow reallocation of capital towards the styles and factors that
have recently done well, combined with the slowness with which investors update their view of the
macroeconomic or risk regimes that favour one factor or another. Fund managers are organised by style,
index and smart-beta products receive flows, and investors learn late which premium is currently being
paid. Factors thus become relative "assets" in their own right, with inertia. The mechanism is partly
structural and partly behavioural. The effect concerns factor sleeves, sectors with strong factor
loadings, countries with a dominant style, and even crypto-assets whose exposure to crypto factors
changes.

## Prediction

Factors with high recent returns go on to earn higher returns than factors with low recent returns,
a persistence distinct from stock-level and industry-level momentum.

## What would refute it

- Factors' past returns do not predict their subsequent returns.
- The persistence of factor returns is fully accounted for by stock-level momentum or by industry
  momentum.
