## Mechanism

The theory concerns the relation between crypto-assets and traditional financial markets, including
technology stocks and the stocks of firms tied to the crypto ecosystem. The idea is that, as crypto
becomes a more institutionalised segment, its shocks to returns and to volatility are transmitted
more to the rest of the financial system, and shocks travel from traditional markets to crypto as
well. The phenomenon is particularly visible in stress episodes, when correlations and spillovers
rise sharply.

The mechanism is not a shared fundamental value but a shared risk infrastructure: the same marginal
investors, the same liquidity constraints, the same margin calls, the same narratives of duration,
speculation and technological innovation, and in some cases direct economic ties, for the shares of
miners, exchanges, blockchain infrastructure firms and companies used as bitcoin proxies. This creates
complexes of assets in which an initial move in crypto can precede the adjustment of the exposed stocks,
or the reverse, depending on the regime. The effect concerns crypto spot, crypto-related equities and
technology or growth sectors.

## Prediction

Return and volatility spillovers between crypto-assets and traditional markets (technology stocks,
crypto-related stocks such as miners and exchanges) grow as crypto becomes more institutionalised,
and correlations and spillovers rise sharply in stress episodes. The institutional literature and
research papers show a growing interconnection, above all in risk-off phases, and a direction of
leadership that varies across periods: moves in crypto-assets lead those of the exposed stocks in
some periods and follow them in others.

## What would refute it

- Spillovers between crypto-assets and traditional markets do not grow as crypto becomes more
  institutionalised, and correlations do not rise in stress episodes.
- Crypto-related stocks (miners, exchanges, infrastructure firms, bitcoin proxies) are no more
  connected to crypto prices than other stocks with similar technology exposure.
- Neither market leads the other in any regime: moves in crypto-assets and in the exposed stocks are
  simultaneous.
