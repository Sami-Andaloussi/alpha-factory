## Mechanism

The January barometer is not a return anomaly over a short window but a predictive theory: the sign of the stock market's return in January would serve as a proxy for the regime of the following eleven months, or even of the whole year. The proposed mechanism is not that one month "causes" the year, but that January reveals a latent state of the market: appetite for risk, the dynamics of flows at the start of the year, confidence, and the macro-financial environment.

## Prediction

The sign of the market's January return indicates the regime of the following eleven months, or even of the whole year. The books present the relation as asymmetric: a positive January is more informative than a negative one, and years in which January is negative but the year ends positive tend to produce more modest gains.

Results outside the United States are mixed.

## What would refute it

- No relation between the sign of the January return and the regime or the return of the following eleven months.
- A negative January as informative as a positive one, which would contradict the asymmetry.
- January returns unrelated to measures of risk appetite, confidence, flows and macro-financial conditions, which would contradict the reading of January as a revealed state of the market.
