## Mechanism

The literature shows that crypto-asset returns share a common factor structure, dominated by a market
factor and completed by dimensions such as size and momentum. Crypto-assets are therefore not a
collection of entirely independent stories but parts of a common pricing system, and a relative-value
relation between bitcoin, the other major crypto-assets and the altcoins can be thought of from a factor
hierarchy, not only from opportunistic correlation.

## Prediction

The returns of crypto-assets share a common factor structure: they load on a common market factor,
which dominates, and on size and momentum dimensions.

## What would refute it

- Crypto-asset returns show no common factor structure: no dominant market factor, and no size or
  momentum dimension.
