## Mechanism

The operating fundamentals of a crypto network play the role of an income statement, a capital
expenditure budget and a security balance sheet. For a token to keep or increase its value, the
network must grow and remain secure: users, developers, hardware and validators, transaction volume,
hash rate, the distribution of security, and fees able to reward participants. The library stresses
that security is not abstract: it depends on the capital actually committed to the network and on
its degree of decentralisation.

The more the value of the network and its transactions rise, the stronger the incentives for miners
or validators, which reinforces security and can in turn support confidence and use. The circle can
be self-reinforcing, but it can also reverse if activity, price and fees contract.

## Prediction

A token keeps or increases its value only if its network grows and remains secure: users, developers,
hardware and validators, transaction volume, hash rate, the distribution of security, and fees able
to reward participants. As the value of the network and its transactions rise, the incentives of
miners or validators rise, which reinforces security and can in turn support confidence and use; the
circle can reverse when activity, price and fees contract.

## What would refute it

- Token values unrelated to users, developers, transaction volumes, security and fees.
- Security, measured by the capital committed to the network, that does not respond to changes in
  network value and fees.
- Contractions in activity, price and fees that leave the incentives of miners and validators, and
  the security of the network, unchanged.
