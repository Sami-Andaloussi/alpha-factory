## Mechanism

The theory applies the lead-lag logic to crypto-assets: some cryptocurrencies, in practice mostly the
largest, the most liquid and the most central for information, react first, and others follow with a
delay. Bitcoin is often described as the leading asset, with diffusion to the other major
crypto-assets and to the altcoins afterwards: a central asset transmits the information, and the
economically linked assets adjust more slowly.

In the crypto spot market, attention is highly concentrated, a few assets dominate as instruments for
discovering crypto-wide and macroeconomic information, the investor base is fragmented, liquidity is
asymmetric, listings follow a hierarchy, and tokens react at uneven speeds. Aggregate news about the
crypto risk regime, global liquidity, stablecoins, regulation or sentiment is often priced first in the
major coins and then propagated to the other assets. The mechanism proposed for bitcoin's lead is
structural: bitcoin's greater depth and liquidity, its role as an anchor of attention, and its dominance
as collateral and in the formation of expectations, followed by diffusion to smaller or more specialised
assets. The lag persists because trading is fragmented across exchanges, liquidity is uneven, retail
investors dominate, execution is constrained, and market regimes change.

## Prediction

Empirical work documents this cross-predictability between cryptocurrencies: lagged returns of other
cryptocurrencies, and of bitcoin in particular, predict the future returns of a given cryptocurrency,
the smaller and more specialised assets adjusting later. Bitcoin can lead the whole complex durably,
depending on the sub-universe of assets and on the frequency. Market regimes change, and the effects may
be sensitive to the universe of assets and to the role of the dominant exchanges.

## What would refute it

- Lagged returns of bitcoin and of the other major coins do not predict the returns of smaller
  cryptocurrencies.
- The predictability disappears once survivorship bias is removed.
- Smaller, less liquid tokens react to crypto-wide news at the same time as bitcoin and the other major
  coins, even where trading is fragmented across exchanges.
