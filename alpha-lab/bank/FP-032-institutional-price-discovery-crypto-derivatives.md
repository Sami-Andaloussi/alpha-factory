## Mechanism

The arrival, or the growth, of institutional participation through crypto derivatives changes
aggregate positioning, and the change is transmitted to the spot market. The channel is not simply
that derivatives lead prices. It concerns how investors who were absent can express a view, hedge
an inventory, arbitrage or cut risk through derivatives, which changes the net pressure on the spot
market. The introduction of new derivatives can thus create new flows of hedging, cash-and-carry,
basis trading and deleveraging.

The literature on the launch of Bitcoin futures has sometimes stressed the hypothesis that they made
it easier for institutions to express bearish views, or lowered frictions of access, with effects on
the spot price. Other work stresses instead the role of derivatives in price discovery and in making
the market more efficient. The common element is that the structure of the derivatives market makes
a previously latent positioning visible or executable, and this positioning feeds back into the cash
market through arbitrage and hedging. The derivative is an engine of orders in the underlying. Part
of the effect runs through complex arbitrage channels, and it can produce price moves that are not
purely informative about the fundamentals of adoption.

## Prediction

The introduction of new crypto derivatives, such as the Bitcoin futures listed by Cboe and CME, can
be followed by new flows of hedging, cash-and-carry, basis trading and deleveraging, and by changes
in spot prices and spot flows as previously latent views, possibly bearish ones, find expression.
Positioning built in derivatives is transmitted to the spot market through arbitrage and hedging,
and derivatives prices take part in price discovery for the underlying.

## What would refute it

- No change in spot price dynamics or spot flows after the introduction of major crypto derivatives.
- Positioning in derivatives that is never transmitted to spot through arbitrage or hedging flows.
- Derivatives prices that play no part in price discovery, spot prices moving first in every
  episode.
