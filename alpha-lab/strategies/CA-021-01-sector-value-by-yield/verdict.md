# CA-021-01 — Value by yield within the sector funds: verdict

**Stops at gate 2, economic edge. Refuted in this form: the third of the sector funds that paid the
most cash for their price over the past year earned 0.28% a year less than the eleven sector funds
held in equal parts from 2006 to 2022, and the clause, the alpha alone, is met.** Its appraisal
ratio, −0.040, is 0.7 to 1.2 standard errors below the predicted 0.12 to 0.24, and 0.2 below zero:
the run refutes the form the card locked, and cannot tell a vanished premium from the low end of the
long record's. The known part of the outcome, the rule's lasting preference for the utilities and
the staples, earned what the card estimated from published figures, and a little less, as it said it
would: held fixed, the rule's average weights earned +0.32% a year, against an estimate of about
+0.5% that leaned high. The monthly changes of holdings cost the rest, −0.60% a year: the yield's
timing within the high-yield sectors lost. Thresholds, version 4; the battery's figures are the
notebook's, [report.ipynb](report.ipynb), and the fixed mix, the variant against the same funds, the
correlation with CA-024-01's sector tilt, the alpha by year and each fund's months held are computed
apart from it, on the same sessions and costs. The theory is
[CA-021](../../bank/CA-021-value-sectors-countries-asset-classes.md).

## Gate by gate

1. **Hygiene: passes.** No look-ahead on 200 dates; the targets do not change when the prices and
   the distributions older than the declared memory of 1,530 sessions are scrambled, on 100 dates;
   33 decisions, once clustered, over 16.9 years in-sample, from the first holding on 2006-02-01,
   the count made before the card.
2. **Economic edge: fails.** A Sharpe ratio of 0.441, below the benchmark's 0.483, and an alpha of
   −0.28% a year; at twice the costs, 0.437 and −0.34%. Costs are 0.08% a year, 0.004 of a Sharpe
   unit.
3. **Significance: fails.** The probabilistic Sharpe ratio is 0.981; the rule beats 24.0% of 1,000
   placebos, its own weights shifted in time, where 90% are needed.
4. **Multiple testing: fails.** The alpha, as an appraisal ratio, is −0.040, against 0.629 expected
   from the best of seventy-five effective trials by luck: a deflated Sharpe ratio of 0.003.
5. **Stability: fails.** The blend of the base and the variant passes gates 2 and 3 but fails gate
   4. The base's alpha, each block with its own beta, is positive in four of its five blocks:
   2005–07 +0.72% a year, 2008–09 −3.95%, 2010–14 +1.29%, 2015–19 +2.09%, 2020–22 +2.55%; without
   its best year, 2022, it is −1.55% a year.
6. **Robustness: passes.** The neighbours, nine and fifteen months, six and eighteen, keep a median
   of 104% of the base's Sharpe ratio and at least 102% at ±25%; the largest share of the profit is
   XLU's, 29.3%, under the 30% the gate allows, where the reasoning had expected XLU's share to
   exceed it; a day's delay keeps 100%.
7. **Sealed holdout: fails.** Over 2023 to 2025, a Sharpe ratio of 0.21 and an alpha of −6.17% a
   year, below the tenth percentile of the in-sample paths (−5.04%).

## Verdict

The strategy stops at gate 2, and fails gates 3, 4, 5 and 7 as well.

## The card's refutation

Judged on the base, from its first holding on 2006-02-01, by its alpha over the eleven sector funds
held in equal parts, as gate 2 measures it: −0.28% a year (computed apart: −0.277%, an appraisal
ratio of −0.040, a beta of 0.99, a tracking error of 7.0% a year). *The alpha zero or less*: met.
The clause reads the alpha with one beta over the seventeen years, as gate 2 measures it. With a
beta for each of gate 5's blocks the alpha is positive in four of the five: the rule's beta was 1.17
in 2008–09, when it held the financials and the materials, and 0.83 in 2010–14 and 2015–19, when the
benchmark rose 15% and 9% a year, so that it carried the market most when the market fell and least
when it rose. That loss is the rule's own, part of the timing's −0.60% a year, and the clause stands
as locked. The theory is **refuted in this form**: the highest yields among the sector funds earned
no more than the sector funds from 2006 to 2022. The placebos' rank, 24.0%, is reported, not graded,
as the card said: the rule holds XLU in every month and XLP in most, and its placebos keep that mix.

**The test's power.** The card estimated that the clause would refute about half the time with no
edge, 31% of the time at the low end of the prediction and 16% at its top. Over 16.9 years an
appraisal ratio is measured to about ±0.24: −0.040 is 0.2 standard errors below zero, 0.7 below the
low end (0.12) and 1.2 below the top (0.24). The refutation is of this form over these years; the
run cannot tell a premium that vanished from one at the low end of the prediction.

**What was published.** The card estimated, from TM-002-01's published alphas for six of the funds,
that the base's average mix, held fixed, would earn about +0.5% a year over the equal weight, an
estimate that leaned high since the base holds technology and consumer discretionary, which led and
were not published, far under their share. Measured: +0.32% a year (an appraisal ratio of 0.067).
The rest of the base's alpha, −0.60% a year, is the timing: the months in which the rule moved its
weight between the high-yield sectors.

## What was learned

- **About the theory.** On the eleven sector funds from 2006 to 2022, value measured by the cash the
  funds pay — the highest trailing yields — earned less than the funds held in equal parts, −0.28% a
  year, within noise of zero and 0.7 standard errors below the low end of the prediction. What
  earned was the lasting preference for the utilities and the staples, known before the run from
  published figures; what lost was the rule's own timing. The variant, which takes out each sector's
  structural yield, earned +0.99% a year from 2011, within noise, and grades nothing. The card named
  the decade and the crises before the run: the 2010s together lost 3.3 points, about the sample's
  pace; 2008 and 2020 were the worst years, 24.8 points between them, and 2022, energy's, the best,
  17.4 points back. The result says that the yield ranking of the sector funds earned nothing from
  2006 to 2022; the country and asset-class forms of CA-021 were judged in the reasoning and not
  held, and the mechanisms the bank names — extrapolation, career risk — are not tested.
- **About the market.** From 2023 to 2025 the rule, holding the utilities, staples, energy and real
  estate in every month and no technology, earned an alpha of −6.17% a year against the sector
  funds, below the in-sample floor, in years in which technology led.
- **About the lab.** The first card to read the funds' distributions needed a reader of them, point
  in time, which two audits on the most capable model shaped: the first found it unbounded in memory
  and reading ahead of the bars, the second that it gave the paper job other sessions than the
  backtest, and its closing check that the tests guarding two of the fixes did not bite. The reader
  now gives each distribution on the session the bars as they stood confirm it, and a test fails
  when any of its guards is removed; on the bars as they stood, the sector funds' distributions are
  given on the backtest's session in 850 of 859, a session later in 9, none across a month's turn.
  SHY is not read. Its limits are in the runbook's step 6. The distributions are also a lesson in
  what the adjusted bars hold: a fund's steps are its cash, and a fund whose day spans a few cents
  cannot be read from them.

CA-021 is `tested-inconclusive`: its sector form by yield, the one form held, is refuted over 2006
to 2022 — the highest-yield sector funds earned 0.28% a year less than the sector funds held in
equal parts, their lasting preference for the utilities and the staples earning +0.32% a year and
the rule's monthly changes of holdings losing 0.60%; its country form was judged and not held, since
its ranking holds EFA in 189 of 203 months and CA-024-01's verdict published that outcome, and its
asset-class form is not testable on one fundamental. Its carry reading is CA-025's to take up, and
its spread CA-022's.
