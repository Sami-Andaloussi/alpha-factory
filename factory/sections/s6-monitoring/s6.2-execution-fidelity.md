# S6.2 — Live execution fidelity & operational TCA

## TL;DR

Measures the real quality of fills as they happen, compares it with what section 4 led the factory to expect, finds the cause of each gap, and proposes the cleanest immediate action.
Every gap is qualified before anyone acts: a probable cause, a confidence, a horizon; each action carries its expected gain, a time to live and its criteria of revert.
Its control directives go to the execution chain (S5.7); its digest and audit pack close the day.

## Purpose

Measure the real quality of fills live, compare it with the expected scenario that section 4 set,
attribute the cause of each gap, and propose the cleanest immediate action: slow down, reroute,
switch between passive and aggressive, or enter a degraded mode. This is the live watch of
[execution fidelity](../../glossary.md#execution-fidelity), and the factory's operational
[transaction cost analysis](../../glossary.md#transaction-cost-analysis).

**Success looks like:** the gap between expected and realised under control, avoidable costs
removed fast, and auditable proof that the right decision was taken at the right time.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The promotion record of S4.10 brings the expectations of execution that section 4 set with its
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) and cost analysis: bands per segment,
profiles in time, expected costs. The execution log of S5.7 brings the orders, acknowledgements,
cancels, fills, the states of [venues](../../glossary.md#venue) and router, the fees and the latencies; the as-of API of S1.8
the books and quotes as they were. The SLO catalogue of S6.1 gives the definitions; the monitoring
signals of S6.4 the [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime) of the market, for context. The KPI feeds of the execution pages bring
their own measures of fidelity.

The control directives go to the [execution chain](../../glossary.md#execution-chain) (S5.7), which
applies them in its algorithms. A degraded mode ordered here is a local mode of the execution chain,
a rung of the ladder that the [deployment rulebook](../../glossary.md#deployment-rulebook) of S5.9
lets this page activate. The page's decision log records each directive: it goes to stabilisation
(S6.5), which owns the ladder and journals every entry into a mode, and to lifecycle's system of
record (S7.2). The qualified gaps, as monitoring signals, go to
[intervention](../../glossary.md#intervention) (S6.7) when they are critical, and to the loop of
missions (S7.10) as tickets of recalibration. Its live [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) go to S6.1, and
to [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) detection (S6.4), which watches
[slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage), [markouts](../../glossary.md#markout) and the quality of
routing per venue and strategy. The audit pack of the day goes to S6.3. The end-of-day digest and
the changelog of expectations go back to section 4 as a backlog of deeper analyses, and to lifecycle
(S7), as feedback.

**Constraints.**
- A common schema of events, correlation IDs, normalised timestamps (source and known offset).

**Service levels:**
- From a fill to its KPI published: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ a fraction of the T50 of
  acknowledgement of the dominant venue, a relative constraint independent of any absolute figure.
- From a gap to its directive issued: T95 ≤ a fraction of the T50 of the opportunity's average
  lifetime, defined per segment.
- Critical events (suspensions, halts, changes of tick): ingested at T90 within the target delay;
  an alert when exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the contract of expectations is validated by section 4 (segments and bands);
- the canonical film of execution can be replayed (tests of invariants pass, the skew is within
  budget);
- the table mapping strategies to segments and to candidate policies exists.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the live KPIs are stable, and the detectors work, with a rate of false positives below its
  threshold;
- the actions applied by section 5 show a positive net gain;
- the audit pack is complete: a red case can be reproduced end to end.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the [markouts](../../glossary.md#markout) or the costs [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) against
  their bands, persistently, over several days;
- market rules or the microstructure change;
- new profiles of orders or new strategies arrive, or the internal latencies change;
- after any major incident, or a study of cost analysis in section 4 that concludes to a revision.

Within section 6, the [SLOs](../../glossary.md#slo) of [observability](../../glossary.md#observability) are updated when a bottleneck is found. Lifecycle
(S7) receives the tickets of recalibration, and the proposals to roll a policy forward or back.
Section 4 receives a backlog of deep analyses to improve the contract of expectations.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · The contract of expected execution: benchmarks and segments</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without knowing what to expect, the real cannot be judged.

**Actions.**
- Define the reference benchmarks (the arrival price and the
  [implementation shortfall](../../glossary.md#implementation-shortfall), intraday profiles,
  volume-weighted benchmarks) and the horizons of markout (+5 s, +30 s, +5 min, adapted to the
  latencies and the [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover)).
- Segment by strategy, type of order, relative size, [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) of the
  market and [venue](../../glossary.md#venue).
- Fix for each segment a band of expectation (a median and envelopes of risk), taken from section
  4's execution simulator and cost analysis.

**KPI.** Coverage of the contract of expectations ≥ 99% of the cases that can be mapped.

**Guardrail.** A test of the consistency of segments (no empty micro-segments); a check of the
stability of the bands on older data (no [overfitting](../../glossary.md#overfitting) to a single day).

**Output.** The contract of expected execution (tables and rules of mapping) → steps 3 and 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · The canonical film of execution: events, timestamps, lineage</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** A clean flow is needed before anything can be compared with the contract. The rules of
time and of joining are design; building the journal is execution.

**Actions.**
- Build the canonical journal from the execution log and the market: orders, acknowledgements,
  cancels, replaces, fills, quotes and level 2, states of venues and router, fees, latencies.
- Unify time (one reference, a known latency of the probes, a documented maximum skew) and make the
  journal replayable, [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time).
- Issue a correlation ID per intent of execution and per fill.

**KPI.** Events reconciled ≥ 99.9%; the maximum skew of clocks within its constant target;
completeness of fees.

**Guardrail.** Tests of invariants (volumes never negative; amounts equal to price × quantity,
within a tick or a fee); a detector of gaps in the sequences (no hole beyond a threshold); checks
of consistency before and after each join.

**Output.** The film of execution: normalised, enriched and replayable → step 3 (live KPIs), the
audit pack.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Live computations: slippage, markouts, capture of the spread, impact, quality of venue and route</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The metrics must rest on a reliable film.

**Actions.**
- Compute the [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) against its benchmark (per fill, and
  aggregated per intent), markouts over several horizons, the capture of the [spread](../../glossary.md#spread) (for passive
  orders), the latency of fills, the rate of completion, and the impact estimated against the
  natural move of the price.
- Keep live tables per segment (from step 1) and per venue and route.
- Produce a real-time stream of KPIs, with quantiles and confidence intervals.

**KPI.** Latency from fill to KPI published (T95 ≤ a fraction of the mean time of
acknowledgement); freshness of the aggregations, with no delay building up.

**Guardrail.** An independent cross-check, a second and simple chain of computation; tests of
sensitivity to missing ticks; against gaming, the realised P&L and the cost against the benchmark
are watched together.

**Output.** The live stream of KPIs of execution, with aggregated views (per minute, per intent) →
step 4, step 5, section 5 (steering).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Qualified gaps, and the attribution of their cause</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the score known, find the actionable "why".

**Actions.**
- Compare the real with the bands of step 1 online, with detectors of excess that make few false
  positives.
- Attribute the gap: impact against the natural move of the price, adverse selection, a bad venue
  or route, a change of regime (spread, volatility, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity)), internal latency.
- Issue a diagnosis, with a confidence and a horizon of validity.

**KPI.** Precision as perceived afterwards (postmortems confirm ≥ X%); the ratio of false positives
below its threshold; delay of detection at T95.

**Guardrail.** A vote of several signals (no decision on a single indicator); robust windows,
against short noise; a check of contradictions: when the cost rises but the markout of the P&L is
favourable, no aggressive action.

**Output.** The qualified gap (probable cause, severity, expected duration), as a
[monitoring signal](../../glossary.md#monitoring-signal) → step 5, intervention (S6.7) when it is
critical.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Policy of action and modes: a what-if coach and a controlled execution</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Act only on a qualified cause.

**Actions.**
- Define simple rules: when impact rises, slow down or cut participation; when the cause is
  adverse selection, go more passive or change hours; when a venue is toxic, reroute; when internal
  latency is the cause, enter a degraded mode.
- Attach to each rule an estimate of what-if (the expected cost if it is applied), a time to live,
  criteria of revert, and a trace of explanation.
- Issue a formal instruction to section 5, with its level of priority and its window of
  application: the [control directive](../../glossary.md#control-directive).

**KPI.** Net gain against the counterfactual per action (cost avoided minus the cost of the
opportunity lost); rate of correct reverts, with no needless stickiness.

**Guardrail.** Safe limits in code (a floor of aggressiveness, quotas of rerouting); a cool-down
between actions; a cap on accumulated actions, against yo-yo.

**Output.** Control directives, traceable by construction, and the journal of decisions → S5.7
(applied in the algorithms of execution), step 6 (evaluation).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · The post-trade digest and the daily loop of learning</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Check the effect of the actions, and keep the expectations up to date.

**Actions.**
- Produce a clear digest per strategy, venue and slice of the day: notable gaps, actions taken,
  their measured effect, anomalies resolved or not.
- Revise the bands of expectation when needed, with prudence and slow rules of update.
- Issue recommendations: hot, to section 5; cold, to section 4 (deep studies of cost analysis) and
  to lifecycle (postmortems, process).

**KPI.** The end-of-day report ready at its target time; actions evaluated ≥ 95%; quality of the
reviews (items closed against items reopened).

**Guardrail.** A waiting period on updates of the expectations: one applies only after n days of
confirmation; a peer review of the structural changes.

**Output.** The end-of-day report, the changelog of the expectations, the backlog of analyses →
sections 4, 5 and 7; the day's [audit pack](../../glossary.md#audit-pack) → S6.3.

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of the contract of expected execution: segments, bands, horizons.
- Canonical schema of the film of execution, and a guide to timestamps (references, budgets of
  skew).
- Catalogue of KPIs: definitions, units, aggregations, windows.
- Rules of attribution: from signals to causes, with thresholds and confidence.
- Policy of action: rules, time to live, revert, safe limits, cool-down.
- Standard end-of-day dashboard and a template of digest, per strategy, venue and hour.
- Audit pack: replayable samples of inputs and outputs, the journal of decisions, the evidence of
  best execution.
- KPI tracking table, with history, thresholds and active alerts.
- Replacement process: how to freeze a policy, return to the default, and redeploy cleanly.
