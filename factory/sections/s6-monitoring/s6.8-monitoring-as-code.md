# S6.8 — Observability platform & monitoring-as-code

## TL;DR

The factory's single control room: one common base that captures metrics, logs, traces and events, correlates them by the right tags, alerts reliably, and exposes tested buttons of action.
All of it is managed as code: versioned, tested, deployed and reversible; success is to see right, alert right and act fast, at a controlled cost.
It carries every flow of monitoring without owning it: the KPI feeds to their readers, and the signals and directives on its event bus to section 5.

## Purpose

Build the factory's single control room: a common base that captures metrics, logs, traces and
events, correlates them with the right tags (strategy, model, instrument, session), applies
reliable rules of alert, and exposes tested buttons of action. The whole is managed as code
([monitoring as code](../../glossary.md#monitoring-as-code)): versioned, tested, deployed, reversible.

**Success looks like:** see right, alert right, act fast, at a controlled cost.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The SLO catalogue of S6.1 gives the definitions the platform enforces, among them the dictionary
of events and the schema of instrumentation. The platform carries every flow of monitoring without
owning it: each flow stays declared from its producer to its reader. Through it, the KPI feeds of
sections 1 to 5 reach their readers: the market flows, the [reference data](../../glossary.md#reference-data), the [features](../../glossary.md#feature) and
[labels](../../glossary.md#label) and the quality of data; the hypotheses and [SLIs](../../glossary.md#sli) of the lab; the needs of live metrics and
the markers of experiment of validation; the states of the router, fills, rejections, latencies and
controllers of execution. So do the signals of security of S6.6: identities, network,
vulnerabilities.

Its [event bus](../../glossary.md#event-bus) carries the [monitoring signals](../../glossary.md#monitoring-signal) and the [control directives](../../glossary.md#control-directive). Its hooks of actuation
(rate limit, degraded mode, [kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch), module flags) are how the directives of S6.2, S6.3 and
S6.5 reach section 5, and how [intervention](../../glossary.md#intervention) (S6.7) acts. It sends reports to section 4, and the
evidence of metrics, logs and traces to lifecycle (S7). Its own health goes to S6.1 as its KPI
feed, and its runbooks to S6.7.

**Constraints.**
- Reliable source timestamps: the skew between clocks ≤ 100 ms within a zone.
- [Idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent) hooks of actuation.
- Versioned [schema contracts](../../glossary.md#schema-contract), with deprecations announced.

**Service levels:**
- Ingestion at p95: metrics ≤ 5 s, traces ≤ 2 s, logs searchable ≤ 30 s.
- From event to actuation, at p95 for critical events: ≤ 1 s.
- Availability of the platform ≥ 99.9%.
- Critical market events (IPO, suspension, delisting, a change of tick or lot), measured at T90 and
  [T95](../../glossary.md#t95): reflected in the flows of [observability](../../glossary.md#observability) ≤ 60 s at T95, with an alert
  when exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the schemas and tags are validated (step 1), and the points of instrumentation defined (step 2);
- the synchronisation of time is under control;
- the owners are named.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- monitors managed as code ≥ 95%;
- the [drills](../../glossary.md#drill) with synthetic probes passed;
- the ratio of useful alerts > 4:1;
- the cost per strategy within its budget;
- the catalogue of dashboards approved.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun steps 1, 4 and 5 (schemas, rules, tests), and notify
[change management](../../glossary.md#change-management) (S7.5) for its
[gates](../../glossary.md#gate) of return to production, when:
- a [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): the time to detect rises, false positives rise, costs rise;
- the market [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes (volatility, [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity));
- a new family of strategies or models arrives, or execution is redesigned;
- new regulatory requirements arrive (reporting, best execution).

Within section 6, the page proposes the alerts and controllers to automate or to withdraw.
Lifecycle receives material for its postmortems (S7.9), the debt of reliability (S7.4), and the
priorities of retraining and review (S7.6).

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · A common standard and schemas: SLIs, SLOs, tags</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Get everyone to agree on what to measure, how to tag it, and when to alert.

**Actions.**
- Define the base SLIs and [SLOs](../../glossary.md#slo) of the platform: ingestion, query, availability, latencies at p95
  and p99, errors, costs.
- Freeze a schema of metrics, logs and traces, with mandatory tags: strategy, model, [venue](../../glossary.md#venue),
  instrument, run, session.
- Define the [error budgets](../../glossary.md#error-budget) and the levels of alert: symptoms first, causes after.
- Publish a guide of naming, and a linter of metrics.

**KPI.** Standard coverage: the % of services and strategies that comply (target ≥ 90%).
Readability: the % of metrics validated by the linter (> 95%). Useful alerts: the ratio of true to
false positives (> 4:1).

**Guardrail.** A mandatory review of the schemas (a peer and a referent); the build blocked when
tags or names do not comply; a budget of cardinality per tag, with hard thresholds and an alert
before them.

**Output.** The contract of observability (files of specification) → sections 1 to 5 (data to
execution), [SecOps](../../glossary.md#secops) (S6.6), and the platform itself.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Unified collection: metrics, logs and traces, correlated</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Collect metrics, logs and traces, and link them by tags and IDs, on a reliable
synchronisation of time. Designing the points and the pipelines comes first; running them is
execution.

**Actions.**
- Define the minimum points of instrumentation: latency end to end, errors, queues, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) and
  [markouts](../../glossary.md#markout).
- Set up pipelines of ingestion that are separate but correlated (metrics, logs, traces), with
  reliable source timestamps and a maximum skew defined.
- Index the distributed traces from the feature to the fill (span and parent IDs).

**KPI.** Latency of ingestion at p95: metrics ≤ 5 s, logs searchable ≤ 30 s, traces ≤ 2 s. Rate of
correlated samples (trace to metric) ≥ 95%. Maximum skew of clocks ≤ 100 ms within a domain.

**Guardrail.** A test of [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) on a sample; a quota per producer, and a controlled back-pressure;
an adaptive sampling of traces on saturation.

**Output.** Correlated flows of metrics, logs and traces → dashboards, alerts and traces for
execution (section 5), the cost analysis of section 4 on live fills, and SecOps (S6.6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · The event bus and the signals of actuation</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Broadcast normalised events (errors, drifts, changes of regime, security) and signals of
action for the controllers of execution.

**Actions.**
- Define the contracts of events: schema, keys, severity, time to live.
- Create channels per domain (execution, data, security), with a [dead-letter queue](../../glossary.md#dead-letter-queue) and replay.
- Expose standard hooks of actuation: rate limit, degraded mode, kill switch, module flags.

**KPI.** End to end at p95, from event to action ≤ 1 s for the critical signals; rate of compliant
events (schema and authentication) ≥ 99%; rate of successful actuation ≥ 99.5%.

**Guardrail.** Idempotence and limiters on the side of the consumer; cells that bound the blast
radius (isolated scopes and tenants); tests of the loops of control, against oscillation.

**Output.** The topics of events and the API of actuation → section 5 (decision and execution),
intervention (S6.7), lifecycle (S7).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Monitoring as code: a language, a repository, continuous delivery</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Metrics, alerts, dashboards, tests and runbooks live in an auditable repository.

**Actions.**
- Define a simple language (or schemas) for SLIs and SLOs, rules, routes of alert, dashboards and
  runbooks.
- Put continuous integration in place: lint, tests of thresholds with synthetic probes, a dry run
  in a sandbox, a [canary release](../../glossary.md#canary-release) in production, [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).
- Provide a starter kit of instrumentation: templates per type of service or strategy.

**KPI.** The % of monitors managed as code (target ≥ 95%); the median delay from a change request
to the alert in production (target ≤ 24 h); the % of dashboards backed by an SLO (≥ 90%).

**Guardrail.** A mandatory review by the referent of observability; blocked when the tests of alert
are missing; a map of owners (who pages whom).

**Output.** The repository of monitoring as code, and packages to get started → every team
(sections 1 to 7).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Continuous validation: synthetic probes, canaries, targeted chaos</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Check that the alarms ring when they should, and that the controllers react.

**Actions.**
- Synthetic probes: regular probes on the critical paths (latency, errors, simulated outages).
- Canary releases: a small share of traffic under a new rule or alert.
- Targeted chaos: controlled faults (latency, drops, simulated corruption) on a reduced perimeter.

**KPI.** Time to detect of the synthetic probes (target < 1 min); the rate of success of the drills
(≥ 95%); false positives after deployment (falling).

**Guardrail.** Windows and guards on the chaos (a switch to stop it, an observer); runbooks linked
to every alert, and tested; the noise nobody reads is measured, and alerts never consulted are
withdrawn.

**Output.** Validation reports and the evidence of metrics, logs and traces → monitoring (S6),
SecOps (S6.6), lifecycle (S7).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Governance of costs and tenants, and a useful interface</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Avoid an explosion of costs and cardinality, keep the tenants cleanly apart, and deliver
a useful interface, not a museum. Running the quotas and the retention comes first, as execution;
the policy drawn from them is design.

**Actions.**
- [Retention policies](../../glossary.md#retention-policy) (hot, warm, archived), quotas, budgets of cardinality, and [showback](../../glossary.md#showback).
- Dedicated spaces per family of strategies: isolation, rights, budgets.
- A catalogue of dashboards: few, useful, tied to SLOs, with quarterly reviews to clean it up.

**KPI.** Cost of observability per million of notional and per strategy (falling); the % of data
archived against hot (a target by use); the rate of use of the dashboards (≥ 80% consulted per
week).

**Guardrail.** Hard thresholds of cardinality, with automatic dropping and an early alert; a
process of exception per sponsor, for a limited time; content nobody consults is retired.

**Output.** The policy of the platform, the spaces and quotas, and the validated catalogue → every
team.

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: SLIs and SLOs, schemas, tags, contracts of events, hooks.
- Log of choices: decisions, reasons, alternatives rejected.
- Modules ready to test: the pipelines of metrics, logs and traces, the event bus, the hooks of
  actuation, the starter kit.
- The repository of monitoring as code: rules, dashboards, runbooks, synthetic probes, continuous
  delivery.
- KPI tracking table: coverage, time to detect and to repair of observability, costs, use,
  latencies.
- Replacement process: rollback, migration of schemas, retirement of content and costs.
