# S6.3 — Data integrity, traceability & compliance

## TL;DR

Keeps a continuous, replayable journal of truth: every figure (P&L, slippage, latency, fill rate) matches verifiable facts, correctly timestamped, traceable end to end and immutable.
At any moment a KPI can be traced to its source events, each transformation explained, a period replayed point-in-time, and the internal books reconciled with brokers and venues without ambiguity.
Its audit packs prove figures and decisions to lifecycle (S7.2) and regulators; when integrity breaks, its control directive can stop trading (S5.9).

## Purpose

Produce a continuous, replayable journal of truth which guarantees that every figure (P&L,
[slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage), latency, fill rate) matches verifiable facts, correctly
timestamped, traceable end to end ([lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage)) and immutable
([immutability](../../glossary.md#immutability)).

**Success looks like:** at any moment, a [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) can be traced back to its source events (orders,
executions, prices seen); each transformation can be explained; a period can be
[replayed](../../glossary.md#replay) [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time); and the internal records reconcile with the
external ones (brokers, [venues](../../glossary.md#venue)) without ambiguity.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

Section 1 brings the raw events (orders, executions, quotes,
[reference data](../../glossary.md#reference-data)), point-in-time, with the mandatory fields of the
contract of reality: its replay API, the records of its joins and repairs, its scorecards of
quality, and the evidence and gate reports of its [sealed batches](../../glossary.md#sealed-batch).
The feature registry of S2.10 brings the lineage of every [feature](../../glossary.md#feature) and
[label](../../glossary.md#label) (input, output, rule) with its correlation IDs. From section 5, the
execution log of S5.7 brings the statuses of orders and strategies; the risk budget book of S5.4 the
limits that trading must respect. The modes activated come from stabilisation (S6.5), which owns the
ladder of modes: its decision log journals every entry into a mode and every exit. The SLO catalogue
of S6.1 gives the definitions; S6.2 sends the audit pack of each day of execution, and S6.6 the
artefacts of audit of security. The explanation sheets of the system of record (S7.2) give the
reason of each trade, which the audit packs carry. The
[data lifecycle](../../glossary.md#data-lifecycle) (S7.7) sends its KPIs and alerts on lineage,
contracts and backfills.

The audit packs go to lifecycle's system of record (S7.2), with the register of changes, for the
[gates](../../glossary.md#gate) of promotion and [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback). When
integrity fails or a reconciliation breaks, a control directive asks the governance of section 5
(S5.9) to trip its [kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch), with its justification. The page's
decision log records it, and goes to stabilisation (S6.5), so that the stop enters the journal of
modes, and to lifecycle's system of record (S7.2). The monitoring signals of integrity go to
stabilisation (S6.5), to [intervention](../../glossary.md#intervention) (S6.7) and to the loop of
missions (S7.10); the templates of incident, as runbooks, to S6.7. Its KPIs go to S6.1, and to
[drift](../../glossary.md#drift) detection (S6.4), which rests on its timestamps and its invariants.
Section 4 reads the manifests of versions and the replays within ±ε that this page keeps, and
section 5 its signals of confidence.

**Constraints.**
- Stable, versioned schemas, with backward compatibility documented.
- Units and calendars normalised: time zones, trading days, rounding.
- Correlation through IDs is mandatory (step 2).

**Service levels**, generic, by tier:
- Tier A, critical real-time events: from ingestion to the immutable journal at
  [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) = baseline_A + 20%, the [baseline](../../glossary.md#baseline) set by measure.
- Tier B, post-trade intraday and end of day: T95 = baseline_B + 20%.
- Tier C, reference data and slow market events (IPO, suspension, delisting): T95 set by the
  operational risk, with an alert when exceeded, and T90 of propagation to every consumer.
- Rule: a repeated breach opens an incident and a recalibration (S6.7).

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the contract of reality (step 1) is signed, the taxonomy of events frozen (v1), and the schemas
  of IDs and idempotence (step 2) tested;
- the source of time and the policy of skew are defined (step 3).

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- coverage by [WORM storage](../../glossary.md#worm-storage) = 100%, and 0% of non-monotonic
  timestamps over N consecutive days;
- [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility) of the reports ≥ target, within ±ε, over 3 random replays;
- the end-of-day reconciliation ≥ target, and the mean age of the gaps below its target delay, for
  N days;
- an audit pack produced within its service level, 100% complete.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the KPIs of integrity [drift](../../glossary.md#drift), or the false positives rise;
- something changes outside: a new market rule, a new format from a broker or a venue;
- a key computation is redesigned, for example a new logic of aggregation;
- a major incident occurs, and its postmortem calls for corrective actions.

Within section 6, the thresholds and [SLIs](../../glossary.md#sli) are adjusted and the controls enriched. Lifecycle (S7)
receives gates of promotion based on reproducibility and reconciliation, and rollbacks that are
justified and planned.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Define the contract of reality: invariants and the perimeter of events</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without rules of conservation and a clear perimeter, everything that follows can be
disputed.

**Actions.**
- A catalogue of business invariants: conservation of positions and quantities, monotonicity,
  plausible bounds, causality of timestamps.
- A taxonomy of events (orders, children, executions, cancels, quotes, market states, reference
  data) with their mandatory fields and their meaning, for example visible against hidden
  quantity.
- Contracts of data: the fields present, the domains of values, the units
  ([schema contracts](../../glossary.md#schema-contract)).

**KPI.** Coverage of the invariants (the % of entities and events covered): ≥ 95% at the start,
rising to 100%.

**Guardrail.** An independent cross review of the invariants, against an incoherent rule that
would raise false positives.

**Output.** Specification of the invariants and the contract of reality, versioned → step 2
(identity, idempotence), step 4 (online checks).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Identity, idempotence and correlation</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Avoid counting anything twice, and keep the lineage reliable.

**Actions.**
- A system of unique, stable identifiers: parent orders, children, executions, market messages,
  [snapshots](../../glossary.md#snapshot).
- Keys of correlation across systems: desk and strategy, session, batch, day of risk.
- A policy of idempotence: what happens when the same event comes back, changes order, or arrives
  out of sequence ([idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent) processing).

**KPI.** Rate of duplicates detected (parts per million) and rate of events out of sequence; an
alert above a threshold set from the baseline plus a margin.

**Guardrail.** A load test with controlled re-emission and reordering, against partial
idempotence.

**Output.** The schema of identifiers and the policy of idempotence, with tests that every ID is
unique and every sequence in order → step 3 (immutable journals), step 5 (reconciliations).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · An immutable journal and reliable timestamps</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without logical write-once storage and disciplined time, there is neither proof nor
causality.

**Actions.**
- Write-once, append-only journals of the critical events, with
  [fingerprints](../../glossary.md#fingerprint) (hashes) and a chaining of blocks and journals.
- A policy of timestamps: the [reference clock](../../glossary.md#reference-clock), monotonic
  timestamps, detection of skew, and the rules for a clock late, early or resynchronised.
- An audit trail for corrections: never overwrite; always a new event of rectification linked to
  the previous one, with its reason and its author.

**KPI.** Coverage of the critical events by write-once storage → 100%; skew between systems at p95
and p99, and the % of non-monotonic timestamps → 0.

**Guardrail.** A test that cuts and restarts a clock, and injects backdated events, against a
silent rewrite or a broken order in time.

**Output.** The immutable flow of events, the protocol of time and the register of amendments →
step 4 (live checks), step 6 (replay), step 7 (audit artefacts).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Online checks of the invariants: detection, alert, ticket</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn the rules of step 1 into operational detectors, resting on step 3.

**Actions.**
- Checks in the stream (conservation, causality, bounds, volumes), with three levels: warning,
  alert, stop.
- A policy of alert: thresholds set from the baseline, against noise, aggregated per incident.
- A case opened automatically, with its context: raw events, timestamps, fingerprints, correlated
  IDs.

**KPI.** Time to detect ([MTTD](../../glossary.md#mttd-mttr)) and rate of false positives of the
checks: a short time to detect, and false positives below the agreed threshold.

**Guardrail.** A controlled silent period, against alerts that flap because their thresholds are
badly calibrated.

**Output.** Dashboard of integrity, pipeline of alerts and templates of incident → step 5, step 7
(postmortems), intervention (S6.7, its templates of incident).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Reconcile internal and external: brokers, venues, custodians</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Only equality across systems validates the external reality.

**Actions.**
- Mappings of fields and IDs between internal and external, with tolerances (rounding, latency).
- A deferred process (intraday, then end of day) that matches quantities (total and visible),
  prices, fees and positions.
- A classification of gaps (missing data, partial order, post-trade adjustment, technical
  rejection) and a workflow of resolution: who, how, in what delay.

**KPI.** The % aligned per day, the amount of the gaps, and their mean age; objective: gaps above
their threshold handled within the target delay.

**Guardrail.** Inverse sampling: start from a figure on a dashboard and go down to the
confirmations, against a blind spot on a flow.

**Output.** Daily report of reconciliation and the register of gaps and resolutions → step 7,
section 4 (post-trade cost analysis), section 5 (live decisions when a gap is critical).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Versions and point-in-time replay</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without versions of data, code and parameters, there is no reproducible proof.

**Actions.**
- Number every input (data) and every computation (code, parameters), captured when a KPI is
  produced.
- Keep point-in-time [snapshots](../../glossary.md#snapshot), and a policy of freeze for the
  official replays.
- A procedure of replay: replay a day, compare the KPIs and reports, and document the gap
  tolerated, ±ε.

**KPI.** Rate of [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility) of the reports (the share of
KPIs that land within ±ε) → about 100%.

**Guardrail.** Blind replays (another operator, the same manifest), against an implicit dependency
that no version records.

**Output.** A manifest of versions per run, the tooling of replay and the register of gaps within
±ε → section 4 (validation, execution simulation), lifecycle (S7, gates of promotion and
rollback).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Artefacts of audit and compliance: best execution, changes, access</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Make the proofs presentable and binding.

**Actions.**
- Audit dossiers ready to use: chronology, source events, transformations applied,
  reconciliations, decisions and signatures.
- A journal of changes (who, what, why, when) for data, computations, thresholds and invariants.
- [Guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail) of access and of separation of roles: read, write, rectify.

**KPI.** Time to assemble a complete dossier from a KPI (its service level); rate of completeness,
every piece present.

**Guardrail.** Periodic dry runs of an audit, against a piece missing or untraceable.

**Output.** Standard [audit packs](../../glossary.md#audit-pack), the register of changes and the
matrix of access → monitoring (S6, the point of truth), lifecycle (S7.2, governance), internal
control and regulators.

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: the contract of reality, the taxonomy of events, the invariants, the
  policy of time, idempotence, the mapping across systems.
- Log of choices: decisions, alternatives rejected, expected impacts, versioned.
- Testable modules and their documentation: the immutable journal and its register of amendments;
  the online checks of invariants; reconciliation intraday and across days; the procedure of
  point-in-time replay.
- KPI tracking table: integrity in time, write-once coverage, idempotence, reconciliation,
  reproducibility, time to detect and to repair.
- Replacement process: how to substitute a source, a format or an algorithm of control without
  breaking [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) (plan of migration, tests of non-regression, a period of double running).
