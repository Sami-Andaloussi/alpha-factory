# S2.2 — Labelling schemes (side, size, meta)

## TL;DR

Gives every event a reference truth: its side (which way), its size (how much) and its meta label (should it have been acted on, there and then).
Labels are point-in-time, traceable, stable across regimes and free of any execution assumption such as fees or slippage.
They feed sample weights (S2.3), then the strategy lab and validation through the registry of S2.10.

## Purpose

Give the factory a reference truth for each event of the [event index](../../glossary.md#event-index):

- **side**: the expected direction (act or not, long or short);
- **size**: the economic amplitude of the outcome, a normalised gain or loss;
- **meta**: a green or red light on activation: the setup exists, but should it have been acted
  on there and then? ([meta-labelling](../../glossary.md#meta-labelling))

**Success looks like:** [labels](../../glossary.md#label) that are
[point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time), traceable, stable per [regime](../../glossary.md#regime)
and useful downstream (weights and sampling, the strategy lab, validation), without injecting any
assumption about a specific execution.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The sealed batches bring point-in-time adjusted prices and quotes and the [trading calendar](../../glossary.md#trading-calendar)
(trading days, suspensions). The amplitude measure used to scale the labels is fixed at *t0*.
Simple regimes come from S2.6 when available, or are defined locally. The label set holds the
primary labels (side, size, status, t0, t1, horizons, parameters) and the meta labels (meta,
activation window, logged context). The strategy lab (S3), validation (S4) and execution (S5)
receive them through the registry of [S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md).

**Constraints.**
- Point-in-time guaranteed; time zones and currencies normalised; monotonic timestamps.
- Integrity: no field tied to real execution (fees, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage)) in the
  labels.
- Parity: the same rules in training and in serving; the version log is mandatory.

**Service levels:** [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) publication = t1 plus a delay set per frequency, to be fixed per desk.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the event index of S2.1 is validated;
- the market data is replayable and compliant (adjustments, suspensions);
- the amplitude measure is frozen at *t0*;
- the temporal-leakage checklist is at 0.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of steps 1 to 6 are within their bands;
- the "Label QA" report is signed;
- the "Labels v1" contract is published and its parity test passes;
- the latency service level is documented and monitored.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- labels [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): side entropy below its threshold, timeouts out of band, the meta lift falling
  below 1;
- the regime changes structurally (a lasting jump in amplitude);
- market rules change: trading hours, tick size, short selling;
- a new asset or horizon is added;
- the amplitude method is revised, for example a new robust scale.

Monitoring (S6) receives the drift KPIs ([base rate](../../glossary.md#base-rate), timeouts, meta lift, latency) with their
alerts. Lifecycle (S7) receives the version log, the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) criteria and the [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback)
procedure.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Define the outcome geometry: barriers and horizon</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a frame of target, stop and time, no outcome can be called clear or compared.

**Actions.**
- Per asset family, set a rule for profit targets and stops scaled by an amplitude measure:
  volatility, a robust standard deviation, a rank.
- Define an expiry horizon from *t0* to *t1* tied to amplitude (the more volatile, the more room),
  and several parallel horizons (short, medium, long) for multi-scale tests.
- State the resolution priority: hit (target) before stop before timeout, so that nothing is
  counted twice. This is the [triple-barrier method](../../glossary.md#triple-barrier-method).

**KPI.** Clear resolutions = (hits + stops) / total, within a target band (for example 55–85%
depending on the asset).

**Guardrail.** Against temporal [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) (an amplitude measure computed beyond *t0*): a
"freeze-at-t0" test that reruns the computation on *t0* [snapshots](../../glossary.md#snapshot) and compares bit for bit.

**Output.** "Outcome geometry v1" specification: formula for targets and stops, scale, horizons,
priority → steps 2 and 3, S2.3 (weights), S4 (validation of horizons).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Calibrate per asset and regime, over several horizons</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** One geometry does not fit everything; calibrating it removes absurd timeouts and
too-easy hits.

**Actions.**
- Split history into simple regimes (low, medium, high movement) with a state measure defined at
  *t0*.
- For each asset × regime × horizon, sweep a few target and stop pairs (always scaled) and keep
  those that make outcomes most readable (clear, not degenerate) at the lowest complexity.
- Freeze a parameter matrix: (asset, regime, horizon) → (target, stop).

**KPI.** Share of timeouts per cell asset × regime × horizon within a band, for example 15–45%.

**Guardrail.** Against [overfitting](../../glossary.md#overfitting) the [calibration](../../glossary.md#calibration): a simple time-ordered
[cross-validation](../../glossary.md#cross-validation) on expanding windows; the criterion is a
timeout share stable within ±Δ% on [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) segments.

**Output.** Calibration matrix v1 per asset, regime and horizon → step 3, S2.3 (overlap), S6
(drift monitoring).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Produce the primary labels: side and size</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn the film of the market into targets a model can learn.

**Actions.**
- For each event *t0*, apply the geometry and the calibration to get its status: hit, stop or
  timeout.
- Side: encode the direction of the event (for example +1, −1, 0) by the upstream rule, or "act
  versus do not act" when neutral.
- Size: measure the amplitude of the outcome (a return, or ticks) normalised by the amplitude
  chosen at *t0*, and keep its raw order of magnitude too.
- Trace everything: *t0*, *t1*, status, targets and stops in numbers, horizon, version of the
  rules.

**KPI.** Side class entropy (how evenly events are distributed across classes) and status distribution not
degenerate: never more than 90% in one class.

**Guardrail.** Against inconsistent standardisation, a cross-asset comparability test: the
distributions of normalised size stay of the same order (ratio of standard deviations within
[0.5, 2] per asset).

**Output.** Primary label table (event key, side, size, status, t0, t1, metadata) → S3 (learning
direction and size), S4 (naive [backtests](../../glossary.md#backtest)), S2.3 (weights through overlap).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Define the meta label: activation template and label</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Separating the idea (side and size) from its activation in context (meta) improves
precision without distorting the setup.

**Actions.**
- Write the meta template, the truth rule for a "paying activation": following the side from *t0*
  to *t1* with the fixed geometry, is the outcome favourable? Meta = 1 if the target is hit before
  the stop, 0 otherwise.
- Set the activation window, the longest delay between *t0* and a hypothetical entry (immediate or
  a few moments later), always defined at *t0*.
- Log context attributes for future filters, without prescribing any: market state, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity),
  [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), scheduled announcements. No model here: only the truth and candidate covariates.

**KPI.** Naive meta lift = mean P&L when acting only on meta = 1 / mean P&L without meta ≥ a
threshold, ideally above 1.

**Guardrail.** No confusion between labels and execution: no cost correction here, and a lint test
that finds no order-cost or latency field in the meta labels.

**Output.** Meta label table (event key, meta, activation window, logged context) → S3 (learning
the activation filter), S4 (evaluating the meta lift).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Self-diagnosis and sanity backtests of the labels</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Before feeding S3 and S4, check that the labels describe a coherent problem that can be
learned.

**Actions.**
- Run a naive backtest: follow the side at market, with no [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) and no fees (the geometry alone),
  to check that signs and sizes are coherent.
- Check stability: compare side, size and meta per asset, regime and period, and look for
  collapses of the base rate.
- Draw the overlap maps: count simultaneous events (concurrency) and the mean time in position, to
  prepare S2.3.

**KPI.** Naive P&L per asset close to 0 but sensible (signs and sizes consistent with the
statuses); base-rate drift within a band of ±Δ%.

**Guardrail.** Against structural drift: an alert when side entropy falls below a threshold (for
example < 0.3) or when timeouts exceed a ceiling per regime.

**Output.** "Label QA" report (curves, base-rate tables, [overlap map](../../glossary.md#overlap-map),
naive P&L) → S3 (where to focus), S4 (initial parameters), S2.3 (weights).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Traceability contract and publication</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a contract there is no audit and no maintenance.

**Actions.**
- Schema and UID: the mandatory fields are event ID, t0, t1, side, size, meta, status, horizons,
  scaled parameters and version of the rules.
- [Versioning](../../glossary.md#versioning): a new version as soon as the geometry or the calibration changes, with a log of
  reasons.
- Service levels: a publication latency target (for example T95 "labels available" = t1 + X
  minutes or hours) and T90 / T95 integration of special events (IPO, suspensions).

**KPI.** T95 publication latency ≤ its target; [train/serve parity](../../glossary.md#train-serve-parity)
= 100% on a reconciliation panel.

**Guardrail.** Against non-parity, an end-to-end [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) test: offline recomputation against the
online labels on a frozen sample.

**Output.** "Labels v1" contract and the published stream, with train/serve parity → S3, S4, S5
(rules of use), S6 (drift monitoring), S7 (governance).

</details>

## Deliverables

- "Outcome geometry v1" specification: targets and stops, horizons, resolution priority, amplitude
  measure.
- Calibration matrix per asset × regime × horizon.
- Primary and meta label tables, with the overlap map.
- "Labels v1" contract: schema, UID, versioning rules, service levels.
- "Label QA" report: naive backtests, stability, drifts, comparability.
- KPI dashboard for steps 1 to 6, with thresholds and alerts.
- Decision log (choices, reasons, impacts, date, owner) and replacement procedure: how to
  recalibrate, replay and republish.
