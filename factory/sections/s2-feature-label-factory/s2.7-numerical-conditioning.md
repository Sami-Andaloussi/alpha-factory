# S2.7 — Numerical conditioning & orthogonalisation

## TL;DR

Puts features on the right scale, untangles the information they share and secures the computations, so that models learn fast and stay reliable in production.
Monotone transforms, robust scales and targeted orthogonalisation are fitted on training data only, then frozen and versioned.
The result is a well-conditioned, weakly redundant feature space, identical in research and in serving.

## Purpose

Put [features](../../glossary.md#feature) on the right scale, untangle the information that overlaps between them, and secure
the computations, so that models learn fast and stably and stay reliable in production.

**Success looks like:** a feature space that is well conditioned (a low
[condition number](../../glossary.md#condition-number)), weakly redundant and reproducible, with
[train/serve parity](../../glossary.md#train-serve-parity), and with frozen, versioned parameters
ready to be served: the [conditioning package](../../glossary.md#conditioning-package).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The stabilised series are used where [fractional differentiation](../../glossary.md#fractional-differentiation)
or another transformation of S2.5 applies. The conditioning package holds the transformation map
(monotone transforms and caps), the scale vectors, the
[orthogonalisation](../../glossary.md#orthogonalisation) matrices with the order of their axes,
and the numerical contract (condition-number thresholds, tolerances, diagonal padding). All of it
is fitted on the training part of the splits only, versioned in the registry of
[S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md) with a latency and cost service level, and served from there
to S3, S4 and S5. The flags of step 1 (redundant groups, outliers, weak amplitudes) join the
quality flags.

**Constraints.**
- Strictly [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time); every fit on the training part only.
- Train/serve parity verified: numeric types, tolerances.

**Service levels:**
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) latency set per class of use, for example high frequency against low frequency.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting) integrated in under X minutes or hours; an alert
  when this is exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the integrity splits are settled (S2.4);
- the robust features and [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime) are available (S2.6);
- the common clock is in place (S2.1);
- the training, validation and test sets are locked.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the condition number is below its target thresholds;
- the off-diagonal correlation is lowered;
- train/serve parity > 95% on the scale ranges;
- the rate of numerical failures ≈ 0;
- T95 latency ≤ the service level;
- the "ready-to-serve" package is published in the registry.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- a [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): scale or spectrum, parity;
- a market rule changes (tick size, microstructure), or a new asset arrives (IPO);
- a new method is judged better;
- monitoring or lifecycle (S6, S7) sees repeated service-level breaches, rising numerical failures,
  rising clipping in live use, or a condition number climbing on recent windows.

Monitoring (S6) receives dashboards of the condition number, correlations, parity, latency and
failure rate. Lifecycle (S7) receives the recalibration cadence, the upgrade and downgrade
procedures, and the criteria that make an artefact obsolete.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Map the current geometry</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Before touching the scale, find where it jams: amplitudes, correlations, spectrum.

**Actions.**
- On the point-in-time design (integrity splits already set by S2.4, encodings and regimes from
  S2.6), measure per window and regime: amplitudes, skew, extremes, off-diagonal correlations, the
  dispersion of the singular values and the condition number.
- Find the redundant groups (clusters of nearly collinear variables) and the ill-posed variables
  (nearly constant, or with jumps in level).

**KPI.** Mean off-diagonal correlation; alert above 0.3 on key blocks, where strong redundancy
calls for targeted orthogonalisation. Condition number of the effective block; alert above
10³–10⁴, depending on the use.

**Guardrail.** Measures per regime and per window, so that averages hide nothing.

**Output.** "Geometry and numerical risks" report and feature flags (groups, outliers, weak
amplitudes) → step 2 (transforms), step 4 (orthogonalisation), S2.8 (selection and compression).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Stabilise amplitudes: monotone transforms and soft caps</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Reducing tails and skew before calibrating the scale makes the next step cleaner.

**Actions.**
- Choose, per feature family (prices and returns, volumes, [spreads](../../glossary.md#spread), states, time derivatives),
  monotone transforms where they help, log-like or root-like.
- Define soft caps ([winsorisation](../../glossary.md#winsorisation) rules), documented and
  traceable per regime.
- Freeze the parameters on the training part only, for [temporal integrity](../../glossary.md#temporal-integrity).

**KPI.** Reduction of skew and flattening of the tails, from before to after; alert when the
improvement is under 20% where one was expected.

**Guardrail.** Fit on the training part only; non-inversion tests, so that monotonicity is kept.

**Output.** Transformation map (rules and parameters per feature and regime) → step 3 (scaling),
serving in S5 through the registry of S2.10.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Scale robustly: robust centring and scaling</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** After stabilisation, everything goes on a comparable grid.

**Actions.**
- Define robust centres and scales per feature, regime and window, fitted on the training part.
- Specify the rules for live application: expected ranges, tolerated clipping, numeric types
  consistent between training and serving.

**KPI.** Share of features brought into a target range, for example a gap in dispersion under 2×
between features. Train/serve parity of amplitudes: the share of live values inside the [min, max]
seen in training; alert below 95%.

**Guardrail.** Type checks offline and online; a test for excessive clipping.

**Output.** Scale vectors and an application schema (the train/serve contract) → step 4
(orthogonalisation), S3 (models), S6 (drift checks).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Untangle the information: targeted orthogonalisation</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Once the scales are comparable, the frame of reference can be rotated without bias.

**Actions.**
- Orthogonalise where needed: by redundant group (step 1), or on an operational block (per horizon
  or regime).
- Use numerically stable decompositions (pivoted QR, singular value decomposition and similar) to
  produce an orthogonal, traceable basis.
- Keep the change-of-basis matrix, both ways, and the ordering of the axes (importance, energy).

**KPI.** Fall in off-diagonal correlation: target under 0.1–0.2 on the axes kept. A spectrum more
spread out but not crushed: no singular value close to 0.

**Guardrail.** [Traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) both ways (reconstruction); no needless orthogonalisation, so that the
features stay readable for the business.

**Output.** Versioned orthogonalisation matrices and the energy of each axis → S3 (learning), S2.8
(selection on clean axes), S6 ([alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) and beta attribution).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Lock numerical stability: contracts and guardrails</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** After the rotation, the numerical contract is signed: tolerances, thresholds, [fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback)
paths.

**Actions.**
- Set target condition-number thresholds, and the rules for minimal diagonal padding in solves.
- Define tolerances and fallback plans for sensitive operations, such as iterative refinement or a
  controlled drop in precision.
- Test that offline equals online (the same numeric types, the same tolerances), and the T90 / T95
  latency.

**KPI.** Rate of numerical failures (factorisations, solves) ≈ 0. T90 / T95 latency per batch
within the service level; alert on repeated breaches.

**Guardrail.** Offline-equals-online tests (tolerances, machine epsilon, types) and fallback plans.

**Output.** Numerical contract (thresholds, tolerances, fallback paths, service level) → S3 and S4
(training and validation), S5 (execution), S6 (alerts at run time).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Train/serve parity and recalibration cadence</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Deliver only what can be served, and recalibrated without [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage).

**Actions.**
- Package every artefact (transforms, scales, matrices, thresholds) with an ID, a version and a
  validity window.
- Test parity: the share of live observations inside the ranges seen at fit time; drifts of scale
  and spectrum; real latency and cost.
- Define the rhythm of recalibration (KPI triggers) and the procedures for IPOs, suspensions and
  delistings.

**KPI.** Drift of scale and spectrum (the distance between before and after on rolling windows);
alert beyond a set threshold. Share of assets or events outside the contract (IPOs, suspensions)
brought back into compliance within the service level objective.

**Guardrail.** An [embargo](../../glossary.md#embargo) on recalibration, against leakage;
controlled [replays](../../glossary.md#replay).

**Output.** "S2.7 ready-to-serve" package and recalibration protocol → the registry (S2.10), S3,
S4 and S5 (consumption), S6 and S7 (monitoring and lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: scope, transformation rules, scaling schemes, orthogonalisation policy,
  condition-number thresholds, tolerances, service levels.
- Log of choices: the reasons at each step, the alternatives considered, the impact on S3, S4 and
  S5.
- Modules and documentation ready to test: point-in-time functions that apply transforms, then
  scales, then orthogonalisation, with reversibility and checks.
- KPI tracking table, continuous: condition number, correlations, spectrum, parity, latency,
  failures, with alerts configured.
- Replacement process: recalibration without leakage, versioned [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback), a procedure for events
  (IPO, suspension, delisting), a non-regression test.
