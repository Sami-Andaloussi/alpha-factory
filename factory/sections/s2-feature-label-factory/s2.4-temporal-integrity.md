# S2.4 — Temporal integrity & validation-ready indexing

## TL;DR

Builds the factory's official map of time for validation: purged training and test indexes, embargo zones and replayable non-random splits.
Zero leakage, standardised versioned splits, enough effective sample per fold, and built-in diagnostics.
A simple service answers "give me split k", identically for the strategy lab and for validation.

## Purpose

Build the factory's official time map, the [validation-ready index](../../glossary.md#validation-ready-index):
[purged](../../glossary.md#purging) training and test indexes, [embargo](../../glossary.md#embargo)
zones, and replayable splitting schemes for data that are not independent draws (purged
[folds](../../glossary.md#fold), [combinatorial](../../glossary.md#combinatorial-purged-cross-validation)
schemes).

**Success looks like:** zero [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage), standardised and versioned splits, a sufficient sample size,
integrated diagnostics, and a simple interface ("give me split *k*") that S3 and S4 consume
directly.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The validation-ready index has three parts: a **split registry** that gives each observation its
fold, its role, its embargo window, its event and its purge mask; a **manifest** that records the
overlap definition, embargo rule, [cross-validation](../../glossary.md#cross-validation) scheme, minimum effective sample per fold, [seed](../../glossary.md#seed)
policy and a configuration hash; and an **access interface** that returns ordered training and
test indexes, ready to plug in. S3 and S4 consume it through the registry of
[S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md), with a table that maps versions to splits so that every study
can be reproduced.

**Constraints.**
- One [reference clock](../../glossary.md#reference-clock), with explicit conversions.
- The order of time: nothing in the training part of a fold uses information after *t0*.
- Granularity: splits are made at the same level as training; no mix of minutes and ticks without a
  documented mapping.
- Stable, immutable observation and event IDs.

**Service levels:**
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting) integrated at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ one clock granularity,
  configurable per frequency.
- Splits updated after a new event or a recalibration at T95 ≤ two clock granularities.
- Any breach raises an alert in monitoring (S6) and a ticket to lifecycle (S7).

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the common clock is validated and the data is [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time);
- the event ledger is complete (t0, t1, horizon) and the [labels](../../glossary.md#label) of S2.2 are final;
- the weights and overlaps of S2.3 are available or can be computed.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- leakage = 0% (the tests of step 4);
- [effective sample size](../../glossary.md#effective-sample-size) ≥ the minimum per fold, on every published fold;
- the embargo covers at least the target quantile of event tails;
- parity between S3 and S4 = 100% on an end-to-end test;
- the configuration hash is fixed, and the version is published with its manifest.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule; a failed
diagnostic sends the work back to steps 2 and 3.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the overlap definition, the embargo rule or the cross-validation scheme changes;
- event durations [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) (longer or shorter tails);
- market rules change (trading hours, microstructure);
- monitoring (S6) alerts on rising variance across folds, a falling effective sample size or missed
  latency targets;
- new sources or clocks arrive from S1, or the labels of S2.2 are redesigned.

Monitoring (S6) receives the [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) stream (latency, leakage, effective sample size, stability) and
its alerts. Lifecycle (S7) receives the reasons for each recalibration and proposals for new
policies (embargo rule, cross-validation scheme) with their impact on cost and sample size.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Set the event ledger and the rules of time</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Nothing can be purged or embargoed without clean event boundaries, so they come first.

**Actions.**
- Freeze the common clock alignment and the units: time zones, granularity.
- Materialise the interval of every label: event, *t0*, *t1*, horizon, asset.
- Define overlap: when two events "touch" (same asset, same window, and so on).
- Record the metadata: membership across assets, links to the weights ([uniqueness](../../glossary.md#uniqueness)),
  point-in-time timestamps.

**KPI.** One clock: the share of data outside the common clock. Point-in-time integrity: the share
of fields that are not point-in-time.

**Guardrail.** Any observation outside the clock alignment is rejected or mapped; a causality test
on timestamps (no field from the future).

**Output.** The event ledger (the table of truth) and the clock manifest → step 2 (overlap
graphs), S3 and S4 (a shared reference).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Build the overlap graph and the structural purge</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Overlap is measured on the frozen *t0*–*t1* intervals, so this follows step 1.

**Actions.**
- Index the adjacencies: for each observation or event, the set of events that overlap it.
- Derive the purge: every observation linked, directly or by contact, to a test event is removed
  from training.
- Trace the purge at two levels, observation and event.

**KPI.** Raw potential leakage: the mean degree of overlap between training and test before the
purge. Purge applied: the share of training observations still adjacent to test after the purge.

**Guardrail.** A visualisation of the graph, which should be connected but not saturated; an
automatic adjacency test against the overlap definition.

**Output.** Overlap graph and purge mask (a flag per observation) → step 3 (splits), S3
(trainers), S4 (evaluators).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Calibrate the embargo and generate validation-ready splits</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The embargo sits after the test period and beyond the adjacencies already purged, so it
comes after step 2.

**Actions.**
- Calibrate the embargo rule from the distribution of event durations and tails: cover the
  delayed effects without killing the sample.
- Generate splits consistent with the arrow of time (purged folds, combinatorial schemes),
  [deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) under the seed policy.
- Tag each observation: fold, role (train, test, purged), embargo window, event.

**KPI.** Embargo adequacy: the quantile of event tails it covers. Effective sample size per fold,
after purge and embargo. Determinism: the configuration hash is stable for an equal
configuration.

**Guardrail.** If the embargo covers less than its target, lengthen it, up to the limit set by the
minimum effective sample per fold. If the effective sample size falls below its threshold, reduce
the number of folds, or relax the embargo as a last resort. If determinism fails, publication is
blocked.

**Output.** Split registry (purged training and test indexes, embargo windows) and its
configuration hash (signature) → S3 and S4 (validation, execution simulation, cost analysis).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Diagnostics of integrity and viability</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Measure cleanliness and [statistical power](../../glossary.md#statistical-power) on the final splits, so this follows step 3.

**Actions.**
- Test for leakage: the share of training observations that overlap test labels (target 0).
- Measure protection: how well the embargo covers the event tails.
- Measure power: the effective sample size per fold after purge and embargo.
- Measure stability: the variance of a simple, neutral proxy measure across folds and schemes.

**KPI.** Leakage avoided: the share of overlap from training to test labels. Stability of the
splits: the variance of the proxy measure across folds. Cost against protection: the loss of
effective sample size caused by the embargo.

**Guardrail.** Leakage above 0 sends the work back to steps 2 and 3; instability beyond its limit
is a structural signal that calls for a review of the cross-validation scheme; a cost of
protection too high calls for recalibration, while too low a cost risks under-protection.

**Output.** Integrity report (go or hold), gauges per split and reasons for recalibration → step 5
([versioning](../../glossary.md#versioning) and contracts), S6 (monitoring), S7 ([requalifications](../../glossary.md#requalification)).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Version and serve: index interface, contracts, service levels</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Publish only what is clean and measured, so this comes last.

**Actions.**
- Freeze the configuration (rules, signatures, seeds) under a version number.
- Expose a simple interface: "split *k* → training index, test index, purge mask, embargo
  windows".
- Define the update service levels (T90 / T95) and the alert hooks (breaches, drifts).
- Check parity between S3 and S4: the same IDs, the same splits, the same clocks.

**KPI.** [Train/serve parity](../../glossary.md#train-serve-parity): indexes and roles identical
for S3 and S4. Update service level: the latency of a split update after a new event.
Replayability: the hash of the splits on a rerun.

**Guardrail.** A strict equality test on IDs, roles and folds; an alert opens an automatic ticket
and a controlled retry; any divergence blocks the version until seeds and dates are investigated.

**Output.** Split service contract (the specification), version manifest and parity checks → S3,
S4, S6 (surveillance), S7 (lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification: overlap definition, embargo rule, cross-validation scheme, minimum effective
  sample per fold, seed policy, clock alignment.
- Versioned event ledger and clock manifest.
- Overlap graph and purge mask, materialised.
- Split registry (train, test, purged; folds; embargo windows) and configuration hash.
- Integrity report (leakage = 0%, effective sample size, stability, embargo coverage) with go /
  hold lights.
- Split service contract: access interface, formats, constraints, parity tests.
- Decision log (reasons, alternatives considered, impacts) and replacement process (who changes
  what, when, and how it is tested).
- KPI tracking table wired to monitoring (S6), with alerts and thresholds.
