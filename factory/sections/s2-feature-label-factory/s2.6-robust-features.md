# S2.6 — Robust feature construction, microstructure & regime encoding

## TL;DR

Turns living prices and volumes into stable representations that withstand spikes, doubtful quotes and changes of market mood.
Publishes robust features over several horizons, quality flags that say where data is clean, suspect or to be ignored, and dated market states.
Everything is causal, traceable and servable, and holds up under stress.

## Purpose

Turn "living" prices and volumes into stable representations that resist spikes, doubtful quotes
and changes in the mood of the market. Three outputs:

- a [robust feature set](../../glossary.md#robust-feature-set), over several horizons, ready for
  S3;
- [quality flags](../../glossary.md#quality-flag), cell by cell and case by case, that explain
  where the data is clean, suspect or to be ignored;
- market states and [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime) (trend or range, stress, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity)), dated
  and traceable: the [regime encoding](../../glossary.md#regime-encoding).

**Success looks like:** material that is causal, stable, traceable and servable, that does not
collapse under stress, and that guides S3, S4 and S6.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

[Labels](../../glossary.md#label) and weights serve diagnostics only. The sealed batches bring the microstructure data, the
market calendars and the trading hours. The robust feature set carries its metadata: lag, latency,
version, [lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage). Each quality flag carries its cause, its severity and what it inherits. The
segment map gives the breaks; the regime encoding gives each event its state, a confidence and the
rules of transition. Sections 3, 4 and 5 receive them through the registry of
[S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md).

**Constraints.**
- [Train/serve parity](../../glossary.md#train-serve-parity) guaranteed: the same formulas, the same
  lags.
- Stable formats: versioned schemas, a unique ID per [feature](../../glossary.md#feature) and per state.
- Auditability: every value traces back to its upstream and to the cleaning decisions.

**Service levels:**
- Feature classes, each with its own [service level objective](../../glossary.md#slo).
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) integration of critical events (suspensions, changes of trading hours, listings and
  delistings).
- On a breach, an alert and a switch to the degradation policy: features are downgraded to
  "suspect" instead of "absent".

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the event index is validated (S2.1);
- [temporal integrity](../../glossary.md#temporal-integrity) is in place (S2.4);
- the series are stabilised (S2.5);
- the schemas of S1 are aligned.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of steps 2 to 6 are green;
- temporal [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) is zero;
- train/serve parity is verified;
- service levels have held for two weeks in a row, on paper or in simulated live trading;
- the documentation is complete.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): rate of anomalies, chattering states, latency overload;
- the market changes: new tick sizes, microstructure rules, new trading hours;
- new assets or frequencies arrive;
- a promising new method appears; it is benchmarked again under the contracts of S2.6.

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPIs and flags as health metrics, for dashboards and alerts, and
the recalibration runbooks ([robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) thresholds, smoothing of states, degradation policies),
which S6.7 keeps. Lifecycle (S7) receives the review cadence: quarterly by default, and an
immediate [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) on KPI drift.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Frame the work: input and output contracts</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a clear contract, microstructure corrections and measured robustness cannot be
audited.

**Actions.**
- Define the scope, asset × clock (the event index of S2.1), and the state of the series received
  (after S2.5).
- Set the latency contract per feature class (real time or end of [bar](../../glossary.md#bar)) and the quality taxonomy:
  the codes clean, suspect and drop, with their reasons.
- Freeze the catalogue of outputs: feature families, granularities, states and regimes to publish,
  mandatory metadata ([provenance](../../glossary.md#provenance), lag, clock, version).

**KPI.** Contract coverage (asset × horizon × latency defined, over those expected) ≥ 99%. Lag
compliance (largest gap to the contract) = 0.

**Guardrail.** A clock mismatch, when the event index differs from that of S2.1, stops the work and
raises an alert.

**Output.** "S2.6 contracts" specification (schemas, lags, latencies, quality) → the other steps of
S2.6, S2.7 (conditioning), S3 and S4 (consumption), S6 (surveillance).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Microstructure quality control and repair</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Robust features rest on microstructure material that is already clean.

**Actions.**
- Detect and flag microstructure inconsistencies: impossible quotes or prints, stale or
  out-of-order data, aberrant [spreads](../../glossary.md#spread), tick-size errors.
- Damp the [bid-ask bounce](../../glossary.md#bid-ask-bounce) and the effect of price
  discreteness, without touching directional information.
- Produce cleaned series (reference prices, spreads, consistent volumes) and flags with their cause
  and severity.

**KPI.** Rate of microstructure anomalies per type, and the reduction of the bounce from before to
after. Share of data set aside within [0.1%, 3%]; alert outside that range.

**Guardrail.** Against over-cleaning: a fall of more than x% in achievable variance without a fall
in abnormal spreads rolls the rules back.

**Output.** Cleaned stream and table of microstructure flags (per event, cell and reason) → steps 3
and 4, S6 (quality telemetry).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Statistical robustification</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Reduce the weight of fat tails and outliers before building features.

**Actions.**
- Set bounded-influence policies: validity bounds, controlled clipping or
  [winsorisation](../../glossary.md#winsorisation), rules for missing cells.
- Stabilise the aggregations (medians, robust centres and scales), and treat cellwise problems (one
  isolated value) apart from casewise ones (a whole event).
- Log every decision: threshold, rate of impact, [fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback).

**KPI.** Influence index (the share of cells changed and the median size of the change), stable
per asset and regime. Stability under stress (the relative gap of key features around events) ≤ a
set threshold.

**Guardrail.** Against crushed tails, when the extreme quantiles move beyond a threshold:
recalibrate the bounds.

**Output.** Robust stream and influence log (rate of cells changed, intensity) → step 4, S6
(quality drift).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Build the robust features</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** With a solid base, causal and traced representations can be exposed.

**Actions.**
- Build families of descriptors over several horizons aligned with the [event clock](../../glossary.md#event-clock): robust
  returns, robust volatility and dispersion, imbalances of volume and flow, amplitudes and ranges,
  liquidity, cost and frictions.
- Encode the latency and freeze the lags: strict causality.
- Attach provenance and aggregated quality flags, combining microstructure and robustness.

**KPI.** Consistency of lags and latencies: no feature outside its contract. A signal-to-noise
proxy (useful explained variance over microstructure variance), higher than on the raw data.

**Guardrail.** Against temporal leakage: systematic causality tests; a failure blocks publication.

**Output.** Feature table and its metadata (lag, latency, version, inherited flags) → steps 5 and
6, S2.7, S3, S4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Detect breaks and segment time</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Regime boundaries give structure to the encoding of states and to validation.

**Actions.**
- On the robust series, detect changes of level, variance and correlation, and cut time into
  coherent segments, without flicker.
- Publish the segment boundaries with a confidence and diagnostics.

**KPI.** Persistence of segments (median length above a minimum) and a flicker rate below a
threshold. Agreement of diagnostics (the same breaks found by several families) ≥ a threshold.

**Guardrail.** Against over-segmentation, when the number of segments explodes with no gain in
downstream stability: simplify.

**Output.** [Segment map](../../glossary.md#segment-map) (start, stop, type of break, confidence)
→ step 6, S3 (conditional models), S4 (splits per regime), S6 (drift alerts).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Encode regimes and the state of the market</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** States combine segments and robust features to become inputs of S3 and filters of S4.

**Actions.**
- Define a causal state vector aligned with the segments: trend or range, stress and volatility,
  liquidity and friction, before or after an event.
- Calibrate thresholds and scores per asset, and per segment where needed; document their
  sensitivity.
- Publish, for each event, its state, a confidence and the rules of transition, with no temporal
  leakage.

**KPI.** Coverage of states (no very rare state without a reason) and stability of the boundaries
out of sample. Conditional uplift: the diagnostics of S4 improve when conditioned on the state.

**Guardrail.** Against state chattering (too many switches): smoothing, with a documented minimum
latency.

**Output.** State vector per event (values, confidence, version) and a usage guide (lags, risks) →
S3 (conditional [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) logic), S4 (tests per regime), S6 (monitoring), S5 (execution [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail)).

</details>

## Deliverables

- "S2.6 contracts" specification: schemas, lags, latencies, quality taxonomy, families of features
  and states.
- Log of choices: microstructure rules, robustness policies, thresholds, reasons, tests passed and
  failed.
- Modules ready to test (feature construction, flags, breaks, states), with test sets for
  non-regression, causality and latency.
- KPI dashboard per step, with thresholds, alerts and history.
- Replacement process: triggers, [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback) procedure, degradation plan, train/serve parity
  checklist.
