# S2.3 — Overlap-aware sample weights & resampling

## TL;DR

Gives every event the weight it deserves once overlaps and correlations in time are counted, so that dense episodes are not counted many times.
Builds deterministic resampling plans (bags) that show models a representative spread of regimes, without leakage across validation splits.
Delivers traceable weights, a credible effective sample size and replayable artefacts for sections 3, 4 and 6.

## Purpose

Give each event the [sample weight](../../glossary.md#sample-weight) it deserves, taking
[label overlap](../../glossary.md#label-overlap) and correlations in time into account, then build
resampling plans that expose the model to a representative panorama of [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime), with no temporal
[leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) and no double counting.

**Success looks like:** traceable weight vectors, decorrelated bags and samples, a credible
[effective sample size](../../glossary.md#effective-sample-size), and replayable artefacts for S3,
S4 and S6.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The sealed batches bring the grouping by asset and universe, risk attributes and exclusion masks
(halts, market anomalies). The purged, embargoed splits come from S2.4. The bag manifest records
the bags, their [out-of-bag](../../glossary.md#out-of-bag) sets and their projection on each validation split, with the [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed).
Sections 3 to 7 receive weights and manifests through the registry of
[S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md).

**Constraints.**
- One UTC timestamp, aligned with the clock of S2.1.
- Nothing after *t0* is used to compute a weight or an eligibility.
- Split compatibility: the artefacts obey the same time boundaries as validation (S4).

**Service levels:**
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) integration per frequency: tight budgets for high frequency (milliseconds to seconds),
  wider ones for daily or weekly data (seconds to minutes).
- On critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting), weights and eligibility are republished within
  the agreed window; an alert when T95 is exceeded.
- Graceful degradation: when a full recomputation is unavailable, documented backup weights with
  conservative caps are used, under a visible "degraded mode" flag.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the event index and the [labels](../../glossary.md#label) are validated (time, integrity, no leakage);
- the exclusion masks are fed (halts, market errors);
- the weight policy is defined in writing: components, caps, normalisation.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- versioned weight vectors, the bag manifest and the manifest per split are delivered;
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) are green: [uniqueness](../../glossary.md#uniqueness) ≥ threshold, effective sample size ≥ target, contamination = 0,
  out-of-bag variance ≤ threshold;
- [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) is proven: identical hash, seeds recorded.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) beyond its threshold: uniqueness down, effective sample size down, out-of-bag
  variance up;
- the clock or the labels change upstream;
- market rules change: tick size, microstructure, trading hours;
- a new weighting policy is proposed.

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPI stream and its alerts. Lifecycle (S7) keeps the log of
[requalifications](../../glossary.md#requalification), decisions and versions, with automatic triggers whenever S1 or S2 changes.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Map overlap and uniqueness</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a measure of uniqueness, weights and resampling
are blind and overcount dense episodes.

**Actions.**
- From the event index (*t0* → *t1*) and the labels of S2.2, build the overlap map (which event is
  active when) and compute, for each event, its share of unique information: the time it does not
  share with other active events.

**KPI.** Mean uniqueness, the higher the better; alert below a threshold set per asset and regime.

**Guardrail.** A "no-leak" test: the computation uses nothing after *t0*.

**Output.** A table from event to measures (mean uniqueness, overlap rate, active duration), and
base "uniqueness" weights, normalised to sum to 1 when needed → steps 2 and 3, S4 (metadata for
splits).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Compose the weight policy: uniqueness, recency, classes</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** One weight per event, which drives both learning and resampling.

**Actions.**
- Define a clear weighting policy that combines uniqueness (step 1), an optional explicit time
  decay with a documented [half-life](../../glossary.md#half-life), and balancing of classes and rare events so that critical
  cases are heard, with bounds per event to avoid extreme weights.
- Renormalise, and compute the effective sample size.

**KPI.** Effective sample size = (Σw)² / Σw²; alert when it is far below the number of events, a
sign that a few episodes dominate.

**Guardrail.** A sensitivity stress: vary the half-life and the caps slightly and check that the
ranking of weights stays stable, with no wild flips.

**Output.** Final weight vector per event (with the justification of its components), the
effective sample size and the profile of the weight distribution (quantiles, caps) → step 3, S3
(weighted learning), S6 (monitoring of weights).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Design overlap-aware resampling</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Build varied, honest views of the market for credible bagging and
out-of-bag estimates, without showing the same episode from ten
angles.

**Actions.**
- Define a resampling plan in bags that favours events weakly correlated with those already
  chosen and respects the weights of step 2.
- Make the selection sequential and [deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) (fixed seeds), with correlation and overlap
  checks between the members of a bag. The method is the
  [sequential bootstrap](../../glossary.md#sequential-bootstrap): iterative selection in proportion
  to mean uniqueness, fixed seeds, out-of-bag sets recorded.

**KPI.** Out-of-bag variance across bags, on a simple measure: low variance means healthy
resampling.

**Guardrail.** An internal redundancy test: measure the correlation of targets and returns inside
each bag; above the limit, revise the inclusion rule.

**Output.** [Bag manifest](../../glossary.md#bag-manifest) (events per bag, order, seed) and
out-of-bag indices per bag → S3 (bagged learning), S4 (out-of-bag evaluation), S6 (stability).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Make resampling split-aware: temporal integrity</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** The best resampling is worthless if it crosses the time boundaries of the evaluations.

**Actions.**
- Project the weights and bags onto clean splits, [purged](../../glossary.md#purging) and
  [embargoed](../../glossary.md#embargo), provided by S2.4 and S4, now or later.
- Make sure no event of a test split is duplicated on the training side through an overlap in
  time.

**KPI.** Contamination between training and test: expected 0.

**Guardrail.** An automatic purge and embargo check that fails whenever a training event overlaps
the time of a test event.

**Output.** Manifest per split: eligible events, weights restricted to the split, valid bags → S4
(validation), S3 (training on the training side only).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Replayability and governance of the artefacts</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Let S3, S4 and S6 replay identically, and let S7 change the rules without breaking
history.

**Actions.**
- Freeze the seeds, weight vectors, bag manifests, versions of the event index and labels, and the
  weighting rules.
- Provide IDs, timestamps, checksums, a log of choices and a reading contract (formats, columns,
  keys).

**KPI.** Binary [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility): an identical hash on re-execution.

**Guardrail.** A re-execution diff: when the hash differs, a requalification is forced (step 6).

**Output.** A versioned "weights and resampling" package, a readable specification (the interface
contract) and a KPI dashboard for steps 1 to 4 → S3, S4, S6, S7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Monitoring loop and recalibration</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Regimes change; the weight policy must stay adapted, and frugal.

**Actions.**
- Set sentinels on drift of uniqueness, collapse or runaway of the effective sample size, rising
  overlap, class imbalance and rising out-of-bag variance.
- Define the thresholds, the alerts and a recalibration procedure: recompute the weights and
  policies, re-issue the artefacts.

**KPI.** Uniqueness drift (relative gap to the historical median), trend of the effective sample
size, trend of the out-of-bag variance.

**Guardrail.** A change budget against thrashing: changes cannot be too frequent, and more than N
recalibrations over a rolling window force a review.

**Output.** Alert rules, a recalibration runbook and the history of requalifications → monitoring
(S6), lifecycle and governance (S7).

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: weight policy (conceptual formula, caps, normalisation), split-aware
  rules, resampling rules, alert policies.
- Log of choices and decisions: assumptions, thresholds, any half-lives, sensitivity analyses,
  reasons for the caps.
- Modules and documentation ready to test: weight generation, bag creation, projection per split,
  no-leak and purge / embargo checks; replay scripts with seeds and usage examples for S3 and S4.
- KPI tracking table: mean uniqueness, overlap, effective sample size, out-of-bag variance,
  contamination, hashes, T90 / T95 latency.
- A clear replacement process for a new weighting or resampling policy: acceptance criteria, A/B
  tests on the KPIs, documented [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).
