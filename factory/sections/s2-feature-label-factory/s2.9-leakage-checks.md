# S2.9 — Representation sanity & leakage checks

## TL;DR

Checks that features, labels, weights and time indexes can be used at t0: no leak from the future, no statistical illusion, and representations that stay coherent.
A firewall of seven steps: inventory, access at t0, leakage smoke tests, honest importance, stability under purged splits, plausibility, then go or no-go.
Gives S3 a clean go, S4 proof of compliance, and S2.1 to S2.8 clear correction tickets when a check fails.

## Purpose

Check that [features](../../glossary.md#feature), [labels](../../glossary.md#label), weights and time indexes can be played at *t0*: no
[leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) from the future, no statistical illusion, and representations
that stay coherent (stable, not mere substitutes, plausible).

**Success looks like:** a clean *go* for S3, proofs of compliance ready for S4, and clear
correction tickets for S2.1 to S2.8 whenever something jams.

This page is the firewall of the section: it reads everything S2.1 to S2.8 produce, with its
metadata (versions, definitions), including the purged, embargoed and combinatorial split schemes
of S2.4. The leakage report holds the compliance dossier (proofs at *t0*, smoke tests,

reaches S3 and S4 through the registry of [S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md). The other output is

**Constraints.**
- One *t0* timestamp per row; *t1* or the horizon explicit.
- The same across assets: universe, currency, time zone, mapping of [corporate actions](../../glossary.md#corporate-action).
- [Traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability): every decision is tied to an artefact, a split and a test.

**Service levels:**
- [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) of the firewall ≤ 2× the T95 of ingestion in S2.1.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting) integrated at T90 ≤ one cycle of the
  [event clock](../../glossary.md#event-clock).
- An alert when a level is exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- metadata coverage is at or above its threshold (step A);
- the purged and embargoed splits are defined (S2.4);
- the overlap-aware weights are present (S2.3).

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- leakage score at *t0* = 0 on labels, and ≈ 0 on features;
- the time shift makes a significant difference, and label shuffling gives chance results;
- rank stability is above the chosen threshold;
- the plausibility checks show no unexplained systematic gap;
- a *go* note is issued and the artefacts are deposited.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun steps B to G when:
- data or label [drift](../../glossary.md#drift), seen by S6, exceeds its threshold;
- a regulatory or microstructure change occurs;
- a new scheme of events or labels, or a major new transformation, arrives;
- anomalies in S4 (execution simulation, cost analysis) suggest leakage or a proxy.

Monitoring (S6) consumes the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) (leakage, stability, plausibility) for live alerts. Lifecycle
(S7) keeps the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) policies: thresholds, periodicity, owners.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>A · Frame the work and inventory the artefacts</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a frame, testing is blind and yields false positives and false negatives.

**Actions.**
- List what arrives from S2.1 to S2.8: the event index (*t0*), time-stamped features, labels (*t1*,
  horizon), overlap-aware weights, purged and embargoed splits, quality flags, metadata
  (definitions, versions).
- Formalise the map of *t0* and *t1* (what is observable when) and the dependencies (who needs
  what).

**KPI.** Metadata coverage = artefacts with definitions and versions / all artefacts; target ≥ 0.98.

**Guardrail.** Step B does not run while coverage is below its threshold.

**Output.** "Who, what, when" specification and a completeness checklist → every following step,
and S4, which reuses the splits.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>B · Access at t0 and temporal integrity</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the frame set, it is known at last what to check, and when.

**Actions.**
- Check that each feature is available at *t0*: windows, aggregates, lags,
  [as-of joins](../../glossary.md#as-of-join).
- Check the alignment across assets and the synchronisation: no "negative" delay.
- Check the labels: definition of the horizon, barriers and expiries, no information after *t0*.

**KPI.** Leakage score at *t0* = artefacts not available at *t0* / artefacts tested; target → 0.
Alignment rate across assets; target ≥ 0.995.

**Guardrail.** Blocked when labels use information after *t0*; invalid as-of joins are logged.

**Output.** Report of access at *t0* (pass or fail per artefact) and a temporary exclusion mask →
step C (smoke tests), S2.1 to S2.6 (tickets on failure).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>C · Leakage smoke tests</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Only what is supposed admissible at *t0* is smoke-tested. The tests run as routine; an
abnormal result turns them into design work.

**Actions.**
- Time-shift test: shift features or labels by one step; naive performance must fall.
- Label shuffling: permute the labels; the metrics must fall to chance.
- Small purged and embargoed splits for quick tests: hygiene, not a validation of S4.

**KPI.** Shift delta: the relative fall of a naive metric after the shift, expected to be
significant. Chance score after label shuffling, expected ≈ chance.

**Guardrail.** When the shift delta ≈ 0, or the shuffled score exceeds its bound, leakage is
suspected: back to step B.

**Output.** Leakage score, shift delta and randomisation check → step D if clean, otherwise
tickets to S2.1 to S2.4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>D · Substitution and honest importance</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Estimating importance is pointless while the base leaks, so this follows step C.

**Actions.**
- Measure the single marginal value of each variable, without depending on a final model.
- Detect redundant groups (correlation, structure) and estimate an orthogonalised importance: the
  net effect, against proxies.
- Produce a substitution map: which variable replaces which, and to what degree
  ([substitution effect](../../glossary.md#substitution-effect)).

**KPI.** Substitution index per group (high means a proxy). Ratio of net to gross marginal value,
which detects "false glory".

**Guardrail.** A "proxy only" flag when net importance is far below gross importance, again and
again.

**Output.** Single ranking, net (orthogonalised) ranking, map of substitute groups → S2.8
(information and dimension), S3 (research priorities).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>E · Stability under purged splits and combinatorial cross-validation</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Test whether the conclusions of step D hold when the segments change.

**Actions.**
- Redo the measures of step D on purged and embargoed splits and under
  [combinatorial purged cross-validation](../../glossary.md#combinatorial-purged-cross-validation).
- Assess the stability of the ranks: their consistency across splits.

**KPI.** Rank correlation across splits: target high, for example ≥ 0.6. Dispersion of importance:
a bounded standard deviation.

**Guardrail.** When unstable, the variable is frozen for S3 with the status "exploratory".

**Output.** Stability maps (ranks, gaps) and a summary of robust against opportunistic variables →
S3 (priority matrix), S4 (proof that time hygiene is already respected).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>F · Statistical plausibility of features and labels</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** What seems to count is now known; check that the representation itself is plausible,
with [posterior predictive checks](../../glossary.md#posterior-predictive-check).

**Actions.**
- Define the minimal generative hypothesis of labels and features: distribution, key dependencies,
  tails.
- Replicate or simulate data under this hypothesis.
- Compare with the observed data: distributions, extremes, asymmetries, [calibration](../../glossary.md#calibration).

**KPI.** Coverage of the predictive p-values within a target band (for example 0.1–0.9), and the
rate of extremes. Calibration gap: the mean absolute gap to what is expected.

**Guardrail.** Systematic gaps trigger a redesign of labels or features (S2.2, S2.5, S2.6).

**Output.** Diagnostics of the posterior predictive checks
and a list of systematic gaps, with their remediations (labelling, horizons, transformations,
state encodings) → S2.2, S2.5 and S2.6 (design feedback), S3 (confidence in the label scheme).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>G · Go or no-go, and the remediation loop</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Every green and red light is in, so the decision comes last.

**Actions.**
- Compile a reasoned go or no-go: evidence from steps B to F, residual risks, scope.
- Open tickets to S2.1 to S2.8, prioritised and dated.
- Log in the registry (S2.10) and notify S3 and S4.

**KPI.** Go rate at the first pass, and the median time to remediate.

**Guardrail.** No-go whenever at least one hard [guardrail](../../glossary.md#guardrail) is active (steps B, C or F).

**Output.** Delivery note (dataset ready), log and tickets → S3 (starts), S4 (validation), S6 and
S7 (monitoring and lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Firewall specification: *t0* and *t1*, access rules, splits, targeted plausibility checks.
- Versioned compliance report (steps B to F) and the go or no-go note.
- Masks and maps: exclusion, substitution, stability, frozen segments.
- KPI dashboard, in real time and with history, with thresholds and alerts.
- Decision log (causes, remediations) and the tickets to S2.1 to S2.8.
- Interface contract for S3 and S4: formats, latency, traceability.
- Replacement process: what to do when a test turns red — who does what, and how fast.
