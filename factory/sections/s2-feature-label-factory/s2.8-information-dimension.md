# S2.8 — Information & dimension management

## TL;DR

Decides what to keep, what to group and how to compress, so that S3 and S4 receive a short core of features: informative, stable and cheap to serve.
Every measure is made inside the locked validation folds, and a selection counts only if it reappears under resampling and across regimes.
Delivers a traceable feature mask, a compression recipe proven neutral, and a report of value against cost.

## Purpose

Orchestrate **what to keep**, **what to group** and **how to compress**, so that S3 and S4 receive
a core of [features](../../glossary.md#feature) that is short, informative, stable and cheap to serve.

**Success looks like:** a traceable [feature mask](../../glossary.md#feature-mask), a stability
map, a reproducible [compression recipe](../../glossary.md#compression-recipe) and a report of
value against cost, all of which hold out of sample, under a known latency and budget.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The validation-ready index brings the purged and embargoed splits and their metadata; the event
index brings the clock, and the label set the horizons. The feature mask carries its stability map
and its exclusion notes. Sections 3 to 5 receive the concentrated datasets, masks, recipes and
replayable reports through the registry of [S2.10](s2.10-feature-registry.md): S3 to explore, S4
to validate without ambiguity, S5 to serve. The serving contract states IDs, formats, cost,
service levels and update frequencies, and comes with the report of value against cost.

**Constraints.**
- Formats: one time index, normalised asset keys, [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time)
  timestamps, explicit versions.
- Consistency: every transformation is pure (the same inputs give the same outputs), with no
  dependence on future data.

**Service levels:**
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) update targets per latency tier.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting) propagate a refresh of masks and recipes within a
  defined service level objective, for example N minutes or hours depending on the tier; otherwise
  an alert to S6.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the validation splits are validated (S2.4), the [labels](../../glossary.md#label) frozen (S2.2) and the sample weights
  available (S2.3);
- the features are stabilised and conditioned (S2.5 to S2.7), with their quality and costs;
- the budget and the service levels are signed (step 0).

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the final feature mask is versioned, with the stability map, the compression recipe, the report
  of value against cost and the exclusion notes;
- [train/serve parity](../../glossary.md#train-serve-parity) is validated and the service levels are met on a test batch;
- the registration in the registry (S2.10) is complete;
- the [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) table is in place, with alerts wired to S6.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- information scores [drift](../../glossary.md#drift), the [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) lift falls, or latency or cost rise above a
  threshold;
- the [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes (as detected by S2.6 or S6), market rules change (microstructure), or new
  feature families are added;
- a train/serve parity gap or a serving incident occurs;
- the minimum periodic cadence comes round (monthly or quarterly, depending on the tier), to
  refresh masks and recipes.

Monitoring (S6) receives the alert thresholds of the KPIs and the drift signatures (information
scores, stability, cost, latency). Lifecycle (S7) records the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) policy (triggers,
windows, thresholds) and the versioned [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback) plan.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>0 · Frame the budget and the evaluation scope</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Fix the frame of cost and latency, and the rules of temporal validation, before any
measure of information.

**Actions.**
- Define the latency tiers (real time, intraday, end of day, weekly) and a budget for computing,
  storage and serving per tier.
- Lock the validation index provided by S2.4: purged and embargoed time splits, horizons, regimes.
- List the candidate features (from S2.5 to S2.7) with their cost metadata (build and serve) and
  their point-in-time integrity.

**KPI.** Scope coverage: ≥ 99% of candidate features with cost and latency filled in.

**Guardrail.** Reject any feature without proof of point-in-time integrity or without a documented
serving cost.

**Output.** "Information and dimension" frame: tiers, budget, validation scheme, inventory of
features and costs → step 1.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Measure useful and marginal information, inside the folds</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Quantify what each feature tells about the target, and what it adds to the others,
inside the locked validation frame.

**Actions.**
- Assess the useful information of each feature, per horizon and per regime, with scores of
  predictive association suited to the target.
- Measure the [marginal information value](../../glossary.md#marginal-information-value), conditional on
  reference pivots: groups of features already known to be informative, simple [baselines](../../glossary.md#baseline).
- Consolidate an [information matrix](../../glossary.md#information-matrix) (feature × horizon ×
  regime) with its uncertainty: intervals, or the distribution of scores over resamples.

**KPI.** Median out-of-sample marginal lift per feature > 0, with a narrow interval. Rate of
unstable scores (a sign that changes between [folds](../../glossary.md#fold)) below a threshold.

**Guardrail.** Computation strictly inside the folds (no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage)), and no global tuning over all
the folds; an alert when the lift disappears out of sample.

**Output.** Information matrix and uncertainty bands → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Detect redundancy and group patterns</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Find the informational duplicates, and group the features that tell the same story.

**Actions.**
- Build a redundancy map: proximity by similarity of predictive contribution and by relevant
  co-movement.
- Form groups of near-equivalent features.
- For each group, list candidate representatives: more informative, cheaper and faster, more
  stable.

**KPI.** Residual redundancy rate below X%, measured by informational similarity after filtering.

**Guardrail.** Do not drop a "correlated" feature whose out-of-sample marginal information stays
significant and cheap: decide by marginal value.

**Output.** Redundancy map and candidate representatives → step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Stable, parsimonious selection, with far more features than observations</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Keep a minimal but robust subset, one that reappears under resampling and across
regimes.

**Actions.**
- Run a selection with resampling: independent iterations aligned with the splits, taking the
  sample weights of S2.3 into account.
- Compute the selection frequency per feature and per horizon; set stability thresholds that depend
  on the number of features, on the overlap and on the budget.
- Establish a selection mask v1 per latency tier, and per horizon and regime.

**KPI.** Stability at τ: the share of features above the threshold τ. The gap between training and
validation on a reduced, stable reference estimator.

**Guardrail.** No threshold is tuned on the final validation; thresholds are fixed in advance, in
the frame of step 0. An alert when features are never selected, or only in one very specific cut.

**Output.** Feature mask v1 and stability map → step 4, S3 (fast exploration).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Controlled compression, optional and proven neutral for prediction</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** When a shared pattern exists, project it onto a more compact basis
([dimensionality reduction](../../glossary.md#dimensionality-reduction)) without losing net
predictive power, and without making the pipeline opaque or fragile.

**Actions.**
- Define the units of compression: per group, per horizon, per tier.
- Learn a transformation, linear or not, inside the folds; freeze a recipe: parameters,
  normalisations, order of application.
- Test train/serve parity (the same inputs give the same outputs) and predictive neutrality: the
  out-of-sample change ≈ 0, or positive, within budget.

**KPI.** Compression ratio at its target, with an absolute out-of-sample change ≤ a threshold;
sound numerical conditioning of the representations (no explosions).

**Guardrail.** Refuse any compression whose performance depends on a setting fitted to the test
set; an alert when the transformation drifts as the data drifts (S6).

**Output.** Compression recipe and a report on parity and neutrality → step 5, S3 and S4
(compressed datasets).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Packaging, traceability and serving contracts</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn selection and compression into versioned artefacts that can be replayed, and
served under service levels.

**Actions.**
- Manage versions: IDs of masks and recipes, and their compatibility per horizon, regime and tier.
- Issue the report of value against cost (estimated predictive gain against the cost and latency
  of build and serving) and tie it to the service levels; define the T90 / T95 update targets and
  the alerts.
- Produce the exclusion notes (reason, date, tests) and push them to the registry of S2.10; expose
  the formats and interface contracts of features and embeddings.

**KPI.** Serving latency ≤ the service level of each tier; train/serve parity = 100% on control
samples; compute budget respected.

**Guardrail.** Parity tests at every build; an alert when the unit cost rises beyond a threshold,
or when T95 latency exceeds the service level.

**Output.** Final feature mask, stability map, compression recipe, report of value against cost,
exclusion notes and serving contract → S3 (concentrated datasets), S4 (clear validation), S5
(serving), S6 (monitoring), S7 (lifecycle), S2.10 (registry).

</details>

## Deliverables

- "Information and dimension" specification: scope, budgets, splits, horizons, regimes.
- Information matrix and uncertainty bands, per feature, horizon and regime.
- Redundancy map and candidate representatives.
- Final feature mask and stability map: frequencies, thresholds, decision.
- Compression recipe, with its tests of parity and predictive neutrality.
- Report of value against cost: estimated gain against the cost and latency of build and serving;
  compliance with the service levels.
- Exclusion notes: reason, evidence, date, owner.
- Serving contract: IDs, schema, dependencies, T90 / T95 update targets, service level objectives,
  hooks for S6.
- Decision log: full [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability), changelog, links to the validations of S4.
- KPI tracking table with alerts wired to S6, and a replacement and rollback procedure.
