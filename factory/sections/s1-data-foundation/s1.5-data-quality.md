# S1.5 — Data quality: rules, ML and human in the loop

## TL;DR

The factory's customs desk: every dataset is checked by rules, statistical detectors and people before anyone may use it.
Defects are repaired within bounds, never in place and never with future data; each dataset leaves with a quality certificate.
Below the quality threshold, or with a quarantine still open, downstream access is blocked.

## Purpose

Filter, detect, repair, trace and block everything that does not meet the factory's
[point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) standards. The sub-section combines
[deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) rules, anomaly detection (statistical and machine learning), reconciliation across
sources, bounded [imputation](../../glossary.md#imputation) and documented decisions by people
([human in the loop](../../glossary.md#human-in-the-loop)).

**Success looks like:** every dataset leaves [certified](../../glossary.md#certified-dataset),
traceable, replayable and ready for learning without [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

From S1.6 come the raw and adjusted views and their corrections, so that the consistency of raw and
adjusted data can be checked. Sections 2 to 5 — [features](../../glossary.md#feature) (S2), the
strategy lab (S3), validation (S4) and execution (S5) — consume the certificate and the access
contract: they meet them in the [sealed batches](../../glossary.md#sealed-batch) of
[S1.8](s1.8-data-governance.md).

**Constraints.**
- Format: unified types and timestamps; [provenance](../../glossary.md#provenance) mandatory; visibility and imputation flags on
  every value.
- Point-in-time consistency: every usable value respects publish time ≤ *t*.

**Service levels:**
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) integration of critical events (corrections, market actions, suspensions), defined
  per source and cohort.
- An [MTTD](../../glossary.md#mttd-mttr) target for anomalies, per provider, and an MTTR target for
  escalations.
- An alert when a target is missed 3 days in a row.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the time and point-in-time schemas are in place;
- the S1.2 and S1.4 packs are validated (daylight-saving and causality tests pass);
- the quality charter and defect taxonomy (step 1) are published.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- quality score ≥ threshold;
- [quarantine](../../glossary.md#quarantine) = 0, or documented;
- [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) = 100%;
- [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) stable for 2 weeks;
- access contracts active;
- [sentinel backtests](../../glossary.md#sentinel-backtest) green.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- a new provider, source or [venue](../../glossary.md#venue) arrives, or market rules or calendars change;
- distributions [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) persistently, or alerts or MTTR surge;
- the adjustment policies of S1.6 change significantly;
- an automatic trigger fires: the escalation rate rises by more than X%, service levels are
  missed repeatedly, or a sentinel backtest moves by more than its threshold.

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPI stream and the quality events, to correlate them with live
performance. Lifecycle (S7) versions the rule packs, detectors and runbooks; every change comes
with a migration checklist and a [canary release](../../glossary.md#canary-release).

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Quality frame and defect taxonomy</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a shared definition of a defect and of its severity, cleaning is blind: too
much or too little.

**Actions.**
- Write the quality charter: objectives, [fail-closed](../../glossary.md#fail-closed) versus
  fail-open, tolerances per use (research, production).
- Build a taxonomy of defects: missing fields, impossible values, inconsistencies across columns,
  breaks in time, microstructure anomalies, late revisions, duplicates, distribution drift,
  point-in-time misalignment.
- Define reference cohorts (by asset, venue, period, calendar) so that thresholds are contextual:
  never one global threshold.
- Build an annotated sample of known answers, to measure the
  [precision and recall](../../glossary.md#precision-and-recall) of the detectors.

**KPI.** Taxonomy coverage, the share of defects that are typed: ≥ 95%.

**Guardrail.** A monthly review of the severity matrix against real incidents; if more than 10% of
incidents fall outside the taxonomy, it must be updated.

**Output.** Charter, taxonomy, severity matrix and annotated reference set → steps 2 and 3 (rules,
detectors) and 6 (score).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Catalogues of rules and point-in-time constraints</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Simple defects are handled deterministically: fast and explainable.

**Actions.**
- Write checklists: impossible values, legal domains, condition codes, calendars (holidays,
  suspensions), consistency of raw and adjusted data, price within [bid, ask], volumes ≥ 0.
- Define dependency constraints (functional and conditional rules, denial constraints), temporal
  constraints (continuity, no going back, bounds on changes) and point-in-time invariants: no
  field may refer to information published after *t*.
- Formalise error messages: category, severity, action (correct, quarantine, escalate).

**KPI.** Detection rate of the rules (alerts per million rows) and their noise (estimated false
positives), per cohort.

**Guardrail.** Daylight-saving and time-zone tests, property-based tests on synthetic sets; when
noise exceeds its threshold, the rule is relaxed or segmented, never widened for comfort.

**Output.** Versioned rule packs, executable and documented → steps 3 (detection), 4 (repair) and
6 (certification).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Multi-layer anomaly detection: statistics and models</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Subtle defects escape simple rules: unjustified jumps, shifted series, drifts, quotes
arriving early.

**Actions.**
- Build statistical detectors per cohort: distributions, robust z-scores, time windows, calendar
  seasonality.
- Add distance, similarity and probabilistic detectors for outliers and disagreements between
  sources.
- Detect drift (distribution change), lags (abnormal delays) and
  structural breaks.
- Calibrate the sensitivity per cohort on the annotated set: target precision and recall, cost of
  an error.

**KPI.** Precision and recall ≥ their targets per defect type; MTTD at T95 under its threshold per
source.

**Guardrail.** Bitemporal [cross-validation](../../glossary.md#cross-validation), so that no detector consumes information after *t*;
shadow evaluation on frozen periods; an alert if precision stays below its threshold for 7 days.

**Output.** Scored anomaly events (score, type, evidence, cohort, severity) → steps 4 (repair), 5
(human triage), 6 (score) and 7 (impact).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Constrained repair and reconciliation</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Repairing everything is not desirable; whatever is repaired must stay point-in-time
safe, traceable and reversible.

**Actions.**
- Define a repair policy per type of defect:
  - prudent imputation: bounded, never using future data, bounded interpolation, and never
    smoothing away a real shock;
  - coalescing of intervals: states and periods without false edges;
  - de-duplication: grouping identical or similar records without losing their provenance;
  - reconciliation across providers: selection by freshness, error history and consistency,
    keeping the alternative versions.
- Log every repair in the [repair log](../../glossary.md#repair-log): before and after, rule,
  context, author (machine or person), justification.
- Never write in place: repairs are new dated, bitemporal versions.

**KPI.** Imputation rate per field, capped per use; share of reconciliations made automatically
versus escalated; repairs reverted, below a threshold.

**Guardrail.** Imputation caps per window and asset, beyond which the segment goes to quarantine;
automatic "no look-ahead" tests on every imputation strategy.

**Output.** Repaired tables, repair log and the alternative repairs kept → steps 5 (human arbitration) and 6
(certification), canonical views (S1.7), features (S2).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Human in the loop: triage and runbooks</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Some cases need judgment: a split or an error, an ambiguous news item, a rare break.

**Actions.**
- Write runbooks: decision trees, expected evidence, criteria to accept or refuse.
- Organise triage: queues by priority (impact, market urgency), with a service level for
  handling.
- Capture every decision (verdict, reasons) to retrain and recalibrate the detectors.
- Sample actively: send people the cases that teach the detectors the most.

**KPI.** MTTR at T95; escalation rate, which should fall as the system learns; agreement between
analysts (reliability).

**Guardrail.** Against alert fatigue: above X cases per analyst per day, raise thresholds or
segment; a weekly quality-control sample annotated twice.

**Output.** Traceable human decisions and enriched annotated data → steps 3 (better detectors), 4
(repairs), 6 (score) and 7 (impact).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Certification and access contracts</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a quality score and an access contract, downstream use cannot be blocked
cleanly.

**Actions.**
- Compute a quality score, weighted by severity, coverage, imputations and remaining
  uncertainty.
- Issue a certificate, the [data quality scorecard](../../glossary.md#data-quality-scorecard):
  score, alert counts, repairs, quarantined zones, version of the rules and detectors.
- Apply the [gates](../../glossary.md#gate): below the score threshold, or with segments in quarantine, downstream
  queries are blocked, or given a clearly flagged degraded access.

**KPI.** Share of datasets certified; active quarantine (size, duration).

**Guardrail.** Fail-closed by default in production; any exception needs a signed waiver and a
canary.

**Output.** Quality certificate, active gates and access contract (conditions) → features and
[labels](../../glossary.md#label) (S2), the strategy lab (S3), validation and execution simulation
(S4), execution (S5), monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Impact, replay and continuous monitoring</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Quality only means something through its impact on decisions and on [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility).

**Actions.**
- Run sentinel backtests: measure the change in performance and risk between raw, checked and
  repaired data.
- Replay deterministically (bitemporal): the same inputs give the same outputs, checked by hash.
- Build dashboards: MTTD and MTTR, precision and recall, imputations, quarantines, average score,
  drifts per source and cohort.
- Wire the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) triggers and the feedback loops towards rules, detectors and
  runbooks.

**KPI.** Sentinel backtest delta, which should converge to "checked ≈ repaired"; replay
mismatches = 0; stability of the score.

**Guardrail.** An alert when the delta exceeds a business threshold (for example on
[drawdown](../../glossary.md#drawdown) or [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover)); publication is
frozen on any replay mismatch.

**Output.** Impact measures, replay proofs, dashboards and alerts → monitoring (S6), lifecycle
(S7), validation (S4).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Quality charter, defect taxonomy and severity matrix.
- Rule and constraint packs, executable and documented, with daylight-saving and point-in-time
  tests.
- Calibrated detectors, the annotated set, and precision and recall reports.
- Repair policies (bounded imputation, coalescing, reconciliation) and the repair log.
- Escalation runbooks, service levels and decision templates.
- Standard quality certificate, access contracts and gates.
- Sentinel backtests, replay harness and KPI dashboards.
- Incident runbook (quarantine, [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback), root-cause analysis) and provider replacement
  procedure.
