# S1.7 — Canonical market views & microstructure primitives

## TL;DR

Turns the raw flow of ticks, quotes and corrections into one standard set of bars and microstructure measures for the whole factory.
Every view is point-in-time safe and deterministic: zero leakage, bit-identical replays, labelled by family, session and view.
Features, execution simulation, cost analysis and monitoring all measure the market with these yardsticks.

## Purpose

Turn the heterogeneous raw flow (ticks, quotes, corrections, [venues](../../glossary.md#venue)) into stable
[canonical market views](../../glossary.md#canonical-market-view): standardised
[bars](../../glossary.md#bar) and [microstructure primitives](../../glossary.md#microstructure-primitive)
([spreads](../../glossary.md#spread), depth, costs, volatility). Every computation gives the same result bit for bit for a
given as-of date, with no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) and no ambiguity, so that the whole factory shares one grammar
of the market.

**Success looks like:**
- Zero leakage and 100% determinism, [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) publication delays under target, manifests on 100% of
  outputs.
- One definition per measure: results from different teams can be compared.

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the S1.6 policy is in production, with [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) safe raw and adjusted views;
- [bitemporality](../../glossary.md#bitemporality) is tested (as-of [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) works) and the total order is active;
- the canonical grammar (step 1) is signed off and published.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the key [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) are green: leakage = 0, determinism = 100%, T95 under target, manifests = 100%;
- the invariant batteries pass over one month, across several venues and sessions;
- the full pack of deliverables is available.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun in depth when:
- a market rule changes (for example the auction mechanism) or a venue is added;
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) persistently beyond its threshold;
- the grammar gets a new version;
- cost analysis durably disagrees with actual fills.

Monitoring (S6) triggers [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification); lifecycle (S7) manages versions, deprecations and
controlled migrations.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Freeze the canonical grammar: definitions</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a shared grammar, every team reinvents the market and results cannot be
compared.

**Actions.**
- Choose the official bars to maintain: time, tick, volume, dollar and information-driven. For
  each family: windows, alignment, open-high-low-close rule, handling of gaps, halts and auctions.
- Define the standard microstructure primitives: quoted, effective and realised
  [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), the chosen mid, depth and imbalance, micro-horizon
  volatility, ex-ante and ex-post costs (arrival, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage),
  impact), resilience and liquidity.
- Fix the as-of semantics: *as traded*; *as of*, point-in-time, without future corrections;
  *as corrected*, with late corrections.
- Define sessions and venues: open, close, auctions, halts; consolidated versus per venue.

**KPI.** Definition coverage ≥ 99% of the cases met; 0 "unknown".

**Guardrail.** A monthly cross-review, with documented diffs and versions.

**Output.** Canonical specification: a signed document and a dictionary of measures → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Normalise and filter the feeds: point-in-time pre-processing</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Prepare clean, point-in-time safe material before consolidation.

**Actions.**
- Timestamps and time zones: one reference, daylight-saving and high-frequency rules, documented
  precision.
- Condition codes and filters: late or out-of-sequence trades, odd lots, off-book trades,
  corrections and cancellations, auctions. Label them, never destroy the information.
- Segment by session: pre-open, continuous trading, auctions, after-hours.
- Point-in-time [gate](../../glossary.md#gate): as of a given instant, exclude the corrections received after it, and keep
  them for the as-corrected view.

**KPI.** Filtering rate (late trades, odd lots…) stable per asset and venue, with an
investigation when it drifts by more than 3σ. Point-in-time integrity: 100% of [as-of views](../../glossary.md#as-of-view)
without any future correction.

**Guardrail.** As-of tests on sentinel days such as earnings releases and index rebalances.

**Output.** Cleaned, labelled feeds of ticks and quotes, ready to consolidate → step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Consolidate and align: venues, mid, trade signing</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without careful consolidation and alignment, every microstructure measure is biased.

**Actions.**
- Set the consolidation policy: consolidated best bid and ask versus per venue, tie-breaks,
  latencies between venues.
- Choose the [reference mid](../../glossary.md#reference-mid-price) (consolidated top of book, or
  per venue) according to its use: cost analysis or research.
- Align trades with quotes: matching window, priorities, edge cases such as empty quotes.
- [Sign trades](../../glossary.md#trade-signing) by a stable rule (the aggressor's side), and label
  the trades that cannot be signed.

**KPI.** Unmatched trades below a threshold (for example < 0.5%); unknown signs below a threshold
(for example < 1%); trade-to-quote latency at T95 under its target per venue.

**Guardrail.** Audits across venues of the gaps in mid and quoted spread, on fixed windows.

**Output.** Aligned, signed feeds: consolidated quotes and signed trades → step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Build the bars: determinism and gaps</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Bars are the standard board everyone builds on.

**Actions.**
- Generate the bars (time, tick, volume, dollar, information-driven) by the grammar, with a clear
  open-high-low-close rule: priorities, last valid price.
- Represent gaps and halts explicitly, with flags: no implicit filling that invents prices.
- Guarantee determinism: the same inputs in the same order give the same bars, since the total
  order is guaranteed.

**KPI.** Determinism: bit-for-bit variance = 0 on replays. Publication delay at T95, from the end
of the window to bars ready, under target per horizon (for example a few seconds for one-minute
bars).

**Guardrail.** Canaries on days with halts and auctions: no phantom filling.

**Output.** Canonical bar tables, labelled by family, session and as-of view → step 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Compute the microstructure primitives</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** These are the yardsticks used by features and labels, execution simulation, cost
analysis and monitoring.

**Actions.**
- Spreads: quoted (ask − bid), effective (direction × (trade price − mid at *t*)), realised
  (direction × (trade price − mid at *t* + Δ)).
- Depth and imbalance: levels of the [order book](../../glossary.md#order-book), aggregated
  consistently.
- Micro volatility, with explicit definitions and windows.
- Costs: ex-ante (arrival price versus mid) and ex-post (slippage, short- and long-term
  [market impact](../../glossary.md#market-impact)).
- Resilience: the time spread and depth take to recover after a shock.

**KPI.** Consistency with cost analysis: aggregated costs close to the fills of the
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) and of [paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading) (median gap
below a threshold). Stability of the measures outside events (for example the distribution of
effective spreads), with controlled drift.

**Guardrail.** Check that effective and realised spreads roughly agree over a short Δ in calm
markets.

**Output.** Versioned tables of microstructure measures → step 6, and sections 2, 4 and 6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Versioning, replay and quality: manifests and tests</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Guarantee trust, audit and non-regression.

**Actions.**
- Write [manifests](../../glossary.md#dataset-manifest): inputs (versions, as-of date), rules
  applied, hashes of the outputs.
- Test determinism with random bit-for-bit replays.
- Test for leakage with sentinel queries, as of versus as corrected.
- Build dashboards: KPIs, alerts, a red list of assets and venues.

**KPI.** [Reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility): 100% of replays succeed on daily samples. Anomaly rate per million
points, trending down; [MTTR](../../glossary.md#mttd-mttr) < 24 h.

**Guardrail.** Publication blocked when leakage > 0 or when determinism breaks.

**Output.** Version registry, dashboards and test batteries → monitoring (S6), lifecycle (S7), the
publication gates.

</details>

**Specific guardrails.** Five failure modes get a dedicated test:

| Failure mode | Test |
|---|---|
| Leakage: an as-of view that sees future corrections | Daily sentinel queries, as of versus as corrected. |
| Double counting across venues | A conservation test on volumes and prices during consolidation. |
| Wrong trade signing | An audit of trade direction against small moves of the mid, with a threshold on inconsistencies. |
| A definition changed silently (mid, windows, filters) | A mandatory semantic diff, and requalification. |
| Sessions and auctions mishandled | Dedicated checks at the open and the close, so that auctions never contaminate continuous trading. |

## Deliverables

- Canonical specification: bars, primitives, as-of and as-corrected semantics, sessions and
  venues.
- Dictionary of condition codes and filters: late, out-of-sequence, odd lots, corrections,
  auctions.
- Consolidation and mid rules: tie-breaks, per venue versus consolidated, latencies.
- Alignment and trade-signing rules: windows, priorities, edge cases.
- Bar builder for every family kept, with gap and halt handling and strict labelling.
- Library of primitives (spreads, depth and imbalance, micro volatility, costs, resilience), with
  tests.
- Manifests and version registry (hashes, dependencies, as-of dates, signatures), and the replay
  procedure.
- KPI dashboards and alerting: leakage, determinism, T95, drifts.
- Runbooks: diagnosing a jump, a trade-quote mismatch or a spread drift; [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback) and migration.
- Decision log: changes of definitions, measured impacts, validations.
