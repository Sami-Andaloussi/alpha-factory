# S1.4 — Causality & multi-source synchronisation

## TL;DR

Rebuilds, at any instant, exactly what an observer could have seen then, across prices, fundamentals, macro data and news.
A value joins a panel only once it was published: zero causal violations, and every join is logged and replayable.
Canonical market views, quality control and the gate of S1.8 build on these panels.

## Purpose

Rebuild, at every instant *t*, the exact view a person would have had at *t*, by aligning several
feeds (prices, fundamentals, macro data, news) without ever using information published after
that instant. The sub-section imposes verifiable [causal alignment](../../glossary.md#causal-alignment),
controlled latency, full [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) and [deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) [replay](../../glossary.md#replay).

**Success looks like:**
- Zero [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage): no joined value was published after its reference time.
- Temporal consistency across all sources, with every alignment logged and replayable.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

A causal panel carries, per row: instrument, [venue](../../glossary.md#venue) (optional), reference
time, and for each joined value its event time, publish time, value, visibility flags, alignment
rule, alignment lag and [lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage) ID. Downstream, sections 2 to 5 —
[features](../../glossary.md#feature) (S2), the strategy lab (S3), validation (S4) and execution
(S5) — receive panels and feeds through the [sealed batches](../../glossary.md#sealed-batch) of
[S1.8](s1.8-data-governance.md).

**Constraints.**
- Consistency: publish time ≤ reference time for every joined field.
- Format: normalised time zones, unified time types, stable total sequences.
- Integration: reads through the [as-of API](../../glossary.md#as-of-api) only; no access to a
  "latest state" outside a time slice.

**Service levels** (to define per market): a latency target per type of data, for example macro
releases at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ X seconds and corrections at T95 ≤ Y seconds.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) identities and the universe are validated;
- the deterministic [timebase](../../glossary.md#timebase) of S1.3 is available;
- the time schemas (event, publish, ingest) are defined and the daylight-saving tests pass.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- 0 causal violations on the test bench;
- replay = 100%;
- the microstructure [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) are within their thresholds;
- the latency service levels are met 2 weeks in a row;
- the alignment log is operational.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- a source, provider or venue changes, market rules or trading hours change, or a new family of
  feeds is introduced;
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) durably: more than 20% of its threshold over 7 days;
- an automatic trigger fires: alignment rejections rise, late corrections surge, or clock drift
  grows (step 2).

Monitoring (S6) receives a continuous stream of KPIs: violations, latencies, microstructure
anomalies, rejections. Lifecycle (S7) versions the alignment contracts: any change requires a
qualification replay and a [canary release](../../glossary.md#canary-release) in production.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Map each source's visibility</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a visibility calendar (event time versus publication time), every join is wrong
by default.

**Actions.**
- Define, per source, the mandatory fields: event time, publish time (or its equivalent),
  ingest time, time zone, latency hint, source ID.
- Set the timestamp priority rules, for example official publication before network reception.
- List systematic delays (such as news distribution), news held back until a set release time,
  and the cases of errata and corrections.

**KPI.** Visibility coverage: share of records with a publish time; target ≥ 99.5%.

**Guardrail.** Time-zone and daylight-saving tests on pivot dates; a property test "publish time
≥ event time", failing which the record goes to [quarantine](../../glossary.md#quarantine).

**Output.** A versioned visibility calendar and a dictionary of time fields per source → steps
2, 3 and 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Common clock and timestamp normalisation</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** A single logical timebase lets every event be compared and ordered without ambiguity.

**Actions.**
- Choose a [reference clock](../../glossary.md#reference-clock): unit, granularity, rounding
  policy.
- Write the normalisation rules: time-zone conversion, rounding, and the strategy for ties
  (a stable total order).
- Specify the drift accepted per source and how it is measured continuously.

**KPI.** Drift between sources (P95 / P99), |source clock − reference clock|: target P95 < 50 ms
and P99 < 200 ms within a market, adjusted per domain.

**Guardrail.** Monotonic sequences per source; a sanity check on clock-change days.

**Output.** Clock specification and normalisation functions, pure and deterministic → steps 3,
4 and 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Bitemporal ingestion</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Separating validity from knowledge makes "what we knew, and when" replayable.

**Actions.**
- Store for each record its event time, its publish time (when it became visible), its ingest
  time ([transaction time](../../glossary.md#transaction-time)), and a version ID or sequence number.
- Forbid any destructive change: the log is append-only, and corrections are new dated states.
- Define the as-of and time-slice operations used for reads.

**KPI.** Replayability: a replay as of *T* reproduces the sequence identically, with a stable log
hash; target 100%.

**Guardrail.** Bitemporal non-regression tests on interval packing and unpacking; an assertion
that nothing is updated in place.

**Output.** Bitemporal ingest tables and an as-of read interface → steps 4 to 6, canonical views
(S1.7), the replayer, quality control (S1.5).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Causal alignment engine: joins "as visible at t"</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The core of the sub-section: multi-feed panels without look-ahead.

**Actions.**
- Define the causal join operator: at *t*, join only the records whose publish time ≤ *t*, with
  explicit rules for equality and strictness.
- Set the missing-data policy: accept the gap, with no speculative [imputation](../../glossary.md#imputation), and tag it
  "missing by design".
- Use [watermarks](../../glossary.md#watermark) and buffers: wait just long enough, in a bounded
  window, to gather late arrivals without blocking the flow.

**KPI.** Causal violations, the number of values used with publish time > *t*: target 0.
Alignment rejections, the share of joins refused for insufficient visibility, tracked per source.

**Guardrail.** "Time-travel" tests, synthetic cases where leakage is the only possible
explanation; shadow runs comparing causal joins with "nearest timestamp" joins.

**Output.** Causal operator specification and [causal panel](../../glossary.md#causal-panel)
contracts (schema and guarantees) → step 5 (prices and [order book](../../glossary.md#order-book)), canonical views (S1.7), the
feature factory (S2).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Multi-venue consolidation with faithful microstructure</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Mixing venues without rules breaks the causality of prices and order books.

**Actions.**
- Keep the origin of every record (venue, condition code) and rebuild trades and quotes in one
  stable sequence (a total order).
- [Sign each trade](../../glossary.md#trade-signing) against the quote state visible at its
  timestamp; treat corrections and cancellations as new events, never by going back in time.
- Document the [consolidation](../../glossary.md#multi-venue-consolidation) rules: allowed
  aggregations, priorities, exclusions.

**KPI.** Microstructure anomalies, the share of trades outside the observable [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), crossed
quotes or non-monotonic sequences: targets ≈ 0, tolerance under 0.1% depending on the market.
Corrections caught, the share integrated without rewriting history.

**Guardrail.** Microstructure invariants (order and monotonicity; price within [bid, ask] where
it applies) and recovery tests after corrections.

**Output.** Normalised per-venue feeds and a documented, traceable
[consolidated market feed](../../glossary.md#consolidated-market-feed) → canonical views ([bars](../../glossary.md#bar),
[liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity)), execution simulation and cost analysis (S4), monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Alignment log and latency service levels</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Auditability, with costs and latencies under control.

**Actions.**
- Write an [alignment log](../../glossary.md#alignment-log): who joined what, when, with what
  lag, under which rule.
- Define latency service levels per type of event (macro, fundamentals, news, corrections) and
  the degradation policies: deliver partially or wait.
- Provide a deterministic replayer: the same inputs give the same outputs.

**KPI.** Latency across feeds (P95 / P99), panel production time minus the latest publish time of
its sources, with targets per type of event. Replay mismatches: 0%.

**Guardrail.** Hashing of every stage; golden traces replayed daily.

**Output.** The alignment log, the latency service levels and an anomaly dashboard → governance
(S1.8), monitoring (S6).

</details>

## Deliverables

- "Visibility and clock" specification: timestamp priorities, time-zone and daylight-saving
  rules, rounding.
- Causal operator contract ("as visible at t"), with the missing-data policy and watermarks.
- Documented bitemporal schemas and as-of / time-slice interface.
- Multi-venue consolidation rules: origin, corrections, trade signing.
- Alignment log: format, retention, signature, audit queries.
- Test set: daylight saving, time travel, microstructure invariants, golden traces for replay.
- KPI dashboard (thresholds, alerts, service levels) and incident runbook (quarantine, failover,
  root-cause analysis).
- Replacement procedure for a source or venue: acceptance criteria, canary, [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).
