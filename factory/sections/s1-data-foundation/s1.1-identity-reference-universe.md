# S1.1 — Identity, reference data & point-in-time universe

## TL;DR

Knows who each instrument is, where it trades and whether it could be traded, on any past day.
Delivers an identity resolver and a daily point-in-time universe: nothing included before it listed, and a hash that replays identically.
Every downstream join on instruments goes through them, dated "as of".

## Purpose

Bring together *who is what, when and where* for every instrument. The sub-section delivers two
services that can be replayed and are free of [survivorship bias](../../glossary.md#survivorship-bias):

- an [identity resolver](../../glossary.md#identity-resolver): given an identifier and a date, it
  returns the [canonical instrument](../../glossary.md#canonical-instrument) of that day, on the
  right [venue](../../glossary.md#venue);
- daily [point-in-time universe](../../glossary.md#point-in-time-universe) tables: the instruments
  that could actually be traded each day, with the reason each one entered or left.

**Success looks like:**
- The same inputs give the same outputs, today and in a year.
- Zero inclusion before an IPO.
- Zero ambiguity left unhandled.
- [As-of joins](../../glossary.md#as-of-join) enforced for every downstream consumer.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The source feeds are optional here: they are read only to check that an instrument has the
minimum market data needed to count as tradable. The dictionaries of identifier namespaces and
venues are published with the identity resolver. Downstream sections receive the universe through the [sealed batches](../../glossary.md#sealed-batch)
of [S1.8](s1.8-data-governance.md).

**Constraints.**
- An alias (identifier namespace plus value) never points to more than one instrument on the same day.
- An instrument may have several listings, but never two on the same venue at the same time.
- Every eligibility decision can be re-explained: its reason is coded.

**Service levels** (reasonable targets, to adapt):
- IPO: universe updated before 08:00 local time the next day, in 95% of cases ([T95](../../glossary.md#t95)).
- Delisting or suspension: T95 under one hour after it takes effect during market hours;
  before 08:00 the next day otherwise.
- Resolution: 99.9% of requests under 100 ms (logic only), 100% [deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic).
- Manifest: published with every universe delivery, never an "orphan" delivery.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the glossary, identifier namespaces and venues are frozen;
- the schema and its constraints have been reviewed;
- a set of test cases covers the usual ambiguities.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the as-of resolver passes 100% of the deterministic tests and fails cleanly on ambiguous cases;
- the point-in-time universe is delivered over 3 months of history plus 1 current month, with a
  stable hash on [replay](../../glossary.md#replay);
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of the steps are above their thresholds over 2 delivery cycles;
- the as-of join templates and the no-survivor tests are active in the test and CI environments.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the full tests when:
- market rules change (new venues, new calendars);
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): more ambiguities, lower coverage, sources diverging;
- a new provider arrives or an upstream schema changes;
- monitoring (S6) reports a blocking incident, or execution simulation and cost analysis disagree.

Monitoring receives the KPIs, manifests, daily deltas and the ambiguity and survivorship alerts.
Lifecycle (S7) receives changes to eligibility rules, arbitration decisions between sources,
incident postmortems and [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) criteria.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Map the ground: sources, codes, events</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Know every identifier and every identity event the model will have to handle.

**Actions.**
- List every identifier namespace in use: tickers per market, ISIN and SEDOL, issuer codes,
  venue codes.
- List every identity and listing event available: IPO, delisting, suspension, change of symbol
  or name, merger or split-off, move to another venue, halt or unscheduled event.
- Define a controlled vocabulary: entity types (instrument, issuer, listing, venue), event types,
  reason codes.

**KPI.** Namespace coverage = namespaces used / namespaces known; alert below 95% (blind spots).
Typed events = events with a valid type and justification; alert below 98%.

**Output.** Source catalogue, reference dictionaries and event glossary → steps 2 and 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Canonical identity model and temporal graph</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** One single form to link heterogeneous codes over time.

**Actions.**
- Formalise the canonical objects: *instrument* (stable canonical key), *listing* (instrument ×
  venue × eligibility period), *issuer* (parent company, history of changes), *identifier
  alias* (identifier, namespace, validity period).
- Set the temporal constraints: clean closed or open periods (no illegal overlap for one alias
  in one namespace), one meaning per date (on a given date an alias points to at most one
  instrument), causality (an update cannot precede its origin).

**KPI.** Interval conflicts per key (illegal overlaps); alert above 0. As-of [uniqueness](../../glossary.md#uniqueness) (one
alias → at most one instrument per date); alert below 100%.

**Guardrail.** Systematic overlap check on intervals; sequenced keys, so no temporal duplicate.

**Output.** Logical schema and integrity rules, as text and test cases → steps 3 and 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Ingest and normalise into atomic events</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Identities resolve cleanly only if the facts are atomic and dated.

**Actions.**
- Turn raw feeds into normalised events: type, object, start / end, source, justification.
- Clean and standardise timestamps (announcement time versus effective time), namespaces
  (prefix plus value) and venues (one mapping each).
- Mark uncertainty explicitly with an "ambiguous, to arbitrate" flag.

**KPI.** Ingestion success = valid events / all events; alert below 99%. Share of ambiguities
flagged rather than silent; alert if it falls (a sign of masking).

**Guardrail.** Checks on critical fields: effective date not in the future, venue known,
namespace known.

**Output.** Normalised event tables and import log → steps 4 and 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · As-of identity resolution service</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Every downstream join depends on a deterministic resolver.

**Actions.**
- Define the logical interface: in, (namespace, value, date); out, (canonical instrument, valid
  listing and venue, status).
- Write the decision rules: recycled tickers, priorities across venues, explicit tie-breakers,
  and failure when the answer is ambiguous: the resolver never invents one.
- Make it deterministic: the same input gives the same output, versioned (algorithm plus mapping
  tables).
- Build a test harness of edge cases: symbol change at *t*, reused ticker, merger, multiple
  listings, suspension.

**KPI.** Resolution without ambiguity ≥ 99%; alert below 99%. Determinism (same input, same
output) = 100%; alert below 100%.

**Guardrail.** Trap cases in the tests (recycled ticker, multiple listings, merger with symbol
change, long suspension); [fail-closed](../../glossary.md#fail-closed) on ambiguity.

**Output.** Specification, test batteries and version manifests → step 5 and every downstream
consumer ([features](../../glossary.md#feature), the strategy lab, validation, execution,
monitoring).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Build the daily point-in-time universes</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Freeze who was tradable on each date.

**Actions.**
- State the eligibility rules: listed, not suspended, minimum data available, observation
  window, preferred venue when an instrument is listed twice.
- Generate a compact view per day: date, canonical instrument, venue, status, reason for entry
  or exit, first seen, last seen.
- Join the resolver, so that there is zero survivorship, and record every reason for exclusion.
- Version the methods and rules: a [fingerprint](../../glossary.md#fingerprint) (hash) of the rule set and a version ID.

**KPI.** [Point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) coverage = tradable instruments covered / instruments expected, per
market; alert below 99%. First-appearance lag, in days between the official event and the first
inclusion; alert above its service level. Explained churn, the share of entries and exits with a
documented reason; alert below 99%.

**Guardrail.** No inclusion before an instrument's first-seen date; every refusal is logged with
its reason.

**Output.** Daily point-in-time universe tables and their changelog (day-over-day diffs) →
feature and [label](../../glossary.md#label) factory (S2), strategy lab (S3), validation and execution simulation (S4).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Survivorship guardrails and mandatory as-of joins</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Lock out misuse at the source.

**Actions.**
- Refuse by default any join without a reference date.
- Add a "no-survivor" test: a pipeline that includes an instrument before its first-seen date is
  blocked with a hard error.
- Provide standard as-of join templates with the checks built in.

**KPI.** Out-of-point-in-time queries blocked, as a count and a ratio; alert at zero while there
is traffic (a possible bypass) or on a surge (poor adoption). False positives of the no-survivor
test ≤ 0.5% of runs.

**Guardrail.** Every join requires an as-of parameter; without one it fails.

**Output.** Enforcement rules, an intrusion test suite and query templates → every downstream
consumer of market data.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Traceability and replay: manifests, hashes, log</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without proof, there is no credibility.

**Actions.**
- Issue a [dataset manifest](../../glossary.md#dataset-manifest) with every delivery: sources,
  versions, rules, time window, dataset hash, count of ambiguities.
- Keep the diffs (additions, exits, corrections) with their reasons (IPO, delisting…).
- Provide a date-by-date [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) script that rebuilds the universe
  identically, checked bit for bit.

**KPI.** [Reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility) = identical hash on replay; alert on any difference. Divergence between
sources (status or venue differing for the same instrument and date); alert above an agreed
threshold, for example 0.2% of rows a day.

**Guardrail.** No manifest, no delivery: any export without a manifest is rejected downstream.

**Output.** Versioned manifests, replay registers and comparison reports → monitoring (S6),
lifecycle (S7), internal and external audits.

</details>

## Deliverables

- Identity and point-in-time universe specification: model, constraints, eligibility rules.
- Source catalogue and dictionaries: namespaces, venues, events.
- As-of identity resolution service: logical interface and tests.
- Point-in-time universe tables with versioned manifests.
- As-of join templates and no-survivor tests.
- KPI dashboard and decision log.
- Operations runbook: service levels, escalation, requalification.
