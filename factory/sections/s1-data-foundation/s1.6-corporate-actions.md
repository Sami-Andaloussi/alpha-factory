# S1.6 — Corporate actions, adjustments & traceability

## TL;DR

Keeps price and volume series consistent through splits, dividends, mergers and symbol changes, without leaking the future.
Serves three labelled views as of any date (raw, split-adjusted, total-return), with zero adjusted values before an ex-date.
Every adjustment is versioned, idempotent, replayable bit for bit and explained by its event.

## Purpose

Keep series consistent in time despite [corporate actions](../../glossary.md#corporate-action)
(splits, dividends, mergers, changes of symbol), without [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage). For any as-of date, every series
provides the right view (raw, split-adjusted or total-return), replayable bit for bit and
auditable, with guaranteed continuity and an explicit, versioned adjustment policy.

**Success looks like:**
- Zero leakage: no adjusted value is visible before its [ex-date](../../glossary.md#ex-date).
- 100% [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility) and 100% of outputs delivered with a manifest.
- No series ever mixes views.

The source feeds bring raw prices and volumes and the providers' event files. The page's series go

**Constraints.**
- Formats: normalised identifiers, explicit time zones, known granularity.
- Consistency: a view label on every output, with the version of the events it reflects.

**Service levels:**
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) propagation of a corporate action, from ex-date to the views, per listing [venue](../../glossary.md#venue).
- Availability of [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) views ≥ 99.9% during market hours.
- An alert when T95 exceeds its threshold 3 days in a row, or on any leakage.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the point-in-time universe is operational, with identities ≥ 99.9% resolved;
- [bitemporality](../../glossary.md#bitemporality) is tested (as-of [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) works);
- the adjustment policy is signed off (step 1).

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) at leakage = 0, reproducibility = 100%, manifests = 100%;
- the invariant batteries pass on one month of multi-market history;
- the documentation is complete and the correction runbooks are published.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the deep tests when:
- anomalies spike over a quarter;
- a market rule changes, for example the meaning of the ex-date;
- a provider restates its history massively;
- a new type of event is added;
- the T95 latency [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift).

Monitoring (S6) triggers [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification); lifecycle (S7) arbitrates updates of the policy and the
migration of versions.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Define the adjustment policy and the event semantics</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** This is the contract of truth, and everything else bends to it, so it comes first.

**Actions.**
- Define three official views: *raw* (for execution), *split-adjusted* (mechanical) and
  *[total-return](../../glossary.md#total-return)* (dividends reinvested).
- For each field (price, volume, market capitalisation…), write the adjustment rule and the
  visibility date: announcement, ex-date, record date or payment date.
- List the types of event (split, cash or stock dividend, rights issue, symbol change, merger,
  spin-off…) and their impact on each field.

**KPI.** Semantic coverage, the share of events covered by an explicit rule: target ≥ 99%.

**Guardrail.** A monthly cross-review; no "other" or "unknown" event in production.

**Output.** Adjustment policy specification (the contract) and event dictionary → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Model events and factors bitemporally</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Put the policy into a model that can replay "what we knew, and when".

**Actions.**
- Use a bitemporal schema: [valid time](../../glossary.md#valid-time) (when the event is true)
  and [transaction time](../../glossary.md#transaction-time) (when the factory learned it).
- Keep tables of corporate action events and of versioned
  [adjustment factors](../../glossary.md#adjustment-factor) (for example a 2-for-1 split: 0.5 on
  prices, ×2 on volumes), each with its source and timestamps.
- Write the ordering rules for events that collide, and how late corrections (restatements) are
  resolved.

**KPI.** Point-in-time integrity, the share of as-of [snapshots](../../glossary.md#snapshot) in which no event is visible
before its ex-date: target 100%.

**Guardrail.** Systematic as-of tests on sentinel sets built around pivot dates.

**Output.** A bitemporal model of events and factors, ready to populate → step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Build the adjustment calculator and the factor assembler</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Apply the semantics to the series, at the right date, with the right version.

**Actions.**
- Align each event with the point-in-time universe: resolved identities, mappings between
  symbol, ISIN and permanent identifiers.
- Compute the elementary factors (price, volume, market capitalisation…) per event, and compose
  them when events follow one another.
- Make it [idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent): no double adjustment when the provider has
  already back-adjusted.

**KPI.** Idempotence, the rate of double adjustments detected: target 0. Multiplicative
consistency, |product of the factors over a window − cumulative factor|: threshold < 1e-6
relative.

**Guardrail.** Benches of extreme cases: successive splits, a cash and a stock dividend in the
same week, a merger with a change of symbol.

**Output.** Table of composed factors per asset, date and version, and the functions that apply
them per view → step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Point-in-time view engine with anti-leakage gates</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Knowing how to apply adjustments, serve the right view as of any date without revealing
the future.

**Actions.**
- Build an [as-of view](../../glossary.md#as-of-view) selector: before the ex-date, raw; after it, the adjusted view the policy
  prescribes; total-return adds the cash only on the payment date, or by the rule set in step 1.
- Implement point-in-time [gates](../../glossary.md#gate): a back-adjusted series cannot be obtained as of a date before
  the event.
- Distinguish forward-adjusted series (for execution) from back-adjusted ones (for analysis), and
  label every output.

**KPI.** Leakage, the number of as-of queries before the ex-date that return adjusted values:
target 0. Propagation latency from ex-date to corrected view, T95: target under 2 h for equities
in developed markets, and by the next day for fundamentals.

**Guardrail.** Canaries that compare raw and adjusted series, tick by tick or [bar](../../glossary.md#bar) by bar, in
windows around events.

**Output.** A point-in-time query layer producing [adjusted series](../../glossary.md#adjusted-series)
ready for canonical views, the lab and execution simulation → steps 5 ([traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability)) and 6
(quality).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Traceability and replay: manifests, signatures, provenance</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Log what is served, to audit it and replay it.

**Actions.**
- Write a [manifest](../../glossary.md#dataset-manifest) per transformation batch: inputs, policy
  used, event versions, timestamps, hash of the outputs.
- Keep an end-to-end [provenance](../../glossary.md#provenance) chain (raw data → factors →
  views), with cross-references to the events that explain each change.
- Replay bit for bit: the same as-of date and the same versions give the same bytes.

**KPI.** Bit-for-bit reproducibility over 30 days of random replays: target 100%. Manifest
completeness: 100% of outputs delivered with a valid manifest.

**Guardrail.** Hash checks after each build and before publication.

**Output.** Manifest registry and a documented replay procedure → step 6, monitoring (S6),
lifecycle (S7).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Quality and anti-leakage checks, with a correction loop</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the proof in hand, measure and correct.

**Actions.**
- Detect unexplained jumps, inconsistent factors, double adjustments, temporal leaks (an event
  visible too early) and gaps in history after mergers.
- Test invariants: continuity of total return when nothing happens in the market, stability of
  the expected ratios before and after a split, volumes × factor consistent.
- Run a remediation loop: a local correction, a new version, a controlled replay, and the impact
  pushed downstream.

**KPI.** Anomaly rate per million points, trending down. Correction [MTTR](../../glossary.md#mttd-mttr), the median time from
detection to resolution: target < 24 h.

**Guardrail.** Publication blocked when leakage is detected; a red list of assets or sectors under
closer watch.

**Output.** Anomaly reports, versioned corrections and a KPI dashboard → canonical views (S1.7);
every reader of the series, sections 2 to 5, through S1.8; monitoring (S6).

</details>

**Specific guardrails.** Four failure modes get a dedicated test:

| Failure mode | Test |
|---|---|
| Views mixed: raw and adjusted values in one series | A view label is mandatory; label consistency is checked at ingestion and at output. |
| Dividends mishandled: ignored, or confused with splits | Total-return continuity on the payment day, with a gap under 1 bp outside the parameterised fees and taxes. |
| Identifier changes not reconciled | Continuity of history after identity mapping (no unexplained gap), audited against the point-in-time universe. |
| Double adjustment, by the provider and in-house | A heuristic that spots factors already applied, and idempotence proven on reference sets. |

## Deliverables

- Policy specification: views, rules per field, visibility dates.
- Event dictionary: types, fields affected, order of application.
- Bitemporal schema and factor tables: model and integrity contracts.
- Assembly engine: computation and composition of factors, application functions, proven
  idempotence.
- Point-in-time query layer: view selection, [anti-leakage gates](../../glossary.md#anti-leakage-gate), labelling.
- Manifest registry and replay procedure: hashes, dependencies, re-execution.
- Test batteries and KPI dashboards: quality, leakage, latency, idempotence.
- Runbooks: diagnosing a jump, correcting a factor, a controlled [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).
- Decision log: choices, assumptions, impacts, versions.
