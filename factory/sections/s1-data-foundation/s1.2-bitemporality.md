# S1.2 — Temporal semantics & bitemporality

## TL;DR

Gives every fact two clocks: when it was true in the world, and when the factory learned it.
Every read must say "as of" when; a read without that date is refused, and replays are bit-identical.
Everything downstream reads time-varying data only through this as-of access.

## Purpose

Make the two clocks of every data item visible, mandatory and checkable: its
[valid time](../../glossary.md#valid-time), when the fact is true in the world, and its
[transaction time](../../glossary.md#transaction-time), when the factory actually knew it. This
is [bitemporality](../../glossary.md#bitemporality).

**Success looks like:**
- Every read, training run, [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest) or decision sees only the information available at the
  instant it asks for ([as of](../../glossary.md#as-of-view) that instant).
- [Replays](../../glossary.md#replay) are bit-for-bit identical.
- Zero [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage), and every correction is traceable.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The trading calendar brings the validated time-zone and daylight-saving rules; the source
specifications say where to read each source's valid time, and how it publishes and corrects.
The bitemporal manifests (schemas, constraints, vocabulary) are published with the store.
Downstream sections read through the as-of API, gated by [S1.8](s1.8-data-governance.md).

**Constraints.**
- One meaning per ID; no valid-time overlap for one ID; transaction time strictly increasing.
- A time granularity declared for every object; half-open intervals `[start, end)` everywhere;
  storage in UTC only, with time zones and daylight saving mapped at the edge, before entry.

**Service levels** (to define):
- T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) from valid time to transaction time for critical events (IPO, suspension,
  delisting), per data family.
- p95 latency of as-of reads, separately for "hot" and "cold" views.
- Availability of the as-of API, and an [error budget](../../glossary.md#error-budget) on
  integrity tests.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the bitemporal vocabulary is validated (step 1);
- the time-zone and daylight-saving policy is signed off;
- the sources are mapped: where to read valid time, publication cadence, corrections.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the [point-in-time access contract](../../glossary.md#point-in-time-access-contract) is live:
  100% of requests require an as-of date;
- the bitemporal tables are in production, versioned and never overwritten;
- as-of views conform on the test corpus, and bit-identical replays are demonstrated;
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) are live with thresholds and alerts, and the runbooks are operational.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the exit checks when:
- a provider or a schema changes;
- the lag from valid time to transaction time [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) beyond its thresholds;
- market or time-zone rules change (daylight saving, trading hours);
- a new class of events appears, such as complex [corporate actions](../../glossary.md#corporate-action);
- an incident affects the causality of the views.

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPIs, the alerts and the gaps between live and simulated views.
Lifecycle (S7) receives the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) cadence, the decision log and the changes of
thresholds.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Set the canonical bitemporal model</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a single language, as-of reads can be neither tested nor tooled.

**Actions.**
- Write a dictionary of concepts: a point event versus a state that lasts for a period.
- Define canonical table schemas with four bounds: valid-time start and end, transaction-time
  start and end, with +∞ by convention for an open end.
- Use sequenced keys per entity (ID, valid-time start, transaction-time start), with two
  constraints: no valid-time overlap for one ID, and transaction time strictly increasing.
- Set the time-zone, UTC and daylight-saving policy, and the inclusive / exclusive rule for
  bounds, for example `[start, end)`.

**KPI.** Share of objects mapped bitemporally; share of tables without valid-time overlap; share
of columns in UTC.

**Guardrail.** Integrity tests: no valid-time overlap per ID, transaction time strictly
increasing, normalised timestamps, `[start, end)` bounds.

**Output.** Bitemporal specification and integrity markers → steps 2 to 5, and S1.3 for replay.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Install the point-in-time access contract, fail-closed</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a turnstile, leakage starts with the first query.

**Actions.**
- Build a read interface, for queries and extractions, that requires an as-of instant.
- Allow no implicit default: a request without an as-of date is explicitly refused.
- Keep an access log: who asks for what, when, and as of which date.

**KPI.** 100% of requests carry an as-of date; the share of refused requests without one should
fall towards zero over time.

**Guardrail.** A [fail-closed](../../glossary.md#fail-closed) router, and tests that forbid any
fail-open path.

**Output.** The [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) contract and its access router → every downstream section (S2 to
S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Timestamp at the source: two clocks at ingestion</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** If ingestion does not stamp both clocks correctly, everything built on it is shaky.

**Actions.**
- Write, per type of data, the rules that extract valid time: published timestamp, effective
  date, bounds of a state.
- Take transaction time from the arrival clock: monotonic and append-only, with an
  [idempotency](../../glossary.md#idempotent) key per message so that a replay never duplicates.
- Set a lateness policy: how a late correction, with a later transaction time, attaches to a
  past valid time.

**KPI.** Median lag from valid time to transaction time; p95 lag; share of data later than its
threshold, set per data family.

**Guardrail.** Idempotent ingestion, rejection of non-monotonic transaction times, a queue with
[deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) ordering.

**Output.** Raw feeds stamped with both clocks → step 4 ([versioning](../../glossary.md#versioning)) and S1.3 (order and replay).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Version without overwriting: log and coalescing</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Bitemporality dies the moment anything is updated in place.

**Actions.**
- Keep an append-only log of versions: new rows, never a destructive update.
- Close intervals on replacement: set the transaction-time end and the valid-time end of the old
  version.
- Coalesce contiguous identical states, a reduction that loses nothing as of any date.
- Check that no ID has overlapping valid times and that transaction time strictly increases
  along each chain of versions.

**KPI.** Share of corrections captured as new versions; orphans (a version replaced without
being closed) = 0; coalescing rate.

**Guardrail.** Destructive updates are forbidden; integrity triggers make closing the old
version mandatory.

**Output.** Clean bitemporal tables → step 5 (as-of views), S1.6 (corporate actions), S1.7
(canonical views).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Build consistent as-of views</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** This is the interface that [features](../../glossary.md#feature), backtests and execution will consume.

**Actions.**
- Write the time-slice algorithm. For a view as of *T*, keep only the versions whose
  transaction-time start ≤ *T* < transaction-time end; then, for events, keep those whose valid
  time meets the business condition (for example valid time ≤ *T*), and for states, keep the
  intervals `[valid-time start, valid-time end)` that cover *T*.
- Guarantee a consistent [snapshot](../../glossary.md#snapshot): no consequence ever appears without its cause.
- Provide interval operators (coalescing, splitting) that deliver clean periods without
  off-by-one errors.

**KPI.** As-of accuracy on the test corpus of known answers = 100%; p95 time-slice latency under
its service level; share of causal snapshots (no effect without its cause).

**Guardrail.** A "consistent prefix" check: replaying the log up to *T* reproduces the view as of
*T*.

**Output.** A stable [as-of API](../../glossary.md#as-of-api) → features and [labels](../../glossary.md#label) (S2),
hypotheses (S3), execution simulation and cost analysis (S4), decisions (S5), and live versus
simulated monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Anti-leakage proofs and replay</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without automatic proof, the drift comes back.

**Actions.**
- Compare an extraction as of *T* with today's retro-corrected extraction: on data corrected
  since *T*, the two must differ; if they are identical, the corrections leaked into the past.
- Replay bit for bit: the same inputs give the same outputs, checked by hash.
- Place anti-leakage probes at the entry [gate](../../glossary.md#gate) of machine learning: strict separation in time, an
  [embargo](../../glossary.md#embargo) window consistent with the labelling horizon, sequential
  sampling.

**KPI.** Bit-identical replays at or above target; a controlled gap between as-of and
"today, corrected"; anti-leakage tests passed at 100%.

**Guardrail.** Sentinel datasets, embargo windows at least as long as the label horizon, strict
separation by transaction time.

**Output.** Temporal non-regression reports and test scenarios → monitoring (S6), governance (S7).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Observe both clocks and operate them</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Only what is observed can be protected.

**Actions.**
- Build a dashboard: lags from valid time to transaction time, bitemporal coverage, refused
  requests, time-slice latency.
- Write runbooks for extreme delays, mass corrections, daylight-saving switches and time-zone
  incidents.
- Set a retention and compaction policy that shortens history without breaking any as-of view.

**KPI.** Bitemporal coverage (both clocks present); p95 as-of latency;
[MTTR](../../glossary.md#mttd-mttr) of daylight-saving incidents; share of runbooks executed.

**Guardrail.** Real-time alerting and timestamped runbooks.

**Output.** Live KPIs and procedures → alerts in monitoring (S6), requalification rhythm in
lifecycle (S7).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Bitemporal specification: vocabulary, schemas, interval rules, time zones and daylight saving.
- Point-in-time access contract, fail-closed, with request logging.
- Append-only, idempotent ingestion pipelines carrying both clocks.
- Versioned tables with their integrity constraints.
- Time-slice engine and documented as-of API.
- Anti-leakage test battery and replay scenarios.
- KPI dashboard: lags, coverage, refused requests.
