# S7.6 — Model & strategy lifecycle (champion / challenger)

## TL;DR

Gives strategies a permit to operate, and withdraws it cleanly when needed: statuses, a disciplined path (paper, canary, capital tiers), rules of ramp-up and ramp-down, and triggers.
Allocate fast and small to what proves itself, withdraw fast what deviates, with no operational surprise and a traceable journal.
It takes each strategy from S4.10's promotion record and S5.1's entry ticket; its capital ladder goes to section 5, and its trigger-action catalogue to monitoring.

## Purpose

Give strategies a permit to operate, and withdraw it cleanly when needed. The page sets the
[gates](../../glossary.md#gate) of entry, a disciplined path
([paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading), then a canary, then
[capital tiers](../../glossary.md#capital-tier)), the rules of ramp-up and ramp-down, and the
triggers (promotion, freeze, [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback),
[sunset](../../glossary.md#sunset)). This is the factory's
[champion / challenger](../../glossary.md#champion-challenger) discipline, over the whole life of a
model or a strategy.

**Success looks like:** capital allocated fast and small to what proves itself, and withdrawn fast
from what deviates, with no operational surprise, and a traceable journal.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The strategy dossier of S3.9 brings the structured idea: the definition of the signal, its horizon
and its constraints. The promotion record of S4.10 brings the evidence before trading (the
simulator, the envelopes of cost) and the decision to enter paper trading, then capital, with its
ramp-up plan; the entry ticket of S5.1 the initial weight and bounds the strategy entered the
portfolio with. Sections 1 and 2 guarantee [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) data,
[lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage), versions and
[schema contracts](../../glossary.md#schema-contract) through the dossier and the record. The
monitoring signals bring the gaps between live and simulated (S6.1), the
[drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) (S6.4), the incidents of security (S6.6) and the requests of
[intervention](../../glossary.md#intervention) to retrain, roll back or withdraw (S6.7). The
steering note of S7.1 brings the promotions and demotions proposed, and the policy matrix of S7.4
the delays of challengers when budgets are tight.

The capital ladder carries the orders of capital: to S5.4, the bounds of each tier, which the
[risk budget book](../../glossary.md#risk-budget-book) applies per strategy; to S5.9, the stages and
tiers a strategy climbs, with its freezes, ramp-downs and kill; to capital steering (S7.8), the tier
each strategy holds, within which it allocates. The trigger-action catalogue tells monitoring (S6.1)
which thresholds and triggers to watch, and over which windows, and steering (S7.1) which actions
follow. The runbooks of ramp-up, ramp-down and kill go to S6.7; the decisions and the reports of
each gate passed go to the system of record (S7.2). A strategy that needs rework goes back to
section 3, or to section 4 for more tests: each return opens a mission card in the loop of S7.10.

**Constraints.**
- Formats: identifiers of strategy, versions of the artefacts, one reference for timestamps.
- Consistency: the chain from data to [features](../../glossary.md#feature), [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) and order, traceable end to end.

**Service levels:**
- Critical events of data or market (suspensions, a change of exchange), integrated into the
  triggers at T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) targets defined; an alert when exceeded.
- The thresholds and the rules (ramp-up, freeze, rollback) are updated after a decision within a
  documented maximum operational latency.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Before paper trading, the promotion record carries the full evidence: information before
trading, a clean [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest), consistent results of the simulator and cost analysis, no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage),
[capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) estimated, and a horizon compatible with the portfolio.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when the strategy is active and governed:
- it has passed its canary and its tiers by the rules;
- its triggers are connected, and its journal is in place;
- monitoring receives its thresholds;
- the capital ladder works: ramp-up and ramp-down run as written.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- drift is detected (in the signal, the costs, the behaviour);
- the market [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes, a schema contract changes, or new [labels](../../glossary.md#label) prove more relevant;
- incidents (quality, latency, execution) occur, or the capacity drifts.

The effects: retraining, recalibration, a freeze, a ramp-down, a sunset; or back to section 3 or 4
for rework. Monitoring (S6) receives revised thresholds and new triggers; lifecycle (S7) the
decisions journalled and the reports of each gate passed; section 4 requests for more tests and
simulations; section 3 the axes along which to simplify or strengthen the logic of alpha.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Set the rules of the game: champion and challenger</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without clear statuses and tiers, the rise and fall of capital cannot be orchestrated.

**Actions.**
- The statuses: idea, paper, canary, tier 1 to N, champion, [quarantine](../../glossary.md#quarantine), sunset.
- The rights and obligations of each status: maximum capital, frequency of decisions, metrics
  watched, [kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch).
- The capital tiers, and the maximum speeds of rise and fall.
- The budget of challengers: a ceiling of capital, and a maximum number of strategies on trial.
- The thresholds of a global freeze, when the system's budgets of reliability or of risk are eaten
  into.

**KPI.** Rate of tiers respected (% of promotions that comply); % of capital under challengers ≤ its
ceiling; median time of promotion and demotion.

**Guardrail.** Promotions freeze when the ceiling of challengers is exceeded, or while incidents of
reliability or risk are active.

**Output.** The charter of champion and challenger (statuses, tiers, budgets, rules) → step 2 (the
evidence required), step 3 (path and checkpoints).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Standardise the evidence required: information proven before trading</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The statuses and tiers dictate what a strategy must prove to enter and to progress.

**Actions.**
- A template of evidence: information before trading (the quality of the signal), [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) (out
  of sample), capacity and impact, [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) and costs, the consistency of the labels, no leakage and
  no hindsight, compatibility with the portfolio.
- Minimum criteria per target status: entry into paper, the passage to canary, and so on.
- Checklists: point-in-time data, a consistent replication in the simulator, the expected cost
  analysis, risks and limits.

**KPI.** Rate of completeness of the evidence, rate of acceptance at the gate of entry, number of
gaps detected (leakage, inconsistent labels).

**Guardrail.** Refused automatically when the evidence before trading is insufficient, or when the
data are inconsistent.

**Output.** The template of evidence per status, and a grid of scoring → step 3 (path and
checkpoints), for the evaluations.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Set the disciplined path and its checkpoints: paper, canary, tiers</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With what to demand (step 2) and how to allocate (step 1) known, freeze the process.

**Actions.**
- The standard sequence: research validated → a clean backtest → the simulator and cost analysis →
  paper (no capital) → canary (tiny) → capital tiers → champion.
- Checkpoints at each stage: the metrics expected, minimum windows, rules to stop (stop, rollback),
  minimum delays between promotions.
- The canary: its minimum size and duration, the events observed (latency, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage), the gap
  between real and simulated).

**KPI.** The gap between simulator and backtest (performance, costs), the duration of paper, the %
of checkpoints passed at the first attempt.

**Guardrail.** No promotion when the simulated gap leaves its envelope, or when the minimum duration
is not respected.

**Output.** The path and its checkpoints: a diagram, and the criteria per stage → step 4 (ramp-up and
ramp-down in practice), step 5 (triggers).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Operate the ramp-up, the ramp-down and the kill switch</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** With the path defined, apply the rules of rise and fall of capital. Setting the bounds
per tier and the rules of promotion, ramp-down, kill and cooling is design; operating them is
execution.

**Actions.**
- The scale of capital per tier (its bounds), and the rules of promotion: stability net of costs,
  gap from what was expected.
- The rules of ramp-down (a measured degradation) and of the kill switch (a strong deviation, an
  incident, costs out of their envelope).
- Windows of cooling after a rollback or a kill, and the conditions of re-entry.

**KPI.** The gap between real and simulated per tier, the [information ratio](../../glossary.md#information-ratio) and [Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#sharpe-ratio) net
per tier, slippage against its envelope, the time of rollback.

**Guardrail.** Kill switch on a strong drift: a gap beyond the band defined, costs out of their
envelope, incidents. A window of cooling is mandatory after a rollback.

**Output.** The capital ladder (promotions, demotions, rollback) → section 5 (decision and
execution) to apply it, monitoring (S6) to watch it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Define the triggers and the requalifications: drift, regimes, costs, labels</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The possible actions are ready (step 4); now plug in the triggers.

**Actions.**
- Families of triggers: drift of the signal or of the behaviour, a change of regime, labels or
  targets gone stale, costs or turnover exploding, capacity exceeded, incidents of data quality.
- Attached actions: retraining, recalibration, freeze or pause, ramp-down, sunset.
- The maximum latency from signal to action, and the trails of audit (who, what, when, why).

**KPI.** Latency from signal to action; the rate of useful [requalifications](../../glossary.md#requalification) (few that prove false
again); the number of freezes and ramp-downs triggered.

**Guardrail.** A double confirmation, or a window of persistence, against noisy triggers; the
triggers of risk and costs take priority automatically.

**Output.** The trigger-action catalogue (a table of decision) → monitoring (S6), to feed it;
lifecycle (S7), for its rhythm of governance.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Journal, rituals of review, and closure: a living traceability</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Make visible everything that was decided and done, and close the loop.

**Actions.**
- A decision log tied to the artefacts: data → features → model → orders.
- Rituals: weekly or fortnightly points on promotions and withdrawals, and reviews after an incident
  or a rollback.
- A dashboard: gates passed, early mortality, the lifetime of champions, the cost of
  opportunity.

**KPI.** Decisions journalled = 100%; the delay to publish the minutes; early mortality (before tier
2); the median lifetime of champions; the cost of opportunity (paper trading too long).

**Guardrail.** No promotion without an entry in the journal; a postmortem is mandatory after a major
kill or rollback.

**Output.** The operational journal, the cadence of reviews and the dashboard → every section, as a
record and for audit.

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: the charter of champion and challenger (statuses, tiers, budgets, rules).
- The template of evidence: filled for each strategy, and scored.
- The path and its checkpoints: document, criteria, minimum durations.
- The capital ladder: ramp-up, ramp-down, kill, windows of cooling.
- The trigger-action catalogue: a table of decision, with maximum latencies.
- The decision log (who, what, when, why), tied to the versioned artefacts.
- [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) dashboard: gates passed, gaps between real and simulated, early mortality, the life of
  champions, latency from signal to action, cost of opportunity.
- Replacement and sunset process: quarantine, final withdrawal, criteria of re-entry.
