# S7.2 — Traceability, audit & explainability: the system of record for decisions

## TL;DR

Makes the factory traceable and replayable: at any moment, who decided what, when, why, with which data and model, and with what impact down to the fill.
One complete, verifiable chain from data to features, signals, decision, order and execution; a readable reason per trade; a deterministic replay that matches reality, its gaps explained.
Every page writes its decision log here; the record gives back the canonical log, the explanation of each trade, and missions when a gap stays unexplained.

## Purpose

Turn the factory into a traceable, replayable machine. At any moment, the factory can answer who
decided what, when, why, with which data and which model, and with what impact down to the fill;
then [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) the scene exactly. This is the factory's system of record for decisions.

**Success looks like:** a complete, verifiable chain from data to [features](../../glossary.md#feature), signals, portfolio
decision, order and execution; a readable reason for every trade; and a [deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) replay that
matches reality, with the remaining gaps explained.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

Every sub-section of the factory writes its decisions here: its decision log is the entry of the
record. Section 1 brings the versioned datasets with their manifests, tests and reports through its
evidence locker (S1.8), and the as-of [snapshots](../../glossary.md#snapshot) through its replay API. The parameter registry of
S2.1 brings the thresholds of sampling and their changes; the feature registry of S2.10 the
features and [labels](../../glossary.md#label), [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time), with their [fingerprints](../../glossary.md#fingerprint) and the mapping from each training set
to its live period. The promotion record of S4.10 brings section 4's evidence before trading:
[robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness), simulated costs, scenarios. The decision logs of section 3 carry its hypotheses, its
rules of decision and the versions of its logic of [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha). The execution log of S5.7 brings the
orders, executions, costs and rejections; the audit packs of S6.3 the proofs of monitoring, with
its alerts, [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) and gaps between simulated and real.

The canonical decision log goes back to steering (S7.1), which reviews whether its decisions
landed, and to [incident response](../../glossary.md#incident-response) (S7.9), as material for postmortems. The explanation sheets answer
"why this trade?" for steering (S7.1), for [intervention](../../glossary.md#intervention) (S6.7) and for the audit packs of S6.3. An
unexplained gap beyond its threshold opens a mission card in the loop of S7.10. The runbooks (four
eyes, [kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch), [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback), reversible releases) go to S6.7. Monitoring (S6.1) receives, through
the KPI feed, the explained gaps, the alerts of missing [lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage) and the real times of explanation.

**Constraints.**
- Unique IDs, [immutability](../../glossary.md#immutability), UTC timestamps, and a clear convention for "as-of".
- [Idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent) writes; the critical snapshots referenced by their fingerprint.
- Compatible evolution of schemas: additions that break nothing, deprecation at a set cadence,
  windows of migration.

**Service levels:**
- Critical events (a decision, an order, a fill) reach the journal at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95)
  ≤ 60 s, at T99 ≤ 5 min.
- The explanation is available at T95 ≤ 2 min after the fill.
- The lineage is complete, with its snapshots, at T95 ≤ 15 min after the fill.
- An alert on a repeated breach (more than 3 times in 24 h), or when coverage falls below 99%.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the contract of truth is validated (step 1), and its tests of unique IDs and of immutability pass;
- sections 1 and 2 deliver point-in-time data and features, with their fingerprints;
- the points of emission of the journal are identified for each section (3 to 6).

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of steps 2 to 5 are at target for at least 4 weeks;
- the replays cover ≥ 95% of the representative cases, with the gap explained ≥ 90%;
- the safeguards are tested: exercises of kill switch and rollback passed.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the KPIs drift: coverage, latency, [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility);
- the schema of data or features changes, market rules change (suspensions, microstructure), or a
  new method of decision or risk arrives;
- incidents or unexplained gaps stay above their threshold for 2 cadences.

Monitoring (S6) receives the explained gaps, the alerts of missing lineage and the real times of
explanation. Lifecycle (S7) receives the decisions of arbitration (freeze, ramp-down, priorities of
missions), and material for its postmortems and its [steering cadences](../../glossary.md#steering-cadence).

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · The contract of truth: identities, time, immutability</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a frame of identities and timestamps, nothing that follows is reliable. The
contract builds on section 1's identities and times (S1.1 to S1.3); the
[data lifecycle](../../glossary.md#data-lifecycle) (S7.7) restates it for the facts of data.

**Actions.**
- Set the dictionary of canonical identities: dataset, feature set, [label set](../../glossary.md#label-set), strategy, model,
  parameter set, portfolio decision, order, execution, run, change request, person and role.
- Define the reference times and the meaning of "as-of": the [valid time](../../glossary.md#valid-time) of the event (what happens
  on the market), the decision time (when the computation runs), the knowledge time (when the
  information became known) and the [transaction time](../../glossary.md#transaction-time) (when it is recorded); one time base, and a
  rule for time zones.
- Set the invariants: append-only, immutable records; timestamped [reference data](../../glossary.md#reference-data); idempotent
  writes; the critical snapshots (data, features, models) referenced by their fingerprint.
- Define the roles, responsibilities and consents: who may decide what, and four eyes for the
  sensitive changes.

**KPI.** Collisions of identities = 0; mandatory fields missing < 0.1% at T95.

**Guardrail.** Tests of unique IDs and of immutability; a write is refused when its knowledge time
comes before its valid time in a way that is not consistent.

**Output.** The specification of the contract of truth (identities, time, invariants), the
dictionary of fields, the rules of unique IDs and of immutability → step 2 (the journal), the whole
chain (sections 1 to 6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · The append-only operational journal: events and versions</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** The journal applies the contract of truth; without robust identities and times, the trail
limps.

**Actions.**
- List the events to journal: the creation or change of an artefact (data, features, labels,
  models, rules of risk), portfolio decisions, orders, fills, exceptions and incidents,
  postmortems, approvals.
- Define the envelope of each entry: the identities linked, the timestamps, the fingerprints and
  snapshots referenced, and the minimal sufficient context (metrics seen, hypothesis, constraints,
  risks accepted).
- Connect the points of emission: research (strategy lab), validation and simulation, decision and
  portfolio, execution, monitoring, governance.
- The golden rule: no fix outside the journal; every change goes through a durable entry.

**KPI.** Coverage of the events ≥ 99.9%; latency of writing, from a decision, an order or a fill to
the journal, at T95 ≤ 60 s.

**Guardrail.** A daily reconciliation of the volumes of the journal against the source systems; an
alert when the gap exceeds 0.1%.

**Output.** The journal of decisions and artefacts, in operation and fed by every section → step 3
(lineage), monitoring and lifecycle.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · End-to-end lineage: a navigable graph, upstream and downstream</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Link the entries of the journal into one coherent causal chain.

**Actions.**
- Define the edges of lineage: raw data → features → labels → signals → portfolio decision → order
  → execution, and the returns (execution → real costs → attribution).
- For each edge, state its proof: the parent's ID, the fingerprint or snapshot, the rule or model
  evaluated, the version of the parameters.
- Expose ready-made paths: (a) from a fill back to the raw data; (b) from a raw datum to the trades
  it influenced.
- Detect the missing links (paths that cannot join), and impose their correction.

**KPI.** Orders with a complete chain ≥ 99.5%; the reverse path, from a datum to its trades, ≥ 98%.

**Guardrail.** A job that detects orphan links; a promotion is blocked when more than 0.5% are
missing.

**Output.** The graph of lineage, which can be queried (an API, queries), covering every order and
fill → step 4 ([explainability](../../glossary.md#explainability)), step 5 (replay), monitoring.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Unified explainability, per trade and per decision</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The explanation rests on the lineage and on the "as-of" context.

**Actions.**
- Define the standard explanation sheet: a readable summary in 1–3 lines (for example "signal X
  above its threshold, cost acceptable, risk available"), then the details: the values seen at time
  T, the active constraints, the contributions of the factors (for a learnt model, its contributions
  or importances at the moment of the decision), the rules passed or failed, and the alternatives
  considered when relevant.
- Forbid cosmetic explanations: the explanation must replay the real reasoning, not tell a story
  afterwards.
- Sample regularly by hand: understandable by a reasonable reader, and in agreement with the real
  trigger.

**KPI.** Orders with a readable reason ≥ 99%; delay to answer "why this trade?" at T95 ≤ 2 min.

**Guardrail.** A weekly review of samples; the discrepancy between explanation and real trigger
stays < 1%, otherwise the related releases freeze.

**Output.** Readable reasons for every trade and decision, available at once → steering cadences
(S7.1), monitoring (S6), compliance and audit.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Deterministic replay, and the measure of the explained gap</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the lineage and the explanation in place, the factory can replay and quantify.

**Actions.**
- Define the protocol of replay: rebuild the "as-of" environment (snapshots of data and features,
  the version of the model and its parameters, the rules of risk, simulated latencies), run the
  decision or the order at the right grain of time, and record the outputs.
- Compare the replay with reality, and classify the gaps: latency, costs, rejections,
  microstructure, drift of data, non-determinism, errors of integration.
- Store the reports of gaps as artefacts of the journal; open missions to S6 and S7 when the
  unexplained gap exceeds a threshold.

**KPI.** Reproducibility (identical outputs, within a defined tolerance) ≥ 99%; the share of the gap
explained ≥ 90%.

**Guardrail.** Tests of determinism ([seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), order of iteration, floating point); an alert when the
time zone or the "as-of" is inconsistent.

**Output.** The engine of replay and the reports of gaps, with their causes attributed → validation
and simulation (section 4, as missions through the loop of S7.10), monitoring (S6), lifecycle (S7).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Controls of change and safeguards: four eyes, kill switch, rollback</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Lock operations down, with the proofs and tests already in place.

**Actions.**
- Formalise the [gates](../../glossary.md#gate) of change: four eyes for rules, risks and models; "replay passed" and
  "explanation passed" checked before production; complete journals mandatory.
- The kill switch and the rollback tested: regular training scenarios ("game days"), written and
  verified procedures.
- Release strategies that can be reversed (progressive), and a targeted freeze when the budgets of
  reliability or of risk are eaten into.

**KPI.** Changes not traced = 0; rollback at T95 ≤ 5 min; the time to repair incidents falling,
over a rolling window.

**Guardrail.** A monthly exercise of the kill switch; merges forbidden automatically when the checks
of step 5 fail.

**Output.** Operational safeguards active, with runbooks and alarms → decision and execution (section
5), lifecycle (S7), compliance.

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of the contract of truth: identities, time, invariants, schemas.
- The journal of decisions and artefacts, in operation (append-only, idempotent), with its points
  of emission connected.
- The graph of lineage, to query upstream and downstream, with a coverage ≥ 99.5%.
- Standard explanation sheets, available for 100% of the orders.
- The engine of replay, and the reports of explained gaps, built into the cadences.
- Runbooks: four eyes, kill switch, rollback, release strategies that can be reversed.
- KPI tracking table (real time and weekly), with thresholds and alerts.
- Log of choices and decisions (reasoning, arbitrations, exceptions), linked to the artefacts.
