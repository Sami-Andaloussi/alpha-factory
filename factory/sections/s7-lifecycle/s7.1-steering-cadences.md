# S7.1 — Steering cadences & directional vision

## TL;DR

A short, regular ritual, weekly or fortnightly, that rules on the reliability of the system, reads the health of alpha and research, and adjusts the course: budgets and priorities.
Each review ends with 3–5 written decisions, assigned and dated, which become missions; success is decisions that land on time, and a short delay from observation to action.
Monitoring compiles its pre-read (S6.1); its steering note sets the budgets and the release cadence that section 5 and lifecycle apply.

## Purpose

Set up a short, regular ritual (weekly or fortnightly) that rules on the reliability of the system
(can the factory accelerate?), reads the health of [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) and research (what works, what weakens,
and why), adjusts the strategic course (the budgets of reliability, risk and capital, the
priorities), and ends with 3–5 written decisions, assigned and dated, which become living
missions for the sections concerned. This is the factory's [steering cadence](../../glossary.md#steering-cadence).

**Success looks like:** decisions that land at the planned rhythm, a short delay from observation
to action, and a visible consistency between what the machine reports and what the factory does
next.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

Monitoring compiles the pre-read (S6.1) from the KPI feeds of every section: from monitoring itself,
the [SLOs](../../glossary.md#slo) and [SLIs](../../glossary.md#sli), the alerts, live against
simulated, the [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift), the costs and impact; from data (section 1),
freshness and consistency, the status of backfills and of change data capture, the incidents of
providers; from [features](../../glossary.md#feature) and [labels](../../glossary.md#label) (section
2), versions, drifts, recomputes and weights; from the lab and validation (sections 3 and 4), the
signals, the results before and after the fact, the cost analysis; from decision and execution
(section 5), the real fills, costs and impact, and the latencies of execution. The decision log of
S7.2 gives back the decisions of past reviews, and whether they landed; its explanation sheets say
why a trade was made, when a review asks. The trigger-action catalogue of S7.6 says which action
follows each trigger; the reviews govern it.

The steering note carries the course: to S5.4, the budgets that section 5 applies within the day;
to S7.4, the budgets of reliability and risk; to S7.5, the release cadence and the freezes; to
S7.6, the promotions and demotions proposed; to S7.8, the allocation of capital. Each written
decision opens a mission card in the loop of S7.10, which routes it to the section that does the
work (data, features, validation and simulation, execution, infrastructure). The decisions go to
the system of record (S7.2).

**Constraints and service levels:**
- The pre-read is delivered 12 hours before the review (SLO 98%).
- Integration of critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting): ingested at T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95)
  ≤ X min; an alert when exceeded.
- The end-of-day computation of live against simulated takes 15 min at most after the close; when
  it is unavailable, the status is "uncertainty".
- A mission opens ≤ 24 h after a gap is detected (SLO 95%).

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the charter of cadence is validated (format, roles, [gates](../../glossary.md#gate));
- the thresholds of the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) are defined;
- the pipeline of the pre-read works;
- the decision log is in place.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when the cadence is alive for 4 consecutive weeks:
- the sessions are held, and the pre-reads are on time;
- ≥ 85% of the decisions land;
- a freeze is applied whenever reliability is red;
- the drifts between live and simulated stay below their thresholds of alert.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the KPIs drift for long: landing < 70%, lead time rising, drifts between live and simulated
  rising;
- new market rules, or changes of microstructure, affect costs or latencies;
- a promising method or strategy needs a different course ([capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity), [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity));
- a major incident occurs: its postmortem leads to actions, and to a revision of the gates and
  thresholds.

Monitoring (S6) receives new signals of alert, for example a specific gap between live and
simulated, or a budget of cost. Lifecycle (S7) updates its [fitness functions](../../glossary.md#fitness-function) of reliability, risk
and cost, the release cadence, and its rules of freeze and thaw.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · A condensed pre-read, 12 hours before (3 screens at most)</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Align attention before entering: no discovery in session.

**Actions.**
- Aggregate 3 panels:
  1. reliability, SLOs and SLIs: fresh data, jobs, latencies, [error budget](../../glossary.md#error-budget) consumed;
  2. alpha and research: P&L net of costs, signals rising or falling, live against simulated,
     costs and [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover);
  3. the strategic course: exposures, capacity and impact, aggregated risk against its bounds,
     debts to repay.
- A simple colour code (red, amber, green), and one line per finding.

**KPI.** Pre-read delivered 12 hours before ≥ 98%; length ≤ 3 screens.

**Guardrail.** When a critical figure is missing: an "uncertainty" label, and a gate of prudence
at the start of act 1.

**Output.** The timestamped pre-read, which monitoring compiles and lifecycle distributes →
everyone.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Act 1: technical health, go or no-go</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Decide whether to accelerate or to repair.

**Actions.**
- Review the SLOs and SLIs: freshness of data, latency end to end, rate of failed jobs, quality of
  the feeds, red alerts.
- Rule, do not debug: "clear to accelerate", or "a targeted freeze until back to green".

**KPI.** Error budget consumed over a rolling period; freezes respected when the budget is
exceeded = 100%.

**Guardrail.** A hard rule: when reliability is red, novelties freeze; only fixes of reliability
pass, with acceptance criteria.

**Output.** The status of reliability → freezes or deployments in [change management](../../glossary.md#change-management) (S7.5), and
missions opened for data and infrastructure.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Act 2: the health of alpha and research, where information wins or loses</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Tell signal from noise, and link performance to costs and frictions.

**Actions.**
- A table "works, weakens, to freeze" per family of ideas, explained by its drivers: [regime](../../glossary.md#regime),
  costs, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage), crowding, drift of labels.
- Live against simulated: drifts of performance, real costs, the gap of execution.
- Propose promotions and demotions ([champion](../../glossary.md#champion-challenger) and challenger), and targeted experiments.

**KPI.** [Information ratio](../../glossary.md#information-ratio) net of costs per idea, rolling; cost and turnover against their bounds;
the gap between live and simulated, for alpha and for costs.

**Guardrail.** No promotion when size or impact exceeds capacity, or when the gap between live and
simulated exceeds its threshold of alert 2 sessions in a row.

**Output.** A short list of actions on alpha (strategy lab, validation, simulator), and missions on
cost analysis and execution.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Act 3: the strategic course and the budgets (reliability, risk, capital)</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Say where to put the speed, and at what risk.

**Actions.**
- Arbitrate the budgets:
  - reliability: the effort, or a freeze until back under the threshold;
  - risk: [tracking error](../../glossary.md#tracking-error), [expected shortfall](../../glossary.md#expected-shortfall) and stress against their bounds; [rebalancing](../../glossary.md#rebalancing), speeds;
  - capital: allocation and deallocation per idea, by [information coefficient](../../glossary.md#information-coefficient), information ratio,
    [breadth](../../glossary.md#breadth) and capacity net of costs.
- Set the priorities for 7–14 days, the release cadence that goes with them, and demotions when a
  debt is too high.

**KPI.** Use of the bounds of risk (median and p95); the % of capital on the top ideas against its
target; the gap of exposure from the plan.

**Guardrail.** No acceleration when two budgets out of three (reliability, risk, capital) are under
constraint.

**Output.** The steering note → section 5 implements it within the day; lifecycle sets its cadence
across the weeks.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Written decisions become living missions, with follow-up</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn findings into traced commitments, and make them land.

**Actions.**
- Write 3–5 decisions at most, each in one sentence: what, why, who, when, with measurable
  acceptance criteria.
- Open missions (generic tickets) linked to the decision log; set a limit of work in progress; review
  the landing at the start of the next session.

**KPI.** Rate of landing by the due date ≥ 85%; median delay from observation to mission ≤ 24 h;
the limit of work in progress respected.

**Guardrail.** When landing falls below 70% two cycles in a row: a short retrospective, a lower
limit of work in progress and smaller scopes; nothing is added while that limit is reached.

**Output.** The decision log (lifecycle's system of record) and the queue of missions → the
target sections.

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: the charter of cadence (agenda, roles, gates, limit of work in progress,
  rules of freeze).
- Log of choices and decisions: one source of truth, timestamped, chained to the missions and their
  acceptance criteria.
- Modules and documentation ready to test: the template of the pre-read (3 panels), the
  definitions of the metrics (SLOs and SLIs, net information ratio, live against simulated, cost
  and turnover, tracking error and expected shortfall).
- KPI tracking table: landing, lead time, budgets, drift between live and simulated, limit of work
  in progress respected.
- Replacement process: a substitute per role, rules when the quorum breaks, continuity during an
  incident.
