# S5.5 — Regime adaptation (sequential control)

## TL;DR

Steers the portfolio actively and cost-consciously: it scales the exposures with the market context (volatility, dispersion, liquidity, stress), without over-reacting and never beyond the budgets.
Every period it publishes adjusted targets with a short explanation; an adjustment is made only when its benefit in risk beats its cost, and the policy falls back to neutral when in doubt.
Its regime adjustment goes to the transition planner (S5.6), and to portfolio construction (S5.3) as the regime's parameters for the next cycle.

## Purpose

Give the portfolio an active, cost-conscious way of driving: scale and orient the exposures by the
market context (volatility, dispersion, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity), stress), without
over-reacting and without breaking the budgets. This is [sequential control](../../glossary.md#sequential-control)
applied to [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime).

**Success looks like:** every day or period, adjusted targets (weights, scalers, limits) with a
short explanation, that improve the pair of return and risk net of fees, and reduce the
[drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown) in hostile regimes: the
[regime adjustment](../../glossary.md#regime-adjustment).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The target portfolio of S5.3 brings the weights of a "clean world", with their sensitivities, to
see which constraints bind; the risk budget book of S5.4 the living budgets; the interaction map
of S5.2 which families of [alphas](../../glossary.md#alpha) break together under stress; the cost surface of S5.8 the costs
before trading, the cost analysis after it, and the diagnostics of impact and adverse selection.
Sections 1 and 2 bring the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) data and the calendars
of events (through the as-of API) and the registered [features](../../glossary.md#feature), among them the dated regimes of
S2.6's [regime encoding](../../glossary.md#regime-encoding). Monitoring (S6) sends its live alerts
of [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) and its comparisons of live against simulated.

The regime adjustment carries the adjusted targets, their confidence, the explanation, the expected
cost and the bands of inaction to S5.6 and S5.7; the multipliers of the regime to S5.3, for its next
cycle, and to S5.4, with recommendations to adjust the budgets when a change is structural; its
journal (regime, scalers, trigger, costs expected and realised) to S5.8. S5.3 and S5.5 read each
other: the loop is closed by the cycle. S5.9 releases each new version of the matrix of allocation.
Drift detection (S6.4) reads the regime in force: it is monitoring's one source of regime. The
runbook of [fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback) to the base policy goes to S6.7.

**Constraints.**
- Never beyond the budgets of S5.4: when they conflict, the budgets win.
- Neutral by default when the radar of context is unavailable.
- Traceable: every adjustment carries a short justification and a timestamp.

**Service levels** (examples, to calibrate):
- Daily: the regime and the adjusted targets at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 15 min after the
  close, or at a set batch.
- Intraday: T90 ≤ 5 min and T95 ≤ 15 min after a validated trigger.
- Critical events (suspensions, halts, an illiquid regime): T95 ≤ 1 [bar](../../glossary.md#bar) of the granularity of
  decision.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the target weights of S5.3 are available, and the budgets of S5.4 active;
- the sources of the radar are validated point-in-time;
- a reference level of cost analysis exists.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass*, ready for production, when all of these hold:
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of step 6 beat their thresholds against the base policy, over a representative
  [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) period;
- zero violations of budget in [replay](../../glossary.md#replay);
- the journal and the KPI tables are operational;
- the neutral mode is tested.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the distributions of the radar drift, or the rate of switches between regimes rises abnormally;
- cost beats benefit over N periods (the action yield below its threshold);
- the microstructure changes (market rules, structural liquidity), new families of alphas arrive,
  or the budgets of S5.4 are revised;
- after an incident, when a [kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch) fired: a full review.

Monitoring (S6) receives the regimes, for the attribution of returns between alpha, market and
costs, and alerts when stability leaves its bounds. Lifecycle (S7) receives the criteria for
promoting or withdrawing a version of the matrix, and a planned cadence of minimum reviews.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Charter of regimes and its requirements</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Before measuring or classifying, name what is sought, and forbid any feedback from the
factory's own decisions.

**Actions.**
- Write a short [regime taxonomy](../../glossary.md#regime-taxonomy) (3–6 regimes at most), each
  with its operational intent: "fluid directional", "costly chop", "stress and thin liquidity".
- State the non-negotiable principles: point-in-time, signals exogenous to the decisions, no
  [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage), no self-reference.
- Set the doctrine of persistence (minimum [hysteresis](../../glossary.md#hysteresis), a target
  median duration), the bands of inaction, and the threshold of cost against benefit required to
  act.

**KPI.** Clarity of the taxonomy (≤ 6 states); rate of ambiguity, the time spent in a grey zone,
< X%.

**Guardrail.** A no-leak audit: tests of revision and latency on every source, and a ban on
features derived from the decisions.

**Output.** Charter of regimes (definitions, drivers observed, rules of persistence and inaction,
criteria of exogeneity) → steps 2, 3 and 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Radar of context: exogenous measures, normalised</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** A classification is only as good as its instruments. Building the panel is execution;
choosing and normalising its measures is design.

**Actions.**
- Build a minimal, robust panel: realised and intraday volatility, cross-sectional dispersion,
  liquidity ([spreads](../../glossary.md#spread), depth, average daily volume), macro and event stress, the recent quality of
  execution, the stability of correlations.
- Normalise into comparable units; document the latency and the revisions of each series; flag
  every imputed value.
- Produce a point-in-time picture per period: timestamped, signed, replayable.

**KPI.** Completeness ≥ 99%; drift of the distributions watched; stability of the ranks of the key
measures.

**Guardrail.** A test of exogeneity (no dependence on the decisions); a
[latency budget](../../glossary.md#latency-budget): every measure within its service level.

**Output.** The radar stream: a vector of normalised measures and their metadata (latency,
reliability) → step 3, step 6 (monitoring and regret).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Classify the regime, with persistence and confidence</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a stable regime state, there is no policy to execute.

**Actions.**
- Turn the radar into a regime state and its confidence, by explicit rules or explained latent
  states; impose hysteresis and a minimum duration.
- Calibrate how often the regime is classified again (daily, every 4 h, other), without exceeding
  the threshold of cost against benefit set in step 1.
- Keep a short journal of explanation: the top indicators and the thresholds crossed.

**KPI.** Switches per day below their cap; median duration of a regime within the target range;
stability beyond noise: switches reversed < Y%.

**Guardrail.** Bands of inaction on the state (no switch while the confidence stays inside the
band); out-of-sample tests of stability, with shifts in time.

**Output.** The streams of regime, confidence and justification over time → step 4 (matrix of
allocation), step 5 (cadence of adjustment).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Matrix of allocation by regime: scalers and dedicated limits</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** A regime is worth something only if it changes the action, traceably.

**Actions.**
- Build a matrix of regime × family of alphas: scalers of exposure (up or down), priorities of
  rotation (what to overweight, what to put on standby), dedicated limits (participation,
  concentration, maximum costs).
- Define the rules of smooth transition: a ramp, a [half-life](../../glossary.md#half-life), a
  maximum step of change per period.
- Guarantee strict compatibility with the budgets of S5.4, never exceeded; write a neutral policy,
  the return to neutral when the radar is unavailable.

**KPI.** Coverage: 100% of the families mapped; the expected cost of a typical switch against the
risk it removes; conflicts resolved, with zero violations of budget in simulation.

**Guardrail.** Worst-case simulations (a thin market); caps per channel and latency; the neutral
mode when in doubt.

**Output.** Versioned matrix of allocation (scalers, rules of rotation, limits and neutrality) →
step 5, S5.3 (diagnostics when a constraint binds), S5.4 (proposed adjustments of budget).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Cadence of adjustment and the test of cost against benefit</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Even a good matrix, badly paced, pays useless fees. Setting the cadence and the test is
design; issuing the adjusted targets is execution.

**Actions.**
- Set a cadence of evaluation (daily, intraday, or both) and its triggers: thresholds of
  confidence, a band crossed, an alert of liquidity.
- Test cost against benefit before acting: the benefit in risk (value at risk or
  [expected shortfall](../../glossary.md#expected-shortfall) reduced, or the marginal utility)
  against the cost of the adjustment (before trading, plus the penalties of [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover)).
- Issue the adjusted targets (weights, scalers, limits), with a note of explanation (indicators to
  action) and an estimate of cost.

**KPI.** Action yield, the realised benefit in risk divided by the realised cost, ≥ its threshold;
the rate of actions refused (useful inaction); the gap between expected and realised cost.

**Guardrail.** A [rate limiter](../../glossary.md#rate-limiter) (a maximum number of adjustments
per period); a maximum step per asset and family; a kill switch of adaptation when the action
yield stays below plan over N periods.

**Output.** Adjusted targets, their justification and their expected cost, within the
[no-trade zones](../../glossary.md#no-trade-zone) → S5.6 (transition planner), S5.7 (execution), S5.8 (cost analysis, expected against
realised).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Validate out of sample, go live, and close the loops</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Make sure the adaptation pays, net, and keeps learning. The comparison is design; the
production and its journal are execution.

**Actions.**
- Compare the adaptive policy with the base policy under the same budgets, gross of fees then net;
  include the regret against a clairvoyant oracle, as a theoretical yardstick.
- Go live with strict logging: regime, confidence, scalers, costs expected and realised, a short
  justification.
- Close the loops: to S5.8 (gaps between expected and realised costs recalibrate costs and
  capacities), to monitoring (drifts raise alerts), to S5.4 (proposed budgets, when a change is
  structural).

**KPI.** Net [Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#sharpe-ratio) up against the base; maximum drawdown
down in hostile regimes; the turnover due to adaptation contained; regret falling; quality of
explanation, 100% of the actions justified.

**Guardrail.** A [canary release](../../glossary.md#canary-release) and a
[progressive rollout](../../glossary.md#progressive-rollout); an automatic
[fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback) to the base policy when the thresholds of under-performance
or cost are crossed.

**Output.** Net out-of-sample report, validated parameters, the journal in production and the KPI
dashboard → governance (S5.9), lifecycle (S7, promotions and withdrawals).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of the charter of regimes: definitions, exogeneity, persistence, inaction.
- The matrix of allocation, regime × family of alphas (scalers, rotations, limits, neutral mode),
  versioned.
- The radar stream, documented (schema of data, latencies, revisions), with replayable pictures.
- The journal of adaptation, in a clear format: date and time, regime, confidence, action, costs
  expected and realised, a short justification.
- KPI dashboard for steps 1 to 6, with thresholds and alerts.
- The procedure of fallback and replacement: an instant switch to the base policy, and the
  criteria of reactivation.
- Out-of-sample report: base against adaptive, net of costs, regret against the oracle, worst-case
  tests.
