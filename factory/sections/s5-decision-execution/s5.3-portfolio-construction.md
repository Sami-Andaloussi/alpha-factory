# S5.3 — Portfolio construction engine (convex & stochastic)

## TL;DR

Turns validated alphas into executable target weights that maximise net value, with risk and frictions under control, every weight explained by its sensitivities, and no needless round trips.
At every cycle the portfolio is feasible within the budgets of S5.4, robust to uncertain inputs, and solved in under 10% of the rebalancing interval (p95).
The target portfolio goes to the transition planner (S5.6) and the execution chain (S5.7), and its sensitivities to execution analytics (S5.8), which closes the loop.

## Purpose

Turn the validated [alphas](../../glossary.md#alpha) into executable target weights that maximise net value, with risk and
frictions under control. This is the factory's [portfolio construction](../../glossary.md#portfolio-construction):
a [convex](../../glossary.md#convex-optimisation) programme whose uncertain inputs are handled by
[stochastic optimisation](../../glossary.md#stochastic-optimisation), traceable through its
sensitivities, and stable: useful [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) only, no needless round
trips.

**Success looks like:** at every cycle, a portfolio that is feasible, consistent with the budgets
of S5.4, robust to uncertainty, and ready to be moved by S5.6 and executed by S5.7, while the loop
of learning with S5.8 stays closed.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The entry tickets of S5.1 bring the prudent alphas and their horizons; the interaction map of S5.2
the interactions, the [drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown) that coincide and the interaction rules; the risk budget book of
S5.4 the budgets (risk, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity), leverage, [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity)) as constraints and penalties; the regime
adjustment of S5.5 the multipliers of the current [regime](../../glossary.md#regime); the cost surface of S5.8 the costs and
capacities measured live; the deployment rulebook of S5.9 the soft and hard limits. Section 1
brings the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) data: sealed batches to estimate, the
as-of API to read live, the replay API to test on the past. Section 2 brings the registered
[features](../../glossary.md#feature), served in production exactly as in research. Stabilisation
(S6.5) may slow the cadence of computation, or switch a costly module off, by control directive.

The target portfolio holds the target weights per strategy and asset, the planned turnover and
the [no-trade zones](../../glossary.md#no-trade-zone) for S5.6; the execution constraints derived
from them (participation, windows) for S5.7; and the sensitivities and diagnostics for S5.8, whose
attribution closes the loop of costs and risk. S5.5 reads it to see which constraints bind, and
S5.9 audits it. The runbook of resolution, with its [fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback) profiles, goes to S6.7.

**Constraints.**
- Strictly point-in-time; units and horizons harmonised; the same universe across S5.1, S5.2,
  S5.4 and S5.7.
- Formats: dense or sparse matrices and tensors are accepted, with a stable, versioned schema of
  exchange.

**Service levels:**
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting) integrated at T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) in
  less than one decision cycle; an alert when exceeded.
- Update of the portfolio: p95 solve time below 10% of the [rebalancing](../../glossary.md#rebalancing) interval; publication to
  S5.6 within 5 min of the solve (configurable).
- An alert on any breach of consistency: an asset missing from one link of the chain, an
  incoherent budget, a missing cost.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the universe and the budgets (S5.4) are validated;
- the alphas (S5.1) are prudent, and the interactions (S5.2) up to date;
- the cost models are operational, and the tests against [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) are
  green.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the target weights are feasible, the sensitivities complete and the diagnostics documented;
- the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of the steps are within their bands;
- the artefacts are published to S5.6, S5.7 and S5.8.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) beyond its band two cycles in a row;
- the [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes markedly (S5.5);
- market rules or frictions change;
- a major alpha arrives (S5.1);
- the gaps of cost analysis persist (S5.8).

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPIs and alerts: stability, rate of feasibility, budgets drawn.
Lifecycle (S7) receives the policy for promoting or withdrawing a formulation (v1, v2), the journal
of changes to penalties and budgets, and the triggers of [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Frame of feasibility and living budgets</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Set the playable perimeter (universe, limits, envelopes), so that no infeasible problem
reaches the steps downstream.

**Actions.**
- Consolidate with S5.1, S5.2 and S5.4: the universe, the capacities (average daily volume,
  depth), the limits (leverage, concentration, exposures), the quotas by family, the regulatory and
  operational constraints.
- Write the map of constraints: what is hard (never exceeded) and what is soft (tolerated at a
  penalty), the [hard / soft constraint](../../glossary.md#hard-soft-constraint) split.

**KPI.** Rate of feasibility ≥ 99% in simulation; budget used against budget allocated (avoid a
chronic use below 70%).

**Guardrail.** When a problem is infeasible: a matrix of conflicts (which constraint blocks) and a
documented order of relaxation.

**Output.** The bundle of constraints and the budgets in numbers (risk, liquidity, leverage,
turnover) → step 2; they calibrate the objective and the penalties of step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Clean, prudent inputs: alphas, risk, costs</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Convert research into inputs that are net, prudent and point-in-time.

**Actions.**
- Alphas: clean them, align them in time, apply statistical prudence
  ([shrinkage](../../glossary.md#shrinkage) of their optimism), and match their horizons and
  [half-life](../../glossary.md#half-life).
- Risk: estimate covariances and exposures consistent with S5.2 (interactions, drawdowns that
  coincide).
- Costs: curves of [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), impact and latency, set by participation and by market.
- Uncertainty: define realistic margins, or sets of uncertainty, on alphas, risks and costs.

**KPI.** [Out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) stability of the inputs (p95 of the relative variation below a threshold);
a minimum ratio of signal to risk for every alpha kept.

**Guardrail.** Tests against leakage in time, and of replicability: an input that fails is
discarded.

**Output.** Vector of net alphas, structure of risk, cost model and margins of uncertainty →
step 3; they condition the multi-term objective and the constraints on probabilities.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Specify the objective and its penalties</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Write a readable objective: expected net value, minus a penalty for risk, a penalty for
costs and a penalty for change.

**Actions.**
- Define the units (horizon, currency of performance) and the exchange rate between one point of
  risk, one point of cost and one point of turnover.
- Set the weights of the penalties with the budgets of S5.4 and the regime of S5.5.
- List the constraints, hard and soft: leverage, liquidity, exposures, concentration, turnover.

**KPI.** Monotonicity: a higher risk budget raises the optimal exposure (a test of consistency).

**Guardrail.** Controlled sensitivity: the change of weights per change of penalty is bounded,
against over-calibration.

**Output.** A clear mathematical specification (objective, constraints, tolerances) → step 4, the
material of the convex and robust formulation.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · A convex formulation with stochastic guardrails</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn the objective and the constraints into a convex programme that can be solved, with
[guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail) that account for probability and uncertainty.

**Actions.**
- Choose convex penalties that stabilise the weights: convex norms, smoothing of turnover.
- Implement constraints on probabilities (for example the probability of a loss or a cost above a
  threshold) through convex approximations.
- Integrate the sets of uncertainty (alphas, risks, costs), for robust solutions.
- Check that the problem is well posed, with a unique solution: no artificial flat valleys.

**KPI.** Rate of convergence to a unique solution; acceptable conditioning (the
[condition number](../../glossary.md#condition-number)); budgets used within their target band.

**Guardrail.** When the conditioning is weak: documented regularisation, and bounds on weights and
turnover.

**Output.** Formulation v1, ready to solve → step 5; it sets the stability that the policy of
resolution relies on.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Policy of resolution and operational calibration</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Guarantee a reliable, fast solve, with a plan B under stress. Choosing the cadence, the
warm start and the fallback is design; calibrating them in operation is execution.

**Actions.**
- Define the cadence (intraday, daily) and the service level of computation.
- Set the policy of warm start (reuse the previous solution) and the no-trade zones.
- Define the [fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback): when the problem is infeasible or too slow, a
  more conservative version runs (stronger penalties, a subset of assets).

**KPI.** p95 solve time below 10% of the rebalancing interval; solves that fail below 0.5% of the
runs.

**Guardrail.** The conservative profile triggers automatically when the service level is exceeded
or the budgets are drawn to 100%.

**Output.** Runbook of resolution and the fallback profiles → step 6; they keep the service to
S5.6 and S5.7 continuous.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Produce the target weights, sensitivities and diagnostics</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Produce what will become orders, and document why.

**Actions.**
- Generate the target weights per strategy and asset, and the forecast of turnover.
- Extract the sensitivities, the [shadow prices](../../glossary.md#shadow-price) of the
  constraints: which ones bind (risk, liquidity, concentration, turnover)?
- Keep a dashboard: feasibility, budgets used, stability against the previous run.

**KPI.** Stability: p90 of the absolute change of weights below a threshold; useful turnover, the
share of turnover that explains the net performance.

**Guardrail.** A clamp on change when the
[value at risk of change](../../glossary.md#value-at-risk-of-change) exceeds its cap.

**Output.** The [target portfolio](../../glossary.md#target-portfolio): weights, sensitivities and
diagnostics → S5.6 (planner), S5.7 (execution), S5.8 (cost analysis), and governance (S5.9).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Improve with S5.8, and reset the budgets of S5.4</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Close the loop between the forecast (ex ante) and the realised (ex post), and adjust the
engine.

**Actions.**
- Compare the forecast risk and costs with the realised ones; attribute the gaps to the model,
  the market or the execution.
- Recalibrate the penalties and budgets (S5.4), and the tolerances by regime (S5.5).
- Update the map of bottlenecks (sensitivities that persist), to guide future trade-offs.

**KPI.** Gap between ex ante and ex post (risk and costs) within its target bands; the median gap
falls quarter after quarter.

**Guardrail.** When breaches repeat: a full [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification), and budgets lowered for a time.

**Output.** Corrections of parameters and a report of gaps → steps 1 and 3, S5.4, S5.5, S5.8,
S5.9.

</details>

General guardrails, one per step, sum up each step's KPI and guardrail: row 1 states step 1's floor
of use (70% of the budget) as a ceiling on chronic under-investment (30%), and row 3 adds tests of
consistency under stress.

| Area | KPI | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Feasibility and budgets | feasibility ≥ 99%; chronic under-investment < 30% | matrix of conflicts and order of relaxation |
| Net inputs | out-of-sample stability; minimum ratio of signal to risk | no leakage, point-in-time |
| Objective and penalties | monotonicity against the budgets; bounded sensitivity | tests of consistency under stress |
| Convex, robust formulation | a unique solution; budgets used "just right" | regularisation and bounds when the conditioning is weak |
| Resolution | p95 solve time < 10% of the cadence; failures < 0.5% | the conservative profile, automatically |
| Weights and sensitivities | stability; useful turnover | a clamp through the value at risk of change |
| Loop ex post | gap between ex ante and ex post within its band, falling | requalification forced when the gaps persist |

## Deliverables

- Versioned specification of the objective and the constraints: hard and soft, penalties,
  horizons, units.
- Log of choices, readable in audit: budgets, tolerances, bands of KPIs, orders of relaxation.
- The convex formulation, ready to solve, and the conservative fallback profiles.
- Production modules: generation of the weights, extraction of the sensitivities, computation of
  the diagnostics.
- Real-time KPI dashboard: feasibility, budgets, stability, useful turnover, ex ante against ex
  post.
- A battery of tests: leakage, monotonicity, stress by regime, infeasibility simulated and
  documented.
- Replacement process: criteria and the procedure step by step (activating v2, rollback,
  communication between sections).
