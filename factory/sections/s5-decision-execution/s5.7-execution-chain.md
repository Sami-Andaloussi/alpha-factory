# S5.7 — Unified execution chain (pre-trade, smart routing, OMS/EMS)

## TL;DR

Turns target weights and a transition path into real fills at the least total cost of implementation, for the same intent and the same risk.
It chooses the style, slices and paces the child orders, picks venues and order types, adapts in real time to the book and to latency, and stays safe: guardrails, circuit breakers, kill switch.
Every order leaves a complete, immutable trace, the execution log, which execution analytics (S5.8), cost analysis (S4.7) and audit read.

## Purpose

Turn target weights and a transition path into real fills at the least total cost of
implementation, for the same intent and the same risk. Concretely: choose the style of execution,
cut and pace the child orders ([order slicing](../../glossary.md#order-slicing)), decide where and
how to meet the market ([smart order routing](../../glossary.md#smart-order-routing): [venues](../../glossary.md#venue),
passive or aggressive), adapt in real time to the book and to latency, while staying safe
([guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail), [kill switches](../../glossary.md#kill-switch)) and traceable (fine journalling). This
is the factory's [execution chain](../../glossary.md#execution-chain), from the
[pre-trade checks](../../glossary.md#pre-trade-check) to the
[OMS and EMS](../../glossary.md#order-management-system).

**Success looks like:** adherence to the path, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) low and
stable, safety without incident, and complete telemetry for S5.8 and for the loops that recalibrate
S5.4 and S5.3.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The transition plan of S5.6 brings the paths, the windows, the caps of participation, the
[no-trade zones](../../glossary.md#no-trade-zone) and the
[value at risk of change](../../glossary.md#value-at-risk-of-change); the target portfolio of S5.3
the execution constraints derived from the weights; the risk budget book of S5.4 the caps of
scheduling per [venue](../../glossary.md#venue) and window, the hourly rules and the allowed lists;
the regime adjustment of S5.5 the adjusted targets and their urgency; the entry tickets of S5.1 each
strategy's limits of participation and maximum latency; the execution policy of S5.8 the rules per
context, with the laws of cost and [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) recalibrated live; the
deployment rulebook of S5.9 the [gates](../../glossary.md#gate) and the real-time controls, and the
rules of compliance: lists of venues, instruments not to trade (the
[restricted list](../../glossary.md#restricted-list)), limits per instrument. Section 4's
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator), calibrated, prices each style before
trading. The as-of API of S1.8 serves the market: level 1 and level 2 of the books, halts and
auctions, the states of venues. Live [execution fidelity](../../glossary.md#execution-fidelity)
(S6.2) sends its control directives: slow down, reroute, switch between passive and aggressive, or
enter a degraded mode, each with its time to live and its criteria of revert. Stabilisation (S6.5)
sends the directives of its modes: participation, cadence, timeouts, the depth of the algorithms,
routing.

The execution log goes to S5.8 with its [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) pictures,
for attribution and recalibration; the corrections to the budgets and to the objective of S5.3 and
S5.4 go through S5.8. It also goes to section 4's cost analysis (S4.7), whose
[calibration](../../glossary.md#calibration) continues on live fills; to S5.2, which maps the flows
of orders; to S5.9 for audit; to live execution fidelity (S6.2), which rebuilds from it the film of
execution; to data integrity (S6.3), which reconciles it with brokers and venues; and to the system
of record for decisions (S7.2), which links each fill to the decision behind it. Monitoring (S6.1,
and S6.2 for execution fidelity) receives the live metrics: latency, errors, costs, safety events.
The safety runbook goes to S6.7.

**Constraints.**
- Timestamps monotonic and point-in-time; stable IDs from parent to children to fills;
  [idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent) re-sends.
- Formats: a versioned schema, [deterministic](../../glossary.md#deterministic) serialisation, minimum mandatory fields (IDs,
  timestamps, a summary of the state of the book, decision, result).

**Service levels** (templates, to specialise):
- End to end, from the parent order to the child's acknowledgement: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95)
  ≤ X ms.
- Adaptation, from sensor to action: T95 ≤ Y ms.
- The journal visible to S5.8: T95 ≤ Z s.
- Critical events: a suspension or halt blocks routing at T95 ≤ 500 ms after detection; an IPO or
  new listing has its symbols and venues integrated at T95 ≤ 60 s; a delisting is fully blocked
  at T95 ≤ 10 min.
- When a T90 or T95 level above is exceeded, an alert fires: routing switches to its conservative
  profile, and S6 is notified.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the contracts of S5.6 are validated, the budgets of S5.4 active, and section 4's cost models
  signed;
- a dry run on the critical scenarios has passed: a halt, an exploding [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), a venue down;
- fine journalling is tested: 100% [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability).

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when execution has been stable over N sessions:
- median slippage within the budget of steps A and B, and its variance below its threshold;
- adherence to the path (step C) on target, zero safety incidents (step E), complete journals
  (step F);
- the flow of cost analysis to S5.8 is consumable: its schema is validated.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- a [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) by more than 2σ over 3 sessions;
- the structure changes: new market rules or venues, new windows or budgets from S5.4, a change of
  sensors;
- a promising new method arrives from S5.8 or section 4, with a demonstrated cut in costs;
- a safety incident fires a kill switch or a [fallback](../../glossary.md#fallback).

Monitoring (S6) receives the live gauges, the safety events and the breaches of service levels.
Lifecycle (S7) receives the requests to promote or withdraw a policy, the scheduled
[canary releases](../../glossary.md#canary-release) and runs in
[shadow mode](../../glossary.md#shadow-mode), and the versioned plan of
[rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>A · State the intent of execution: from upstream contracts to policies</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** S5.6 defined where to go, the path; this step fixes how to get there, in the language of
execution.

**Actions.**
- Consolidate the upstream budgets: risk, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity), impact, participation, windows of inaction.
- Declare an intent for each parent order: its objective (the
  [implementation shortfall](../../glossary.md#implementation-shortfall), or a share of volume or
  the volume-weighted average price), its horizon, its initial mix of passive and aggressive, its
  constraints of venue (lit, dark, auctions), its local limits (per instrument, venue, latency).
- Define the tests of success before trading: what S5.8 will judge.

**KPI.** Completeness of the contract ≥ 99%; budgets and intent consistent, with no
contradiction; rate of ambiguities = 0.

**Guardrail.** A validator of constraints, which refuses incompatible limits; a logical dry run on
the edge cases (overnight, halts).

**Output.** The execution contract of the parent order, a standard specification → step B.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>B · Style and guardrails: one pre-trade for all</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without an explicit style and guardrails, slicing and routing (steps C and D) have no
consistent heading.

**Actions.**
- Estimate the expected cost under several styles compatible with step A: paths, and mixes of
  passive and aggressive.
- Choose the primary style and its rules of escalation: when to speed up or slow down, switch from
  passive to aggressive, change venue.
- Set hard guardrails: caps of participation, maximum child sizes, windows where nothing trades,
  limits of latency, thresholds of cost per minute.

**KPI.** Median error of the forecast before trading against the simulated result after it ≤ X bp
(set per class of liquidity); coverage of scenarios ≥ 95%.

**Guardrail.** [Stress tests](../../glossary.md#stress-test) of a switch of style (no break above Y bp on a switch); hard caps on
participation and child size.

**Output.** Profile of style and its guardrails, the operating policy → step C.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>C · Slicing and scheduling: the metronome</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn steps A and B into robust, paced waves of child orders. The rules of pacing and
adherence are design; producing the waves is execution.

**Actions.**
- Define the granularity of the children (size, cadence), consistent with participation, average
  daily volume and depth.
- Programme adaptive pacing, a function of the volume observed, the spread, the volatility and the
  time left.
- Set the rules of adherence to the path: how far behind or ahead, and the tolerances.

**KPI.** Adherence to the path: pacing error at T90 ≤ ε%; the sizes of the children dispersed
within the planned envelope; the parent's gap from its path ≤ its threshold.

**Guardrail.** An adherence stop when the delay stays above 2ε%; a lock against over-slicing,
which avoids micro-children that cannot execute.

**Output.** Timestamped plan of the waves of children, which can be resampled in real time →
step D.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>D · Smart routing and microstructure: where and how to take or post</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Each child must choose its venue and its order type for the best quality of fill.

**Actions.**
- Select venues adaptively (lit, dark, auctions), with rules to switch when quality, latency or
  toxicity degrade.
- Set the policy of limit, market and pegged orders, with the management of the queue: position,
  cancels, a prudent jump in the queue.
- Set rules to reallocate at once when a venue turns harmful: rejections, abnormal slippage,
  latency.

**KPI.** Quality of fill per venue (slippage against the arrival price); rate of rejections ≤ its
threshold; time to the first fill at T90 ≤ its target.

**Guardrail.** A score of toxicity per venue, with automatic
[quarantine](../../glossary.md#quarantine) above its threshold, and an automatic switch to the
safe venues.

**Output.** Routing decisions for the children (venue, type, parameters) and the journal of their
reasons → step E.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>E · Real-time adaptation and safety</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The market moves: adapt without zigzagging, and stay safe.

**Actions.**
- Sensors of microstructure: imbalance of the book, bursts of cancels, spreads, latencies, time to
  the first fill.
- Loops of adaptation: modulate participation and pacing, switch from passive to aggressive,
  change venue, on the signals.
- Safety: throttles, local [circuit breakers](../../glossary.md#circuit-breaker), the kill,
  degraded modes ([controlled degradation](../../glossary.md#controlled-degradation)) and the
  fallback, for example to a more conservative profile.

**KPI.** Adverse selection after the fill, the median [markout](../../glossary.md#markout) ≤ its
threshold; capture of the spread, when passive, on target; effective latency at T95 ≤ its budget.

**Guardrail.** Throttles on several levels; circuit breakers (for example a cost per minute above
threshold Z triggers a local kill); a fallback proven in less than T.

**Output.** Signals of adjustment (to steps C and D) and safety events → step F, monitoring (S6),
governance (S5.9).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>F · Fine journalling and acknowledgements: one black box</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a point-in-time record of every order, nothing can be measured or improved
(S5.8).

**Actions.**
- An immutable log per child: timestamps, the state of level 1 and level 2, decisions, parameters,
  rejections, latency, position in the queue, fills.
- Traceability end to end: IDs from parent to children to fills, monotonic clocks, idempotent
  sends and re-sends.
- Standard acknowledgements of sending, acceptance and rejection, for audit and for recovery after
  an incident.

**KPI.** Completeness of the journal: 100% of parents, children and fills linked; logs lost = 0;
delay of acknowledgement at T95 ≤ its budget.

**Guardrail.** An order is refused when it cannot be traced (no parent ID); a channel is
quarantined when a loss of logs is detected.

**Output.** The complete [execution log](../../glossary.md#execution-log) and its stream of
telemetry → S5.8 (cost analysis and simulation), monitoring (S6), S5.4 and S5.3 (recalibration,
through S5.8), governance (S5.9).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of the execution contract of the parent order (step A): a standard template.
- Profile of style and guardrails (step B): documented rules of escalation.
- Plan of the waves of children, and the rules of adherence (step C).
- Tables of routing decisions (step D): criteria, priorities, switches.
- Safety runbook (step E): throttles, circuit breakers, kill, fallback, tests of recovery.
- The black box (step F): schema of the logs, a real example, the procedure of audit.
- KPI dashboard per step, with thresholds (alert at +2σ, cut at +3σ), and the process of
  [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification).
- Log of choices, for audit, and the replacement process: how to introduce or withdraw a style, a
  rule or a venue.
