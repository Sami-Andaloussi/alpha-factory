# S5.2 — Interaction mapping & alpha diversification

## TL;DR

Turns a pile of strategies into a coherent portfolio by mapping who resembles whom, who completes whom, who gets in whose way, and who breaks together.
The map follows dependences over time, shared factors, crowding of liquidity and drawdowns that coincide under stress, with simple gauges that light up before the damage.
It ends in interaction rules (caps, penalties, neutralisations) that portfolio construction (S5.3) and the budgets (S5.4) apply as they are.

## Purpose

Turn a pile of strategies into a coherent portfolio: map which strategies resemble one another,
which complete one another, which get in each other's way, and which break together.

**Success looks like:** a living [interaction map](../../glossary.md#interaction-map) of the
relations between strategies (dependences, shared factors, crowding, [drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown) that coincide), and a
set of interaction rules (caps, penalties, [neutralisations](../../glossary.md#neutralisation))
ready to plug into S5.3 and S5.4, with simple gauges that light up before the damage.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The entry tickets of S5.1, with the normalised dossiers they reference, bring each strategy's
card (quality, horizon, estimated [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity), universe, constraints),
its [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) series of P&L and its aggregated exposures;
the execution log of S5.7 brings the flows of orders; the cost surface of S5.8 the costs and
impact, expected and realised, the latencies and the frictions by segment. Monitoring (S6) sends
its alerts of [drift](../../glossary.md#drift), incidents of execution and market events.

The interaction map holds the film of dynamic dependences, the matrix of overlap between
strategies, the heatmaps of crowding, the stress map, the watchlist, and the interaction rules with
their files of constraints and budgets. S5.3 and S5.4 apply the rules; S5.1 reads the map to judge
a new strategy's compatibility; S5.5 reads which families break together under stress; the
execution side (S5.6, S5.7) receives the caps on crowding through the budgets of S5.4. The
Strategy Lab (S3.4) reads the map to see how a strategy under study would sit among those already
running. Monitoring (S6.1, S6.4) receives the gauges and the watchlist.

**Constraints.**
- Formats: stable strategy IDs, normalised timestamps, documented conventions of sign and scale.
- Consistency: aligned horizons (windows of analysis matched to the
  [half-life](../../glossary.md#half-life) of the [alphas](../../glossary.md#alpha)), and aggregations that can be replicated.

**Service levels** (examples, to calibrate):
- Dynamic dependences: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) on D+1, after the close.
- Intraday crowding: T95 ≤ a few minutes after the flows are consolidated.
- Export of the rules: T95 before the [rebalancing](../../glossary.md#rebalancing) window.
- Critical events (IPOs, suspensions, delistings): T90 / T95 of ingestion; an alert when a level is
  exceeded, and the default caps put in place.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the strategies are eligible (S5.1), with series of P&L and positions that are point-in-time;
- the universe and the metadata are filled in: horizon, capacity, constraints;
- the data contract (step 1) is validated.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the interaction rules are versioned and tested in a dry run of at least 30–90 days;
- the files of constraints and budgets are accepted by S5.3 and S5.4 (tests of ingestion pass);
- the dashboard is in place, with its [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) green over the period of validation.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the tests when:
- a material strategy is added or removed (more than X% of the targeted risk);
- the KPIs drift beyond their thresholds: fewer independent bets, more drawdowns that coincide,
  more crowding;
- the [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes durably (volatility, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity)), a market rule changes, or
  a gap from the cost analysis persists;
- the method of estimation or the segmentation is updated, which resets the local validations.

Monitoring (S6) receives the lists of alerts, their thresholds and the events to listen for:
drawdowns that coincide, pressure on a cluster, a broken link. Lifecycle (S7) receives the triggers
of promotion and withdrawal, the cadence of reviews, and the requirement to run the [backtests](../../glossary.md#backtest) again
when a major rule changes.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Inventory and data contract of interactions</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Lay clean, replayable foundations: which series and events are needed to analyse the
interactions without [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage). The inventory is execution; the unit of
analysis and the contract are design.

**Actions.**
- List the minimum inputs per strategy: point-in-time series of P&L, aggregated positions and
  exposures, flows of orders, key timestamps, the universe traded, estimates of capacity and
  costs.
- Define the unit of analysis (the strategy's natural horizon) and the keys of alignment:
  calendars, time zones, holidays, suspensions.
- Set a contract of freshness (maximum latency) and of completeness (the share of gaps tolerated)
  for each source.

**KPI.** Useful completeness: the % of points usable per window of analysis; alert below 95%.

**Guardrail.** Point-in-time and no-look-ahead tests on the sensitive fields; automatic rejection
of contaminated windows.

**Output.** The interaction base: aligned data and its contract of quality → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Dependences that move: a film, not a photograph</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Measure how the strategies move together over time (linear and non-linear, at the same
time and with a lag), and when their links strengthen or weaken.

**Actions.**
- Build measures of dependence in time, on rolling windows consistent with the horizons of the
  alphas.
- Capture the direction and the lag (who leads, who follows), and how the links vary by regime:
  volatility, liquidity, dispersion.
- Produce a timeline of matrices of interaction: the film of the dependences.

**KPI.** [Out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) stability of the strong links: the rate at which the top-k interactions
persist; alert below 60%.

**Guardrail.** A minimum [effective sample size](../../glossary.md#effective-sample-size) per
window; links that are not robust are masked (a minimum score of confidence).

**Output.** Film of dynamic interactions, by date and by pair → steps 3, 5 and 6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Shared factors and ideas: the real overlap</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Tell apart the common idea behind signals that look different, and quantify the share of
risk and P&L that is shared.

**Actions.**
- Extract latent drivers consistent with the horizons: directional factors, carry, dispersion,
  microstructure.
- Break each strategy down into normalised exposures to these drivers, plus its own residual.
- Build the matrix of overlap: the share of variance and P&L explained by common drivers.

**KPI.** Effective diversification: the [effective number of bets](../../glossary.md#effective-number-of-bets),
here 1 / the sum of the squared shares of overlap; alert when it falls by more than 20% over a
month.

**Guardrail.** [Cross-validation](../../glossary.md#cross-validation) over distinct periods and regimes; leakage controlled by keeping
estimation and evaluation apart in time.

**Output.** Matrix of overlap between drivers, and the exposures per strategy → steps 4 and 6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Crowding and shared capacity: where the factory steps on its own toes</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Measure where the factory consumes the same liquidity, at the same hours, on the same
names and segments. Mapping the consumption is execution; the clusters and indices of pressure are
design.

**Actions.**
- Map the relative consumption per instrument, segment and hour, against the depth available.
- Aggregate by operational cluster: the same windows, the same [venues](../../glossary.md#venue),
  the same micro-signals.
- Derive indices of pressure per cluster: aggregated participation, overlap of [order books](../../glossary.md#order-book),
  internal competition.

**KPI.** Aggregated rate of participation per cluster (median and P95); alert when the P95 exceeds
the internal threshold (for example X% of the relevant average daily volume).

**Guardrail.** Automatic blocks on market events (suspensions, IPOs, delistings); temporary caps
when the pressure stays above its threshold 3 days in a row.

**Output.** Heatmaps of capacity and crowding, and the operational clusters → steps 5 and 6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Stress and drawdowns that coincide: the fragile zones</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Identify the dangerous pairs and clusters that break together under shock, and quantify
how much their links strengthen under stress.

**Actions.**
- Define a library of scenarios: shocks of volatility, liquidity, dispersion, macro and
  microstructure.
- Measure, per pair and cluster, how much the dependence rises under stress and how often the
  [drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown) coincide, on a horizon aligned with the strategies.
- Draw up a prioritised watchlist of combinations to watch or to limit.

**KPI.** Rate of coinciding drawdowns beyond a threshold of loss (frequency and median depth);
alert above 2× the base on 3 scenarios.

**Guardrail.** Tests of sensitivity to the parameters of the scenarios; [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) required over
several periods.

**Output.** Stress map of the interactions and the watchlist of coinciding drawdowns → step 6,
monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Interaction rules and budgets, ready for S5.3 and S5.4</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Convert the map into constraints, penalties and interaction budgets that the optimiser
and the [allocation layer](../../glossary.md#allocation-layer) can use. The rules are design;
their delivery as formal interfaces is execution.

**Actions.**
- Define caps per cluster (risk, [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover), capacity), penalties for overlap (per pair or cluster),
  and targeted neutralisations of unwanted exposures.
- Specify the interaction budgets (maximum common risk, drawdowns that may coincide, pressure on
  capacity) and the order of priority between rules.
- Set the cadence of updates (for example daily for the dependences, weekly for the factors, on
  event for crowding and stress) and the triggers of [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification).
- Deliver a formal interface to S5.3 and S5.4: formats, tolerances, latencies.

**KPI.**
- Dilution: the real marginal gain of a new strategy after penalties (> 0 expected); alert at 0 or
  below.
- The effective number of bets left after the constraints, as S5.3 uses them; alert below the
  strategic threshold.
- Coinciding drawdowns, realised against the rule: occurrences above the cap; alert when exceeded.

**Guardrail.** A dry run of the decisions before deployment (simulated over 30–90 days); soft and
hard limits on the critical rules ([hard / soft constraint](../../glossary.md#hard-soft-constraint));
a documented [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).

**Output.** Interaction rules v1, the files of constraints and budgets, and the contract of
interface with S5.3, S5.4 and S6 → S5.3 (construction), S5.4 (budgets), S5.5 (regimes), S5.6 and
S5.7 through S5.4 (execution), monitoring (S6).

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: scope, definitions, conventions, horizons, formats of exchange.
- Log of choices: hypotheses, trade-offs, versions, results of the dry runs, criteria of go /
  no-go.
- Modules and documentation: the film of interactions, the matrix of overlap, the heatmaps of
  capacity, the stress map; the generator of interaction rules (caps, penalties, neutralisations)
  and its standard export.
- KPI tracking table: a summary view (effective number of bets, overlap, crowding, coinciding
  drawdowns, marginal gain) with history and alerts.
- Replacement process: how to update or withdraw a rule, the rollback, the expected impacts, the
  tests to run again.
