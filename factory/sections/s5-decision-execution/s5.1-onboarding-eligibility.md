# S5.1 — Onboarding & capital eligibility

## TL;DR

Turns a strategy promoted by section 4 into a line of the portfolio ready to live: whether it enters, at what initial size, within which bounds and guardrails.
It reasons net of costs, impact and liquidity, at the strategy's own pace, and without redundancy with the strategies already running; the verdict is green, orange (a dated pilot) or red.
Its signed entry ticket, executable from the next day, is what the rest of section 5 sizes, budgets, trades and deploys.

## Purpose

Turn the validated dossier that leaves section 4 into a line of the portfolio that is ready to
live: decide whether the strategy enters, at what initial size, and with which bounds and
[guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail). The decision is taken net of costs, [market impact](../../glossary.md#market-impact)
and [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity); at the right pace (horizon,
[half-life](../../glossary.md#half-life)); and without redundancy (compatibility, exposures). It is
fully traced, with its triggers of [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification). This is
the factory's [onboarding](../../glossary.md#onboarding), and it decides the strategy's
[capital eligibility](../../glossary.md#capital-eligibility).

**Success looks like:** an [entry ticket](../../glossary.md#entry-ticket) that is robust, can be
explained aloud, is executable from the next day (D+1), and is measurable in the weeks that follow.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The promotion record of S4.10 brings section 4's evidence: the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) series of P&L, net
and gross, the costs and impact expected before trading, the results of the
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator), the [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) segments and the
operating charter. The dossier of S3.9 brings the universe and its rules of inclusion, the logs of
artefacts, and the factor references of sections 2 and 3. The as-of API gives section 1's
data point-in-time: the [reference data](../../glossary.md#reference-data) (the instrument register, the calendar) and the volumes. The
interaction map of S5.2 shows the current correlations and the [drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown) that coincide among the
strategies already running; the risk budget book of S5.4 gives the budgets and constraints (risk,
liquidity, [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity), leverage); the deployment rulebook of S5.9 the
soft and hard limits that apply.

The entry ticket carries the verdict and the term sheet (initial weight, minimum and maximum
bounds, target [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover), maximum intraday and daily losses, limits
of participation, cadence), the eligibility score, the compatibility report, the note on net
value and capacity, the stop rules, the monitoring hooks and the triggers of requalification.
S5.2 updates its map with it; S5.3, S5.4, S5.6 and S5.7 size, budget, plan and trade from it;
S5.9 deploys it by stages; lifecycle (S7.6) receives its cadence of reviews and its criteria of
promotion, demotion and archiving. A request to change a budget goes to S5.4 with the ticket.

**Constraints.**
- Everything point-in-time: versioned identifiers, explicit units, harmonised time zones.

**Service levels:**
- From intake to verdict: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 2 business days per strategy.
- A critical event of the universe (IPO, suspension, delisting that affects capacity) triggers a
  local re-evaluation at T95 ≤ 1 business day.
- Monitoring hooks published ≤ 4 h after the verdict.
- An alert when a level is exceeded; deployment is blocked while the hooks are missing.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- section 4's dossier is complete and reproducible: the promotion record is signed;
- costs and impact are present, the out-of-sample segments are identified, and the universe is
  fixed;
- no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) alert is open.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the entry ticket is signed, with its bounds fixed;
- the monitoring hooks are active, and the consumers notified;
- the journal is sealed.

The ticket's colour decides what follows: *green*, activation is allowed; *orange*, a dated pilot
with its criteria of graduation; *red*, the reasons and the lines of work go back to the Strategy
Lab (section 3). *Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- a [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): the gap between promised and realised above 30%, two or
  more violations of bounds in 10 sessions, or a [marginal Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#marginal-sharpe-ratio)
  that falls to 0 or below;
- costs or liquidity are shocked: cost models revised, microstructure changed, a significant fall
  in average daily volume;
- exposures change: a new marginal correlation above 0.8 with a line already running;
- the market moves: an IPO, suspension or delisting that affects capacity; new market rules;
- a method changes: a substantial improvement proposed by section 3 or 4.

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPIs, their thresholds and the alert webhooks. Lifecycle (S7) receives
the cadence of reviews, the criteria of promotion and demotion, and the triggers of archiving. The
realised costs that S5.8 measures come back here, to compare what was promised with what was
realised.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Take in and normalise the dossier</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Receive section 4's package ([backtests](../../glossary.md#backtest) and execution simulation, costs, out-of-sample
results, universe, rules) and put it in this sub-section's fixed, point-in-time format: a common
language, and no lopsided comparisons.

**Actions.**
- Check integrity: periods, calendars, [survivorship bias](../../glossary.md#survivorship-bias),
  fees.
- Align the definitions (P&L net and gross, turnover, universe), and timestamp the versions.

**KPI.** Completeness of the dossier: ≥ 98% of the required fields.

**Guardrail.** Schema validation, and a [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) test of a sample P&L: rejection when it does not
reproduce.

**Output.** Normalised dossier (fixed schema, completed checklist) → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Net value added and capacity</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Estimate a realistic net edge, and the capacity the strategy has before it impacts
itself. On a normalised dossier, the [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) can now be confronted with costs, impact and liquidity.

**Actions.**
- Translate the expected alpha after costs and impact into a range: central and conservative.
- Draw a capacity curve (size against the share of average daily volume, and participation) and
  define its zones: safe, prudent, forbidden.
- Fix limits of participation and a target turnover compatible with the horizon.

**KPI.** Conservative net alpha ≥ 0 at the proposed initial size; the slope at which the alpha
degrades as participation grows.

**Guardrail.** Sensitivity to the assumptions (costs and impact × 1.5): the alpha must stay
positive under light stress.

**Output.** Note on net value and capacity (net alpha as a range, capacity by latency and by
market, limits of participation) → steps 3 and 5, and the budgets of S5.4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Horizon, half-life and confidence</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Set the strategy's natural pace and quantify the confidence in its figures: knowing the
speed of the signal and its out-of-sample stability allows sizing without [overfitting](../../glossary.md#overfitting).

**Actions.**
- Estimate the half-life and the useful horizon; choose a coherent cadence of adjustment.
- Assess the out-of-sample stability (by [regime](../../glossary.md#regime), slice of time and
  universe) and the coverage of scenarios.
- Assign a confidence tier (high, medium, low) that moderates the initial size.

**KPI.** Ratio of out-of-sample to in-sample on the key metrics (net return,
[drawdown](../../glossary.md#drawdown)), and stability by regime.

**Guardrail.** Tests of orthogonality in time, or simple permutations, to detect
overfitting.

**Output.** Profile of pace and confidence (recommended cadence, half-life, confidence tier, rules
of minimum persistence) → step 5; a reference for monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Exposures and compatibility with the portfolio</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Check that the strategy completes the whole without doubling a risk already held. With
its pace and net edge known, measure what the strategy carries and with whom it moves.

**Actions.**
- Map the exposures: factors, sectors, styles, single names, regions.
- Measure the co-movements, and the drawdowns that coincide, with the lines in place; estimate the
  marginal contribution to diversification.
- Derive the local structural rules: caps, [neutralisations](../../glossary.md#neutralisation),
  exclusions.

**KPI.** Simulated marginal Sharpe ratio at portfolio level > 0; marginal correlation under
control.

**Guardrail.** A test of overlap by instrument and sector; a rule against counting twice the
near-identical signals.

**Output.** Compatibility report (vector of exposures, score of complementarity, recommended caps
and neutralisations) → step 5; S5.2, which updates its map.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Decision and entry ticket</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Issue the verdict and the executable term sheet. Once net value, capacity, pace and
compatibility are established, the strategy can be sized and bounded.

**Actions.**
- Classify green, orange or red:
  - green: initial weight, minimum and maximum bounds, target turnover, maximum losses (intraday
    and cumulative), limits of participation, maximum latency tolerated;
  - orange: a pilot at reduced size, its duration, and criteria of graduation (the gap between
    promised and realised, the discipline of bounds);
  - red: precise reasons, and a return to section 3.
- Define the stop rules (soft and hard) and the signals of pause tied to the KPIs.

**KPI.** The gap between promised and realised, net, over 4 to 6 weeks; respect of the bounds
(turnover, losses, participation).

**Guardrail.** The conservative option wins: when a parameter is in doubt, a smaller size and a
conditional graduation.

**Output.** Signed entry ticket and eligibility score (an aggregate of net value, confidence,
capacity and compatibility) → S5.3 (construction), S5.4 (budgets), S5.6 (transitions), S5.7
(execution).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Journal, authorisations and operational handshake</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Make the decision auditable, and connect the sensors of monitoring and lifecycle: after
the decision come its record and the wiring of its follow-up.

**Actions.**
- Journal the reasons (with the key calculations), the timestamps and the signatures, research
  kept separate from deployment.
- Publish the monitoring hooks (this sub-section's KPIs) and the triggers of requalification.
- Notify the consumers formally (S5.2, S5.3, S5.4, S5.6, S5.7), and check that they have read it.

**KPI.** Delay from decision to operational availability (its service level); completeness of the
journal: 100% of the critical fields.

**Guardrail.** Two pairs of eyes, mandatory, on the ticket and the hooks; no deployment is possible
without the journal.

**Output.** Sealed onboarding dossier, a follow-up plan for 4–6 weeks and a checklist of
authorisation → monitoring (S6), lifecycle (S7), governance (S5.9).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of the onboarding identity card: its schema and its guide of assessment.
- Note on net value and capacity: ranges, zones, limits of participation, target turnover.
- Report on pace and confidence: half-life, cadence, confidence tier.
- Report on compatibility and exposures: marginal contribution, recommended caps and
  neutralisations.
- Entry ticket, green, orange or red: the complete term sheet and its stop rules.
- Onboarding journal: reasons, signatures, versions, links to the artefacts.
- KPI table of this sub-section, ready for monitoring, with thresholds and alerts.
- Replacement plan: the procedure of pause and withdrawal, and the conditions of re-entry.
