# S5.8 — Execution analytics & the TCA/XSIM feedback loop into decisions

## TL;DR

Measures what really happened in execution, explains the gap with what was forecast before trading, then reprograms costs, capacities and execution rules so that the next decision is better.
Success: the gap between forecast and realised costs small and stable, more slippage explained than unexplained, and clean, versioned updates that improve the net P&L without chasing noise.
Its cost surface feeds construction, budgets, regimes and transitions (S5.3 to S5.6); its execution policy goes to the execution chain (S5.7), released by canary (S5.9).

## Purpose

Measure what really happened in execution, explain the gap with what was forecast before trading,
then reprogram the costs, the capacities and the rules of execution so that the next decision is
better. This is the factory's live [execution analytics](../../glossary.md#execution-analytics):
the loop from [transaction cost analysis](../../glossary.md#transaction-cost-analysis) and the
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) back into decisions.

**Success looks like:**
- the gap between the costs forecast before trading and those realised after it, small and stable;
- more [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) explained than unexplained;
- clean, versioned updates that improve the portfolio's net P&L without over-reacting to noise.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The execution log of S5.7 brings the detailed logs of orders and fills, and the parameters of
execution applied; the transition plan of S5.6 the plan that the fills are measured against; the
target portfolio of S5.3, the risk budget book of S5.4 and the regime adjustment of S5.5 the costs,
capacities and expected costs in force, to compare with the previous version. Section 4's
execution simulator gives the simulated reality that the counterfactuals are built on. The as-of
API of S1.8 gives the market as it was (quotes and trades, minimum depth, states of [venues](../../glossary.md#venue)), and
its replay API [replays](../../glossary.md#replay) a whole day with an alternative version, for audit.

The cost surface goes to S5.3 as versioned costs and penalties, to S5.4 as adjusted budgets (impact,
[liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity), [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover)) and capacities, to
S5.6 for its plans, to S5.5 for its test of cost against benefit, to S5.2 for its map of costs by
segment, and to S5.9, which governs its release. Capital steering (S7.8) reads it per unit of
allocation, for its register of [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) and costs. The execution
policy goes to S5.7: the rules of execution per context (mix, cadence, venues), released by canary
through S5.9. Monitoring (S6.1, and S6.2 for
[execution fidelity](../../glossary.md#execution-fidelity)) receives the live
[KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi), the thresholds of alert and the [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift)
detected. What is learnt also goes back as feedback: to the execution simulator of section 4, which
it recalibrates, and to S5.1 and S5.2, which compare the realised costs with those promised.

**Constraints.**
- Strictly [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time): nothing is ever rewritten.
- Synchronised timestamps across the market, the [OMS](../../glossary.md#oms) and [EMS](../../glossary.md#ems), and the engine of decision.
- Stable IDs of parent, child and venue; [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) from child to parent to strategy.
- Minimum granularity: the child order.

**Service levels:**
- Intraday cost analysis available at T90 on T+15 min, and at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) on
  T+30 min.
- An interim cost surface, for intraday decisions, at T90 on T+60 min.
- The validated versions published every night, at T95 on T+1.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting, a change of a venue's rules): integrated at T95 in
  less than 24 h; an alert when exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the point-in-time logging is validated (step 1);
- the basic cost analysis works, closing within ε (step 2);
- a calibrated execution simulator exists for the families of orders concerned.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the surfaces of cost and [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) are versioned, with their difference from the previous
  version documented;
- the budgets (S5.4) and the objective and constraints (S5.3) are updated by the protocol;
- the rules of execution are tested in a [canary release](../../glossary.md#canary-release),
  with KPIs at or above their threshold and a plan of [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback);
- the dashboards of S6 are connected to the new KPIs.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- a KPI [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): the error between forecast and realised above its
  threshold over 7 days, or the unexplained share up two windows in a row;
- the structure changes: a new venue or fee, a market rule, a change of tick, a new family of
  orders;
- a new [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) sets in: a lasting jump of volatility or liquidity;
- lifecycle (S7) signals a promising method, recalibrated in parallel and compared against a
  control.

Monitoring (S6) receives the thresholds of alert, the definitions of the KPIs, the states of the
canaries and the events of failure and success. Lifecycle (S7) receives the cadence of review of
the versions, the matrix of eligibility of the rules, and the conditions to promote or withdraw
them.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Contracts of data and point-in-time logging</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a clean log that the future never rewrites, everything else is wrong. Defining
the schema is design; freezing the references and capturing the estimates is execution.

**Actions.**
- Define the canonical schema of execution: parent and child, timestamps on the system's side and
  the market's, order IDs, tags of strategy, style of execution, venue, status (partly filled,
  cancelled, expired).
- Freeze the market reference of each child (quotes, trades, the minimum depth required) at the
  exact moment of the decision and of the fill.
- Capture the estimates made before trading (expected cost, target participation, local capacity)
  point-in-time, with their versions.

**KPI.** Rate of alignment between orders and market ≥ 99.5% on T+0; skew between clocks, median
gap < X ms; estimates before trading missing < 0.1%.

**Guardrail.** Tests against look-ahead (no update after T+0), a strict ordering in time, checks
that the states of orders move monotonically, and a reconciliation of volumes from child to
parent.

**Output.** Two aligned streams, the facts of execution and the estimates before trading, keyed by
order and timestamp → steps 2 and 3, monitoring (S6) for its controls of integrity.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Cost analysis after trading, and the decomposition of slippage</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** The measures of reality are the ground truth against which the models are judged.

**Actions.**
- Compute the [implementation shortfall](../../glossary.md#implementation-shortfall) per parent
  and per child.
- Break the slippage down: the [spread](../../glossary.md#spread) paid or avoided; the impact, temporary or persisting
  locally; timing (waiting, or the opportunity missed); routing and venue; and the opportunity of
  what was not executed.

**KPI.** Coverage of the cost analysis ≥ 99% of the orders; arithmetic closure, the sum of the
components equal to the implementation shortfall within ε; intraday stability, the residual
variance below its threshold.

**Guardrail.** Consistency across levels (child to parent); detection of anomalies (an
implementation shortfall above the historical p99); days of extreme events flagged.

**Output.** A normalised table of cost analysis, one column per component → steps 3 and 4,
monitoring's dashboards (S6), and the attribution of the portfolio (S5.1, S5.2).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Align forecast and realised: the error of the models</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Move from what happened to where the model is wrong, and in which conditions.

**Actions.**
- Join, for each order, the point-in-time estimate made before trading and the cost analysis
  after it.
- Compute the errors of the models: bias, mean absolute percentage error, conditional dispersion.
- Segment by context: instrument, hour, size, participation, regime of
  [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity).

**KPI.** Mean absolute percentage error of the expected costs, falling over the long run; mean
bias close to 0, within ±δ; coverage of the contexts ≥ 95%.

**Guardrail.** Periods of estimation and evaluation strictly apart; robust aggregation, so that a
few extreme orders do not dominate; bounds of physical sense: costs ≥ 0, simple monotonicities.

**Output.** A matrix of the error between forecast and realised by context, and versioned series
of errors → step 4, monitoring (S6, alerts of drift), S5.4 (early signs of recalibration).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Attribution and diagnostics along several dimensions</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Know what to change, where and in which direction, before any recalibration.

**Actions.**
- Explain the error ([execution attribution](../../glossary.md#execution-attribution)): the
  shares due to spread, impact, timing, routing and venue, opportunity.
- Map where it breaks: by strategy, style of execution, instrument, hour, venue, regime.
- Measure the explained and unexplained shares, and isolate the stable drivers.

**KPI.** Rate of slippage explained, rising; stability of the patterns (their persistence over
rolling windows); the net gain that could be earned, estimated, the basis of the return on the
work.

**Guardrail.** Tests of [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) out of time; a control against gaming, so that no local KPI is
optimised at the expense of the net P&L; penalties for complexity, preferring simple rules when
the gain is similar.

**Output.** A report of attribution of the errors, and a table of priorities (the top contexts to
correct, with their expected net gain) → step 5, S5.7 (rules of execution per context).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Recalibrate the surfaces of cost and capacity, for budgets and optimiser</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn the diagnosis into parameters of decision that the portfolio can use.

**Actions.**
- Update the surfaces of cost (as functions of volume, participation, liquidity and hour) and the
  sustainable capacities.
- Derive the living budgets of S5.4 (impact, liquidity, turnover); adjust the penalties of
  turnover and the bands of inaction that feed the objective and constraints of S5.3.
- Version each surface with its interval of uncertainty and its rules of damping: a progressive
  deployment.

**KPI.** Change of the surfaces per day ≤ its threshold; stability after deployment, the error
between forecast and realised falling; use of the budgets within its bounds.

**Guardrail.** Smoothing over time; caps of change per cycle; a [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest)
of non-regression (simulated net performance ≥ the control); no double counting: a cost sits in the
objective or in a constraint, never in both.

**Output.** The [cost surface](../../glossary.md#cost-surface), version X.Y, with its difference
from the previous version and its plan of deployment → S5.3 (optimiser), S5.4 (budgets), S5.6
(transition plan).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Bridge to the execution simulator: from counterfactuals to deployable rules</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Close the loop by putting the gain found into operation, without [overfitting](../../glossary.md#overfitting).

**Actions.**
- Build targeted counterfactuals, in the same context: another venue, another cadence, another mix
  of limit and market orders, another window.
- Estimate the regret and the expected uplift per style of execution and family of orders.
- Write simple, testable rules of execution per context, with a plan of canary release and
  [shadow mode](../../glossary.md#shadow-mode) (S5.9) and its criteria of promotion and rollback.
- Loop: after deployment, measure the gap between forecast and realised again, and feed it back to
  step 3.

**KPI.** Realised net uplift against a control, on the net P&L; rate of success of the canaries ≥
its target; time to detect a failure at T95; the reversion after the fill (the
[markout](../../glossary.md#markout)) falling when execution improves.

**Guardrail.** Local [kill switches](../../glossary.md#kill-switch); limits of loss specific to the
tests; flows of test and production kept apart; a minimum window of observation before promotion.

**Output.** The versioned [execution policy](../../glossary.md#execution-policy), its protocol of
test and its journal of decisions → S5.7 ([execution chain](../../glossary.md#execution-chain)), S5.9 (governance), monitoring (S6, the
tests), lifecycle (S7, the cycle of promotion).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of the facts of execution and of the estimates before trading: schema,
  timestamps, keys.
- A report of cost analysis, with its closure demonstrated and its decomposition validated.
- The matrix of errors between forecast and realised by context, and its protocol of robust
  aggregation.
- The report of attribution, and the table of priorities of the corrections.
- The versioned surfaces of cost and capacity (version X.Y), with their uncertainties, rules of
  damping and difference from the previous version.
- The plan of deployment: rules of execution per context, canary and shadow mode,
  the criteria of promotion and rollback.
- Live KPI dashboard and journal of decisions: who, what, why, impact.
- Replacement process: how to return to the previous version in less than N minutes, and how to
  replay the day with the alternative version, for audit.
