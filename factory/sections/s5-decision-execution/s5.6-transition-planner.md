# S5.6 — Transition planner (path-aware rebalancing)

## TL;DR

Takes the portfolio from its current state to its target weights at the least real cost, the least needless risk and the least time spent away from target.
It decides what to move, when and how fast: no-trade zones against noise, rules of participation that respect liquidity, and the rest of the path re-planned whenever the targets or the market move.
Its dated transition plan is what the execution chain (S5.7) carries out and what execution analytics (S5.8) measures.

## Purpose

Take the portfolio from its current state to the target weights while minimising the real costs,
the needless risks and the time spent away from target. In plain words: decide what to move, when
and how fast, with [no-trade zones](../../glossary.md#no-trade-zone) that avoid trading noise,
rules of participation that respect [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity), and continuous
re-planning when the targets or the market move. This is path-aware
[rebalancing](../../glossary.md#rebalancing).

**Success looks like:** the same destination with less friction; the gap from target under
control; costs in line with the forecast made before trading; a plan that is followed and
explained: the [transition plan](../../glossary.md#transition-plan).

<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The target portfolio of S5.3 brings the target weights with their timestamp and implied horizon; the
risk budget book of S5.4 the living budgets (risk, participation,

[cost of change](../../glossary.md#cost-of-change) and the caps on crowding of S5.2; the regime
adjustment of S5.5 the scales of exposure and urgency; the entry tickets of S5.1 each strategy's
limits of participation and cadence; the cost surface of S5.8 the costs and impact expected before
trading, calibrated on section 4's [execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) and

the market constraints and the corporate events, [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time).
Monitoring (S6) sends its live alerts: gaps between live and simulated, market anomalies.
Stabilisation (S6.5) may slow the pace of the transition, by control directive. The capital
allocation plan of lifecycle's capital steering (S7.8) brings the calendar of rebalancing: its
frequencies, the cheap windows of the market, and the days and hours to avoid.

The transition plan carries the dated quantities per window, the tempos, the [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail) and the
triggers of execution (conditions to stop or slow down) to S5.7; S5.8 measures the plan against
what was realised. When the capacities or costs observed diverge, the gap goes back to S5.4 and
S5.3 as feedback. Monitoring (S6.1, and S6.2 for [execution fidelity](../../glossary.md#execution-fidelity)) receives the journals of plan
against realised; lifecycle (S7) the policies of re-planning.

**Constraints.**
- Unified references (securities, [venues](../../glossary.md#venue), currencies), normalised timestamps, point-in-time.
- Minimum granularity: one line per window; aggregations by cluster and factor.
- [Idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent): running the same input again never duplicates a
  move.

**Service levels:**
- From targets received to the initial plan: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) configurable, for
  example ≤ 5 min.
- From a major trigger to a new plan: T95 configurable, for example ≤ 2 min.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting): integrated at T90 within a defined window; an
  alert when exceeded.
- Update of the intraday guardrails: for example within the minute, on signals of liquidity and
  volatility.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the target weights are valid, and the budgets active (S5.3, S5.4);
- the estimates of costs and liquidity are fresh;
- the checks of inventory (cash, borrow) and the [hard constraints](../../glossary.md#hard-soft-constraint) pass.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the dated plan is feasible, its constraints are met, and its
  [value at risk of change](../../glossary.md#value-at-risk-of-change) is within budget;
- the journals of decisions are ready for S5.7 and S5.8;
- the thresholds, tempos and guardrails are set, and tested on a sample.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the gap from target, or the [implementation shortfall](../../glossary.md#implementation-shortfall),
  leaves its control bands;
- the [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) of liquidity or volatility changes durably;
- a new market rule changes the capacity or the microstructure;
- the gap between cost analysis and the forecast before trading lasts (reported by S5.8);
- a promising alternative method of scoring or prioritising appears.

Monitoring (S6) receives the thresholds and triggers, the journals of plan against realised, and
the parametric alarms. Lifecycle (S7) receives the cadence of re-planning, the rules to promote or
withdraw a policy of tempo or inaction. The runbooks of stress go to S6.7.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Define the tradable gap and the perimeter of the transition</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** A starting point that is indispensable and traced: any planning on a blur means
over-trading.

**Actions.**
- Freeze a point-in-time picture: positions, orders in flight, borrows and availabilities, active
  constraints and budgets.
- Compute for each line the tradable gap, the target weight minus the current weight, and isolate
  the parts that cannot trade: suspensions, hard limits, no borrow, operational blocks.
- Break the gap down by family of constraints (risk, liquidity, concentration), to see where the
  tensions are.

**KPI.** Completeness of the frozen state at P95 (lines covered, attributes required); alert below
99%, a risk of blind spots.

**Guardrail.** A test of consistency of mass (sum of the weights, cash) and of double counting of
the orders in flight.

**Output.** Map of the tradable gaps (by line, sector, factor), the statuses of blocking, the
tensions by constraint → steps 2, 3 and 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Set the thresholds of materiality: the no-trade zones</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the gaps known, remove the moves that do not pay.

**Actions.**
- Define the thresholds of no action per line and cluster, from volatility, expected costs and the
  noise of the signal.
- Set bounds of trading to the boundary: when a line moves, it goes to a stable boundary, to avoid
  micro round trips.
- Fix the tolerated gap from target during the transition, mean and maximum.

**KPI.** [Turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) avoided against a naive [baseline](../../glossary.md#baseline) that rebalances
everything; objective ≥ X% saved.

**Guardrail.** A test of sensitivity: when small changes of the thresholds reverse too many
decisions, the thresholds are unstable and are calibrated again.

**Output.** The moves eligible today and those deferred, with their bounds → steps 3 and 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Prioritise and window the moves</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Rank only what is worth doing, in the order that creates the most value per unit of cost
and risk.

**Actions.**
- Rank the eligible moves by a score of urgency and value: the size of the gap, the marginal cost
  estimated, the risk that the gap grows, the constraints of the calendar (opens and auctions,
  rolls, events).
- Cut into windows (intraday, day, week) and sequence: first what creates the most net value, or
  frees a constraint.

**KPI.** Rate of completion of the plan against what was executable (the share of the backlog
filled in its window); alert below 85%, a plan too ambitious or badly windowed.

**Guardrail.** Urgency is not importance: a check that small lines at very low cost do not crowd
out large gaps of high value.

**Output.** Backlog prioritised by window, with the reasons of priority → steps 4 and 5, the
decision log (S7.2).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Rules of speed and participation: the tempo</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the quantity and the timing known, set how to consume them over time.

**Actions.**
- Set profiles of participation (a share of activity or of average daily volume), adaptive by line
  and window.
- Define the maximum slopes (ramp-up, ramp-down), the tiers and the pauses allowed, against jolts.
- Frame them with dynamic limits: a wider [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), a thinner depth, extreme volatility or volume
  mean slowing down or stopping.

**KPI.** Budgets of participation respected (zero breaches); smoothness, with no jump above its
threshold.

**Guardrail.** Local [circuit breakers](../../glossary.md#circuit-breaker): stop when the volume
observed falls below the plan, the spread exceeds its quantile of stress, or the instant
[slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) exceeds its threshold.

**Output.** Curves of tempo per move (target participation, minimum and maximum, rules of
adaptation) → step 5, S5.7 (execution).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Compose the path of cost, risk and time: the global plan</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the quantity, the timing and the manner known, optimise the trade-off and validate
the plan's global feasibility.

**Actions.**
- Assemble the prioritised moves and their tempos into a plan over several windows that minimises
  the expected cost while bounding the risk of the gap.
- Compute and watch the value at risk of change per line, cluster and portfolio; keep its
  contribution within the budgets.
- Check the feasibility (liquidity, constraints, cash, borrows) and the stacking of moves: no
  orders colliding.

**KPI.**
- Implementation shortfall of the transition against the forecast before trading: the median gap
  and the tail.
- The mean and maximum gap from target along the path.
- Regret of transition, the loss from slowness minus the loss from impact: close to 0, with the
  right sign.

**Guardrail.** A test of constraints before the plan is issued: no budget (risk, participation,
concentration) above its cap, or the plan is rejected or reduced.

**Output.** The dated transition plan (quantities per window, tempos, guardrails, budgets
consumed) → S5.7 ([execution chain](../../glossary.md#execution-chain)), S5.8 (measure), S5.4 (budgets updated when needed).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Path-aware re-planning: regimes and living targets</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The plan exists; keep it optimal while the world moves.

**Actions.**
- Define the triggers of a new plan: a change of targets, a shock of liquidity or volatility, a
  [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) of costs against the forecast, an idiosyncratic event.
- At each trigger, optimise again the rest of the path only: never insist on an obsolete plan.
- Journal the plan against the realised, the causes of deviation, and the decisions taken:
  continue, slow down, cancel.

**KPI.** Reliability of the plan (gaps between plan and realised); rate of re-plans justified by
a cause; alert when frequent re-plans bring no better [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi), a sign of instability.

**Guardrail.** A safety freeze: the transition stops when the value at risk of change or the
instant costs exceed a set threshold of stress, and resumes only after a new assessment.

**Output.** The loop of re-planning (rules, frequencies, thresholds) and the journals of gaps and
decisions → S5.7 (live adaptation), S5.8 (corrections of cost analysis and simulation), lifecycle
(S7, updates of the policies).

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: formats of input and output, rules of eligibility, scoring, tempos,
  guardrails, triggers of re-planning.
- Log of choices: the reasons for the thresholds, the tolerated gaps from target, the rules of
  priority, the budgets that may be consumed.
- Modules and documentation ready to test: the tradable gap, the filter of materiality and
  inaction, prioritisation and windowing, the generator of tempos, the composer of the plan, the
  loop of re-planning.
- KPI dashboard: turnover avoided, implementation shortfall of the transition, gap from target,
  participation, value at risk of change, reliability of the plan.
- Replacement process: how to replace a policy (thresholds, tempos, prioritisation) through an
  internal comparison against a control, with criteria of acceptance and a [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).
