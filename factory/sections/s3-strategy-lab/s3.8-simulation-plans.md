# S3.8 — Simulation plans & pass criteria (towards section 4)

## TL;DR

Builds a realistic test bench for each candidate strategy, then freezes an objective pass contract, green, orange or red, towards section 4.
Scenarios cover the thesis's critical regimes; replayed execution respects queues, priority, rejections, latencies and partial fills; the cost analysis is coherent and never clairvoyant.
The go / no-go thresholds are simple and public in the factory: they decide without debate.

## Purpose

Build a realistic test bench for each candidate strategy, then freeze an objective pass contract,
green, orange or red, towards section 4: the [simulation plan](../../glossary.md#simulation-plan).

**Success looks like:**
- the scenarios cover the critical [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime) of the thesis;
- replayed execution respects the queue, priority, rejections, latencies and partial fills;
- the [transaction cost analysis](../../glossary.md#transaction-cost-analysis), from forecast to
  realised, is coherent and not clairvoyant;
- the go / no-go thresholds are simple, public in the factory, and decide without debate.

<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The strategy arrives frozen: thesis, objective and constraints from S3.1 and S3.3, signal and risk
modules from S3.2 and S3.4, candidates and [variants](../../glossary.md#variant) from S3.5 and S3.6, and its robustness card from
S3.7. The sealed batches bring [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) data, with synchronised [order books](../../glossary.md#order-book) and prints. The
simulation plan is the handoff package: the plan and its coverage (step B), the ledger of the
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) (C), the cost analysis report (D), the
reading dossier (E) and the contract (A, F), with the optimality and [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) artefacts promised
upstream (primal–dual gap, optimality conditions, influence curves). Section 4 runs it again to
judge, and S3.9 keeps it in the dossier. Monitoring (S6) receives the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) to follow live:
error of the cost analysis, fill rate, time to fill, stability per regime.

**Constraints.**
- One timestamp, in nanoseconds or microseconds depending on the market; time zones frozen.
- An order ledger with: strategy order ID, parent and child, [venue](../../glossary.md#venue), times of sending, of arrival at
  the venue and of fill, quantities requested and filled, price, state, reason, latencies.

**Service levels:**
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting, rules) ingested at T90 = 24 h, [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) = 48 h.
- Plan and scenarios updated by the next day.
- An alert when a level is exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the thesis, objective and constraints are frozen (S3.1, S3.3);
- the modules are ready and the point-in-time data is validated;
- latencies and venues are documented.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the green / orange / red grid is complete;
- the handoff package is signed;
- [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), versions and hashes are frozen.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun steps E and F when:
- monitoring (S6) sees a KPI [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): cost-analysis error up, fill rate down, time to fill up,
  stability broken, [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) down;
- market rules change: matching, priority, tick, lot;
- new data arrives: structural revisions, a wider universe;
- a major new method arrives from the earlier sub-sections of S3; rerunning for anything less would
  let the test results steer the design, a form of [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage).

Monitoring (S6) publishes as its live thresholds the thresholds set here, with the same
definitions. Lifecycle (S7) versions the plan, the simulator runs and the cost analysis; its
[requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) triggers are a KPI exceeded N days in a row, or a critical market event.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>A · Freeze the evaluation contract: the same objectives as upstream</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Align from the start what is optimised (the objective and constraints of S3.3) with what
will be judged here, and fix the green / orange / red grid.

**Actions.**
- Take over the [net-of-cost objective](../../glossary.md#net-of-cost-objective) of S3.3, and no
  other ratio.
- List 4–6 metrics that section 4 will recompute identically: net performance, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage), fill rate,
  time to fill, stability per regime, capacity.
- Define the thresholds: green (all fine), orange (minor gaps that do not change the thesis), red
  (a critical lock).
- Write the pass contract on one page, readable in committee.

**KPI.** [Traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) of the contract: version, hash, signatures; an alert if it changes after the
first runs.

**Guardrail.** One set of metrics: no evaluation on metrics other than those of S3.3. A time freeze:
thresholds are never retouched after execution; otherwise the runs start again.

**Output.** Frozen evaluation contract → step B (plan), step F (reading of the results), section 4
(validation).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>B · Simulation plan: scenarios and stress aligned with the thesis</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Define where, when and how to test: periods, venues, latencies, order types, and the
regimes and stress specific to the thesis.

**Actions.**
- Choose windows that cover calm, volatility, announcements, gaps, wide [spreads](../../glossary.md#spread), thin books, halts
  and suspensions.
- Define venues and latencies: the full round trip, from decision to market to acknowledgement.
- List the order types the thesis allows (limit, market, pegged, iceberg…) and their activation
  rules.
- Create a library of stress tied to the thesis: intraday reversals, fat tails, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity) shocks.
- Build a coverage matrix: scenarios × regimes × assets and venues × sizes.

**KPI.** Regime coverage: ≥ N "calm" and "shock" windows per universe; coverage gap = 0. Realism of
latency: median and percentiles consistent with the historical telemetry.

**Guardrail.** Point-in-time timestamps, with no revised data; balance, with no plan "all calm" or
"all shock"; imposed latency, never an implicit zero.

**Output.** Plan and coverage dossier → step C (execution simulation), step D (cost analysis), step
E (runs), step F (reading).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>C · A replayable execution simulator: queues, priority, rejections, partial fills</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** [Replay](../../glossary.md#replay) execution in the order book as it happens for real.

**Actions.**
- Place each order to the millisecond or microsecond, and apply the matching rules of each venue:
  priority, queue, no favours.
- Simulate partial fills, rejections, cancel-and-replace, expiry, and the effects of the tick.
- Enforce the end-to-end latencies: decision, sending, venue, fill, return.
- Produce a complete order ledger: IDs, timestamps, state, quantity, price, queue position, events.

**KPI.** Determinism: the same seed gives the same fills. Rate of anomalies (impossible fills,
inverted times) = 0.

**Guardrail.** No clairvoyance: no future mid, no next tick. Queue fidelity: strict respect of the
venue's queue, minimum lot and priority. One clock: drift across feeds below a threshold.

**Output.** Order-by-order ledger and fills → step D (cost analysis), step E (analysis), step F
(go / no-go), section 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>D · A coherent cost analysis: from forecast to realised</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Measure the realised costs and confront them with the costs the strategy predicted.

**Actions.**
- Break the costs down: spread, fees, timing slippage, [market impact](../../glossary.md#market-impact)
  (by relative size and participation), latency.
- Use the same aggregations as the thesis: by venue, hour, size, regime.
- Compute the errors, bias and root mean square error, between the predicted (promised) costs and
  the realised (simulated) ones.

**KPI.** Error of the cost analysis (root mean square error, bias) ≤ threshold Y. Fill rate ≥ Z;
time to fill ≤ Zₜ, per bucket.

**Guardrail.** The same clock for the execution simulator and the cost analysis; a non-clairvoyant
model, whose explanatory variables are available at decision time; a sanity check per bucket, with
no suspiciously "perfect" error.

**Output.** Cost analysis report (tables and series) → step E (analysis), step F (contract).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>E · Run, and analyse robustness and capacity</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Run the tests, stabilise the results, and test sensitivity and capacity.

**Actions.**
- Launch with frozen seeds; log the versions (data, venue rules, logic code).
- Read performance from gross to net, the cost analysis, and stability per regime, venue, hour and
  depth.
- Parametric sensitivity: move windows and thresholds within "reasonable" margins.
- [Capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) and footprint: P&L and costs against size, share of average
  daily volume and participation; find the point of collapse.

**KPI.** Net performance (the objective of S3.3) ≥ X, with the gap from gross to net explained.
Stability: sign and order of magnitude kept in ≥ K/K regimes. Capacity: the maximum size before
degradation above threshold C.

**Guardrail.** [Reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility): a global hash of the run. Runs decorrelated: no leakage between
runs. Adverse stress: no incoherent collapse, or the verdict is red.

**Output.** Reading dossier (figures, tables, conclusions) → step F (decision), section 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>F · Read the contract and give the verdict: go or no-go</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Apply the green / orange / red grid of step A, without renegotiation.

**Actions.**
- Fill in the contract sheet: each KPI against its threshold.
- Classify: green (straight to section 4), orange (touch-ups that do not change the thesis), red
  (blocked, back to the earlier sub-sections of section 3).
- Freeze the package: plan, scenarios, seeds, ledger, cost analysis, readings, signed contract.

**KPI.** Decision delay ≤ D, from contract to verdict. Open gaps = 0 before handoff, or listed as
orange.

**Guardrail.** No retouching after the verdict, or step E runs again. Public in the factory: the
same contract for every strategy.

**Output.** Handoff package → section 4 (validation, execution simulation, cost analysis).

</details>

## Deliverables

- A clear specification: the evaluation contract (A), the plan and its coverage (B), the rules of
  venues, latencies and order types.
- Log of choices and decisions: changes, reasons, signatures, versions, seeds, hashes.
- Modules and documentation ready to test: the replayable execution simulator (C), the cost
  analysis (D), the scripts of runs and stress (E).
- KPI tracking table: a dashboard with the KPIs of each step, and alerts.
- A clear replacement process: what to do if the simulator, the cost analysis or the plan breaks —
  who replaces it, how to revalidate, in what delay.
