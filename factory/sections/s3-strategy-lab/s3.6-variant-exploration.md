# S3.6 — Controlled variant exploration & experiment design

## TL;DR

Explores controlled variants around the admitted strategy, never new ideas: a frozen trial contract, a few bounded axes, a small balanced plan of experiments, one identical harness.
Every variant run joins the dossier's trial registry, within a trial budget declared before the first run.
Delivers a clear map of the viable variants and a short-list with its simulation brief, ready for inference (S3.5) and for section 4.

## Purpose

Explore controlled [variants](../../glossary.md#variant) around the admitted strategy, in a space of
trials that is clean, bounded and comparable. The factory does not search for new ideas here: the
strategy's thesis, grammar and optimisation programme are frozen, and only the variants around
them are tried.

The page freezes a trial contract, defines a few axes (structure against settings), builds a
small balanced plan of experiments ([experiment design](../../glossary.md#experiment-design)), runs
it under an identical harness, and maps the viable variants, with a short-list ready for simulation
(section 4). Every trial is appended to the dossier's [trial registry](../../glossary.md#trial-registry),
within the [trial budget](../../glossary.md#trial-budget) declared in step 2: S3 improves, S4 judges.

**Success looks like:** enough coverage without a combinatorial explosion, perfect comparability,
full [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility), and few candidates, but solid ones.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The dossier brings the thesis (falsifiable hypothesis, universe, horizons, conditions of
invalidation); the reference implementation the allowed forms, neutralities and symmetries; the
optimisation package the objectives, constraints, penalties and budgets; the sealed batches the
[point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) datasets, calendars, market events and the reference costs and microstructure. S3.5
receives the raw results, standardised, with the metadata of each trial, for its control of
[multiple testing](../../glossary.md#multiple-testing) and its [shrinkage](../../glossary.md#shrinkage). The variant map holds the comparison table, the sensitivity
map, the short-list and the simulation brief (scenarios to stress, key assumptions) for section 4,
which receives it through [S3.8](s3.8-simulation-plans.md) and the dossier. Monitoring (S6)
receives a pack of [baseline](../../glossary.md#baseline) metrics and control runs, the references for future live gaps.

**Constraints.**
- Formats: a key–value experiment manifest (the recipe), a tabular results registry, timestamps,
  versioned inputs.
- Consistency: the same costs, the same calendars, the same [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), the same metrics.

**Service levels** ("cycles" are the natural units of the target horizon: minutes, hours or days):
- From the generated plan to the first execution: T90 under N market cycles.
- After a critical event (IPO, suspension, changed microstructure): feasibility relabelled at
  [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 2 closing cycles.
- An alert when a level is exceeded, and a priority restart on the control runs.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the thesis is frozen, the grammar and [invariances](../../glossary.md#invariance) are explicit, and the objective and constraints
  are defined;
- the point-in-time data is validated, the reference cost model is fixed and the metric packs are
  approved.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the experiment registry is complete and reproducible, and every trial is in the dossier's trial
  registry;
- the comparison table is signed, and the short-list and simulation brief are delivered;
- the sensitivity map is published;
- the feedback to S2, S3 and S4 is written down.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- monitoring detects [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): a gap between live and control beyond its threshold;
- a new [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) or new market rules appear, or costs or latencies change significantly;
- a new module of S2 changes the grammar;
- the universe is extended, or the horizon changes.

Monitoring (S6) takes the control runs and metrics as its live references; they raise the drift
flags. Lifecycle (S7) receives the rules for a targeted rerun: which axes to sweep again first.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>0 · Quick gate: ready for variants</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Avoid exploring on shifting sand.

**Actions.**
- Check that the thesis (S3.1), the grammar and invariances (S3.2) and the optimisation programme
  (S3.3) are frozen at the granularity needed: objective, constraints, universe, horizons, costs,
  neutralities.

**Output.** One-page alignment sheet → step 1.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Freeze the trial template, the trial contract</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Guarantee that every variant can be compared with every other.

**Actions.**
- Write a frozen template per strategy:
  - objectives and their derivatives (primary, secondary, penalties);
  - constraints and budgets (exposure, [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover), costs, latencies);
  - metric packs (performance, stability, sensitivity);
  - universe and sampling (point-in-time, cuts by regime);
  - random seeds and stopping rules (reproducibility, early stop);
  - an identical execution harness: the same data, the same costs, the same rules.

**KPI.** Share of the contract's elements made explicit: 100%; alert below 100%.

**Guardrail.** A no-leakage check (point-in-time data, no look-ahead) and a dry reproducibility test
(the same seed gives the same outputs).

**Output.** Signed trial contract v1.0 → steps 2 to 4: everything else conforms to it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Define the space of variants, factorised</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Separate structure (topology, logic) from settings (windows, thresholds…), around the
admitted strategy only.

**Actions.**
- List 2–3 structural axes that make a real difference, among the alternatives the dossier and S3.2
  allow: aggregation schemes, gating against committee, order of the blocks.
- List 2–4 reasonable axes of settings: windows, thresholds, coefficients, simplified cost models.
- Fix bounds and typical values in advance (feasibility rules).
- Allocate a trial budget (compute and time) and a share per axis, against overindulgence.

**KPI.** A balanced ratio of structure to settings, for example 50/50 ± 20%.

**Guardrail.** A veto on any point outside the bounds; a review, for each structural axis, that
the invariances are respected.

**Output.** Grid of axes and bounds, and the trial budget → step 3 (construction of the plan).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Build a compact plan of experiments</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Cover widely, without a combinatorial explosion.

**Actions.**
- Choose a factorised design suited to the budget: full, fractional, orthogonal, or space-filling
  sampling.
- Stratify by regime or universe where needed.
- Randomise the order of execution (against calendar effects) and set aside control blocks (fixed
  references).
- Define early-stopping rules: cut the non-viable variants early.

**KPI.** Coverage: ≥ 80% of the "essential" combinations the grid plans.

**Guardrail.** The trial cap is respected; control runs make up ≥ 10% of the plan, to anchor the
comparisons.

**Output.** Matrix of trials (the list of variants), order of execution and stopping rules →
step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Run under an identical harness, and log</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Produce results that are clean and traceable.

**Actions.**
- Run the variants with the same harness: data, costs, metrics, seeds.
- Apply the early stops according to the rules, and tag the reasons for stopping.
- Log the complete recipe of each run (axes, values, timestamp, seed, version of the inputs) in the
  experiment registry and in the dossier's trial registry.

**KPI.** Reproducibility: 100% on a sub-sample run again.

**Guardrail.** An alarm if a single external parameter differs (hash of the artefacts); a rate of
technical failures below 1%.

**Output.** Experiment registry (ID → recipe → raw results) → step 5 (analysis and reading), S3.5
(inference).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Compare under constant rules, and read the stability</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Decide what really holds.

**Actions.**
- Assess each variant by the metric packs of the contract: level, stability, sensitivity, costs.
- Compare the rankings between sub-periods and regimes; measure the local sensitivities (change of
  performance per change of parameter).
- Set aside the dominated or fragile variants; isolate the frontier of the performing and stable
  ones.

**KPI.** Stability of the rankings: correlation across windows and regimes ≥ threshold.

**Guardrail.** A "no mass inversion" test: above X% of inversions, sweep the axes again or revise the
contract; an alert on extreme sensitivity (a break at ± one notch).

**Output.** Comparison table, stability analysis and a candidate short-list → step 6, S3.5
(large-scale inference).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Map the variants and prepare the handoff</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Keep what was learnt about the admitted strategy, and prepare what follows.

**Actions.**
- Produce a map of the variants: where the strategy holds, and where it breaks.
- Write a targeted simulation brief: scenarios, latencies, [venues](../../glossary.md#venue), order rules to stress, costly
  assumptions.
- Issue recommendations upstream (invariances to strengthen, modules to request from S2) and
  downstream (tests for S4).

**KPI.** Rate of passage to section 4, targeted: for example 5–15%.

**Guardrail.** At 0% or above 30%, a diagnosis: either the space is badly framed (too strict or too
broad), or the contract and the metrics need revising.

**Output.** Sensitivity map, final short-list, simulation and cost brief, feedback to S2, S3 and S4 →
the [variant map](../../glossary.md#variant-map) for S3.8 and the dossier, S3.5 (inference), S6
and S7 (monitoring and lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Trial contract: objectives, constraints, metrics, universe, costs, seeds, stopping rules.
- Grid of axes and bounds (structure against settings) and the trial budget.
- Plan of experiments: matrix of trials, control runs, order of execution, stratifications.
- Versioned experiment registry (ID → recipe → results → artefacts), mirrored in the dossier's trial
  registry.
- Comparison table and analysis of stability and sensitivity.
- Short-list of candidates and the simulation and cost brief (scenarios to stress, key assumptions).
- Map of the variants, and the feedback to S2, S3 and S4.
- [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) dashboard: coverage, reproducibility, stability, rate of passage, budget.
- Replacement process: how to reopen the space, on which axes, with which control runs.
