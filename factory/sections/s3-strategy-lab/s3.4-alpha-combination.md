# S3.4 — Alpha combination & internal allocation

## TL;DR

Turns the strategy's alphas into one target position per asset, at a steady cadence: no double counting, faithful to the thesis, costs held, real diversification.
Alphas are grouped into families with budgets, weighted by quality and by what each adds alone, bounded by guardrails, then converted into positions under risk and cost budgets.
It is also where the strategy is first seen within the whole: its redundancy is measured against the strategies already running.

## Purpose

Produce, at a regular cadence, one position size per asset from a set of heterogeneous
[alphas](../../glossary.md#alpha): without double counting, faithful to the thesis, with costs held
and a real diversification. This is the strategy's [alpha combination](../../glossary.md#alpha-combination),
recorded as its [combination rule](../../glossary.md#combination-rule).

It is also where the strategy is seen within the whole: how it interacts with the strategies
already running. The portfolio view itself belongs to S5.2; here, the strategy's redundancy and
diversification are measured against it.

**Success looks like:** traceable aggregation rules, stable when the data moves slightly, that
deliver positions ready for validation (S4), then for execution (S5).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The alphas arrive as normalised scores (with timestamps, horizons, latencies and signs) through the
reference implementation and the feature registry. The universe and eligibility (inclusions,
exclusions, suspensions, IPOs, delistings), the budgets and constraints (overall risk, caps per
family or asset, [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) budget), the risk and cost estimates and the [neutralisation](../../glossary.md#neutralisation) rules come
from S3.1 and S3.3. The interaction map of S5.2 shows the strategies already running. The
combination rule holds the target positions per asset (with their limits and time of application),
the target orders (position deltas) with their relative priority, the breakdown by family
(contributions to risk and alpha, exposures) and the [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) artefacts (log, versions, "what
changed"). Sections 4 and 5 receive it through the dossier of [S3.9](s3.9-traceability-rationale.md).

**Constraints.**
- Everything is [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time), time-stamped and replayable.
- Scale conventions documented (z-scores, quantiles or other), and fixed.
- Neutralisations aligned with the thesis: no neutralisation "by reflex".

**Service levels** (whatever the cadence):
- Update latency, from new scores to a new target position: ≤ 10% of the decision cadence.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting, change of eligibility) propagated into the positions
  at T90 ≤ 10% and [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 20% of the cadence.
- An alert when a level is exceeded, and a switch to a "safe" mode: sizes on the affected assets
  are reduced automatically.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the thesis, objectives and constraints are validated (S3.1 to S3.3);
- the alpha scores are normalised, with [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) quality metrics available;
- the cost and risk budgets are set, and the neutralisation rules are clear.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- target positions are generated over a representative history, with [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) green: diversification
  above its threshold, turnover ≤ budget, unwanted exposures below their threshold;
- the aggregation dossier is complete: families, weights, [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail), "what changed", versions;
- it is ready for execution simulation and cost analysis (S4): every interface is filled.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- the correlations between alphas or families change structurally;
- costs overrun the budget persistently, or turnover leaves its band;
- alphas are added or removed, or new markets or rules arrive;
- monitoring (S6) sees a KPI [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) beyond its threshold, or a postmortem (S7) calls for it.

Monitoring (S6) receives the KPIs continuously, the gaps between live and simulated, and the daily
explanations. Lifecycle (S7) receives the decision log, the update rules (who, when, what to test)
and the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) checklist.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Map the alphas and their footprints</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Understand who speaks, about what, at what rhythm, and at what cost.

**Actions.**
- Draw up the catalogue of alphas with minimal metadata: horizon and latency, universe covered,
  direction of the signal, expected sign, stability, sensitivity to costs, and the conditions in
  which they fall silent.
- Bring the scores to a common scale: centred and scaled over time, consistently with the thesis.
- Make sure everything is point-in-time (no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage)).

**KPI.** Coverage of the universe (≥ X%); dispersion of the scores (no "crushed" scale); effective
latency against the horizon.

**Guardrail.** Point-in-time tests (zero leakage), consistency of sign and scale; an alert when
latency exceeds 20% of the decision cadence.

**Output.** Normalised alpha book (a panel of time × assets × alphas, with its metadata) → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Form families and remove hidden duplicates</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Avoid double counting, and prepare caps per group.

**Actions.**
- Measure the proximity between alphas (correlations conditioned on [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) and horizon), and
  between the strategy and those already running (the interaction map of S5.2).
- Assign each alpha to a family (trend, contrarian, carry, structural…) and document the overlaps.
- Fix budgets per family: caps on weight and on risk.

**KPI.** Residual redundancy within families (median correlation ≤ threshold); the
[effective number of bets](../../glossary.md#effective-number-of-bets) per family and overall.

**Guardrail.** An alert when an alpha changes family more than N% of the time (instability); a stop
when correlations explode under stress (a regime badly handled).

**Output.** Proximity matrix, table from alpha to family, and group budgets → step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Define the vote and the base weights</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Give each alpha a starting weight according to its quality, and to how it differs from
the others.

**Actions.**
- Assess the out-of-sample quality: regularity, [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness), net cost,
  [drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown).
- Measure what each alpha adds alone: its contribution to diversification beyond its family,
  and beyond the strategies already running.
- Set a weighted vote, with caps per family and a gentle regularisation of small weights
  ([shrinkage](../../glossary.md#shrinkage), soft thresholding).

**KPI.** Stability of the weights (sensitivity to windows and regimes); share of weight on the top
alpha (no dictator); diversification gain against the best single alpha.

**Guardrail.** Internal resampling: an alert when more than K% of the weights flip sign as the
window moves slightly.

**Output.** Matrix of base weights, per alpha and per family, documented → step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Apply the structural guardrails</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn the base weights into usable weights that conform to the thesis.

**Actions.**
- Set very small convictions to zero (soft thresholds), and smooth the extremes.
- [Neutralise](../../glossary.md#neutralisation) the unwanted exposures (for example an unwanted
  overall beta, certain sectors or styles), at the level of the aggregated portfolio.
- Respect the caps per family and per asset.

**KPI.** Residual unwanted exposures, falling below a threshold; a reasonable thresholding rate (the
share of alphas set to zero); caps per family respected.

**Guardrail.** Exposure [circuit breakers](../../glossary.md#circuit-breaker): on a breach,
regularise again or reduce the sizes.

**Output.** Regularised weights per alpha, and one aggregated score per asset → step 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Convert score to position under risk and cost budgets</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Obtain target positions that can be steered and executed.

**Actions.**
- Translate the aggregated score into a unit size, respecting the risk budget, the limits per asset
  and family, and the [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) budget.
- Impose a pace of change (smoothing, step-in and step-out rules) to tame costs.
- Produce a portfolio path at each decision step, and the target orders (position deltas).

**KPI.** Turnover per unit of risk or alpha; expected [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) against budget; clipping rate (the
share of positions stuck at limits).

**Guardrail.** Automatic smoothing when turnover exceeds its budget; a partial freeze when the
estimated cost exceeds its cap; an alert when more than Y% of the portfolio touches the limits.

**Output.** Vector of target positions, operational limits and target orders → S4 (validation,
execution simulation, cost analysis), S5 (decision and execution).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Self-audit and packaging: 30-second traceability</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Make the aggregator auditable, and ready to pass the downstream controls.

**Actions.**
- Compute the combination KPIs: diversification, stability, costs, effective number of bets, and so
  on.
- Generate a "what changed": why a given asset goes up or down today.
- Package the specification, versions, parameters, budgets and decision log.

**KPI.** 30-second traceability (an explanation of the top-10 positions); the gap between live and
simulated (S6); a rate of red alerts below a threshold.

**Guardrail.** Two consecutive days of red alerts trigger an automatic requalification.

**Output.** Versioned aggregation dossier, KPI dashboard and daily explainers → S4, S5, S6, S7.

</details>

## Deliverables

- Aggregation specification (one page): families, budgets, vote, shrinkage, neutralisations,
  conversion from score to position, pace of change, limits.
- Normalised alpha book and its metadata: a living catalogue.
- Weight matrices (base and regularised) and caps per family and asset.
- Target positions and target orders (standard formats), and the portfolio path.
- KPI dashboard: diversification, stability, costs, effective number of bets, exposures, turnover.
- Log of choices and versions (parameters, dates, reasons) and the daily "what changed".
- A clear replacement process: the protocol to remove or add an alpha, reallocate a budget,
  requalify the families and deliver again safely.
