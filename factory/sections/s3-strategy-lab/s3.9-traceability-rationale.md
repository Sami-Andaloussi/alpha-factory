# S3.9 — Traceability & design rationale: the living strategy dossier

## TL;DR

Keeps one living, readable and replayable dossier per strategy: the lab's dossier itself, enriched by every sub-section of S3.
It links the starting idea, the design choices, the experiments with their trial registry, the evidence and the decision: a cold reader understands it in hours, a downstream team replays it exactly.
It is the contract from which validation, execution, monitoring and lifecycle work.

## Purpose

Keep, for each strategy, a living, readable and replayable dossier that links the starting idea,
the design choices, the experiments, the evidence and the decision. It is the direct continuation
of the lab's [strategy dossier](../../glossary.md#strategy-dossier): the same dossier, enriched by
every sub-section of S3, with its [trial registry](../../glossary.md#trial-registry) complete
(every trial of the lab and of the factory) and its [design rationale](../../glossary.md#design-rationale).

**Success looks like:**
- a reader who comes to it cold understands the logic in a few hours;
- a downstream team [replays](../../glossary.md#replay) the results exactly;
- the dossier serves as a clear contract for validation, execution, monitoring and lifecycle.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The dossier arrives admitted from S3.1 and gathers what S3.2 to S3.8 produce; the trial registry
gathers the trials of S3.3, S3.5 and S3.6 with those of the lab. The feature registry brings the
specifications of modules, formats and versions of the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) datasets; the batch registry
gives stable references to the data (extraction date, window, universe), in fixed units and
calendars. Section 4 receives the pass contract, the execution manifest, the scenarios and
thresholds, and the trial registry, for which S4.2 and S4.3 correct their statistics. Section 5
receives the objectives and constraints, the latencies and tolerances, and the expected readings.
Section 6 receives the mapping between simulated and live metrics, and the recipes of the sentinel
figures. Section 7 receives the versions, the changelog and the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) triggers.

**Constraints.**
- Format: a human document plus machine-readable artefacts, with reproducible IDs and signatures.

**Service levels:**
- Dossier updated after a change of strategy: T90 ≤ 4 h / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 8 h.
- Transfer kit published after a freeze: T90 ≤ 24 h / T95 ≤ 36 h.
- Critical market events (IPO, suspension, delisting, change of rules) reflected in the dossier:
  T90 ≤ 1 business day / T95 ≤ 2.
- An alert when a level is exceeded, recorded in the changelog.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the skeleton is validated (step 1), the thesis is falsifiable (step 2), and the architecture and
  interfaces are fixed (step 3);
- the data is referenced point-in-time, and the modules are available in section 2.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- 100% of the figures are reproducible (step 5);
- 100% of the sections are complete;
- freshness is within its service level (step 7);
- the pass contract is signed and the decision traced (step 6).

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- monitoring sees a [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): a gap between live and simulated beyond the agreed threshold;
- the market changes: microstructure, rules, universe;
- an upstream module changes significantly;
- a competing method brings a gain in [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) or simplicity;
- the periodic formal review comes round (quarterly by default), with the sentinel runs replayed.

Monitoring (S6) receives the causes of gaps, classified ([alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha), beta, costs, latency), with
references to the evidence. Lifecycle (S7) receives the rules of [sunset](../../glossary.md#sunset) and upgrade (when to
retire or reinforce), with objective triggers.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Frame and identities: the skeleton of the dossier</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Set the common grammar before any content, to avoid ghost versions.

**Actions.**
- Define the canonical structure of the dossier: mandatory sections, order, format (text plus
  executable artefacts). The fields of the [handoff contract](../../../handoff/README.md) come
  first.
- Define the IDs (strategy ID, version ID, experiment ID) and the rules of naming, dating and
  signature (the [fingerprint](../../glossary.md#fingerprint) of inputs and outputs).
- Specify the minimal execution manifest: references to point-in-time data, parameters, random
  [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), windows, metrics.

**KPI.** Conformity of form: the share of dossiers respecting 100% of the sections; alert below
100%. Drift to watch: an inflation of [variants](../../glossary.md#variant) outside the frame.

**Guardrail.** Against parallel versions: a test that every ID is unique, and a signature of the
manifest.

**Output.** Template, conventions and model manifest → every following step, and sections 4 to 7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Thesis and alternatives: a testable intent</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Fill the skeleton with its intellectual compass; otherwise everything else floats.

**Actions.**
- State the [falsifiable thesis](../../glossary.md#falsifiable-thesis), as S3.1 restated it:
  universe, horizon, expected mechanism, contexts of invalidation.
- List 2–3 credible alternatives (paths B and C) and the conditions in which each is preferred.
- Define the measurable objective (gain, risk, costs) and the constraints (exposures, [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover),
  budgets).

**KPI.** Falsifiability: explicit conditions of invalidation are present; alert when they are
missing. Drift to watch: untestable theses.

**Guardrail.** Against vague objectives: a cross review asks whether a contrary test can be built
from the text.

**Output.** Thesis note (1–2 pages) and an invalidation checklist → step 3 (design), step 4
(experiments), S4 (simulation plan).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Architecture and the rules of the game: a traced design</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Translate the thesis into an architecture that can be handled.

**Actions.**
- Describe the decision chain: inputs, modules used, assembly logic (sequence, gating, votes),
  outputs.
- Fix the interfaces and contracts between blocks (formats, units, timings) and the target
  [invariances](../../glossary.md#invariance) (scale, time translation, neutralities).
- Set the optimisation programme at the right level (objective, constraints, parsimony) and the
  reading metrics, beyond a single ratio.

**KPI.** Clarity of interfaces: zero ambiguity (formats, units, latencies) found by a third-party
reader; alert at one or more. Drift to watch: local tinkering that cannot be replayed.

**Guardrail.** Against [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage), or data that is not point-in-time: a point-in-time reread and a
check of the consistency of units and time.

**Output.** Architecture schema, interface specifications and rules of the game → step 4 (plan of
experiments), S5 (execution), S4 (execution simulation, cost analysis).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Plan of experiments and log: what is tested, and how</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The design dictates which factors vary, and which stay fixed.

**Actions.**
- Define a bounded exploration plan: structural variants against settings, market scenarios,
  windows, cost schemes.
- Keep the experiment log, the trial registry: one row per run (experiment ID), with
  hyperparameters, data, timestamp, result, comment.
- Plan the control of [multiple testing](../../glossary.md#multiple-testing) and the [calibration](../../glossary.md#calibration) of effects (reading scores as expected
  effects), at the level of protocol, with no tool imposed.

**KPI.** Coverage of experimentation: the share of key factors tested against the plan; alert below
90%. Drift to watch: cherry-picking.

**Guardrail.** Against uncontrolled multiple testing: a protocol with pre-committed thresholds and
a correction for multiplicity (a generic family, no tool imposed).

**Output.** Experiment table, analysis protocol and stopping rules → step 5 (replayable evidence),
S4 (pass criteria).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Replayable evidence: evidence with its recipes</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Crystallise the results and their [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability).

**Actions.**
- For each key figure or table, provide the complete recipe: input manifest, parameters,
  step-by-step instructions to regenerate it.
- Produce the evidence sheet of each variant: level of evidence, uncertainty, sensitivity,
  robustness (dirty data, [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime)).
- Link each intermediate decision to its evidence: ID, date, link.

**KPI.** [Reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility): 100% of the figures replayable bit for
bit from their recipe; alert below 100%. Drift to watch: orphan figures.

**Guardrail.** Against hidden dependencies: regeneration in a clean environment, from the complete
manifest.

**Output.** "Figures with recipes" dossier and evidence sheets per variant → step 6 (decision), S6
(attribution of gaps), audits.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Pass contract and decision: go, stand-by or abandon</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Decide on the evidence.

**Actions.**
- Write the pass contract with section 4: what to simulate, the assumptions to respect, the go /
  no-go thresholds, the stress sets.
- Record the decision (go, stand-by or abandon) and its reasons, citing the evidence by experiment
  ID.
- List the actions required: if go, the kit for execution simulation and cost analysis; if
  stand-by, the missing data or tests; if abandon, a useful postmortem.

**KPI.** Traceability of decisions: 100% of choices linked to at least one piece of evidence (ID and
date); alert below 100%. Drift to watch: decisions by opinion.

**Guardrail.** Against moving thresholds: the criteria are frozen before the results are read (a
pre-commitment).

**Output.** Signed decision note, transfer kit and checklist of requirements → S4 (validation,
execution simulation, cost analysis), S5 (decision and execution), S6, S7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Freshness and synchronisation: alive every day</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Keep the dossier in step with reality: changes of modules, data, rules.

**Actions.**
- Set up the automatic update of the dossier after any structural change: data, modules, logic,
  objectives.
- Keep the changelog (who, when, what, why) and the KPI table.
- Define a cycle of periodic review (see the requalification) and the drift alerts.

**KPI.** Freshness: the delay from a merge to the update of the dossier; objective T90 ≤ 4 h,
T95 ≤ 8 h. Drift to watch: documentation running late.

**Guardrail.** Against silent drift: an alert when the level is exceeded, and a
[sentinel backtest](../../glossary.md#sentinel-backtest) run again.

**Output.** Changelog, KPI table, alarms and review calendar → S6 (live against simulated), S7
(lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Dossier template, charter of IDs, and signatures.
- Thesis note (with its alternatives) and the invalidation checklist.
- Architecture schema, interface specifications and rules of the game (objective, constraints,
  parsimony).
- Plan of experiments and the log of runs: a structured table, by experiment ID.
- Replayable figures with complete recipes, and evidence sheets per variant.
- Pass contract with section 4, decision note (go, stand-by, abandon) and its checklist.
- Changelog, KPI table (freshness, completeness, reproducibility, traceability), requalification
  process and replacement plan.
