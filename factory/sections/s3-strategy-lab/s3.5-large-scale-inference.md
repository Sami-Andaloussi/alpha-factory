# S3.5 — Large-scale inference (calibration, shrinkage, tests, multiplicity)

## TL;DR

Turns hundreds of variants into a small batch of credible candidates, comparable with one another and ready for section 4.
Learns what "no signal" looks like in the factory's own data, cools over-optimistic scores, tests each idea against its relevant alternative, and caps the share of false discoveries.
The count of trials includes every trial of the lab and of the factory, as the dossier's trial registry records them.

## Purpose

Turn hundreds of [variants](../../glossary.md#variant) into a small batch of credible candidates, comparable with one another
and ready for section 4. In practice: learn what "zero signal" means in the factory's data (an
[empirical null](../../glossary.md#empirical-null)); cool the scores that are too optimistic
([shrinkage](../../glossary.md#shrinkage)); test each idea against its relevant alternative; and
control [multiple testing](../../glossary.md#multiple-testing), so that the share of false findings
among the discoveries stays below a chosen threshold (the
[false discovery rate](../../glossary.md#false-discovery-rate)).

The count of trials includes the lab's: every variant in the dossier's
[trial registry](../../glossary.md#trial-registry), from the lab, from S3.3 and from S3.6, counts,
and every variant assessed here joins it. S3 improves; S4 judges.

**Success looks like:** few but true, with calibrated expected effects, an honest uncertainty and
full [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The variants arrive standardised, with their metadata, through the trial registry of S3.3 and S3.6; the
theses, universes, horizons, assumed costs and optimisation constraints through the dossier, the
reference implementation and the optimisation package; [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) market data with [regime](../../glossary.md#regime)
markers, calendars and events through the sealed batches and the feature registry. The candidate
package holds the empirical null profile, the table of expected effects, the evidence table, the
short-list under control of false findings, and one standard package per candidate: net effect,
uncertainty, risk of being false, [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) flags, conditions of use. Section 4 receives it
through the dossier of [S3.9](s3.9-traceability-rationale.md), in formats ready for execution
simulation and cost analysis.

**Constraints.**
- Granularity: one row is one variant × one context (universe, horizon, regime).
- Mandatory fields: IDs, common measure, expected effect, uncertainty, shrinkage penalty,
  indicators of evidence, group or family, condition tags.
- Traceability: version of the data, period, windows, exclusions, checksum of the computations.
- No [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage): the data used to select and the data used to re-estimate are strictly separate.
- Compatible with section 4: plug-and-play formats (net effects, uncertainties, activation
  conditions).

**Service levels:**
- Batch: 1,000 variants → a complete inference cycle ≤ 2 h at T90, ≤ 3 h at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95).
- Incremental: up to 50 variants added → a differential update ≤ 30 min at T90, ≤ 45 min at T95.
- Critical events that affect the null (IPO, suspension, delisting, change of microstructure):
  recalibration triggered and published by the next business day, at T95.
- Alerts to monitoring (S6) when a level is exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the theses are formalised: hypotheses, alternatives, conditions;
- the common measure is validated (step 1);
- the point-in-time data is locked and the assumed costs are documented.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- a candidate package v1 is available for each discovery: expected effect after shrinkage and after
  selection, uncertainty, risk of being false (global and local), robustness flags, complete logs;
- the target false discovery rate is respected on the short-list;
- [calibration](../../glossary.md#calibration) [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) are at or below their thresholds.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- the calibration [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) drift, post-selection bias rises, or the rate of confirmations in S4 falls;
- market rules change, or the universe or the regime changes materially;
- a promising new method of shrinkage or testing appears;
- the minimum frequency comes round: monthly for the null, quarterly for the full pipeline, or on
  an event (IPO, delisting, stress).

Monitoring (S6) receives KPI dashboards (calibration, shrinkage, false discovery rate, stability),
alarms and logs. Lifecycle (S7) receives the rules of rotation and retirement of variants, the
criteria for taking in new variants faster, and the recalibration policy.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Define the unit of evidence and the common measure</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Set the yardstick that makes all variants comparable: pivoted score, unit effect,
uncertainty.

**Actions.**
- Define the unit of measure (effect per trade, per event, per unit of risk) and the window of
  measurement, with a horizon consistent with the thesis.
- Specify the evidence score (a centred statistic on a stabilised scale) and its uncertainty
  interval.
- Fix the minimum metadata per variant: universe, horizon, assumed costs, constraints of S3.3.

**KPI.** Metadata coverage: 100% of the mandatory fields. Rate of schema validation failures ≤ 1%.

**Guardrail.** Against inconsistent units: tests of scale homogeneity on neighbouring variants,
blocking on failure.

**Output.** "Common measure v1" specification and data schema → steps 2 to 6, S4, S5, S6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Calibrate the empirical null: what zero looks like</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Learn, in the factory's own data, the statistical signature of "no [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha)".

**Actions.**
- Sample suitably: representative periods and regimes, free of leakage.
- Estimate the background distribution of the scores when there is no signal: the empirical null.
- Produce calibration diagnostics: systematic gaps, asymmetries, tails.

**KPI.** Calibration score: the gap between observed and expected quantiles ≤ threshold. Stability
across regimes: variation ≤ threshold.

**Guardrail.** Against an unrealistic theoretical null: quantile–quantile diagnostics and tests of
symmetry and tails; on drift, resample by regime.

**Output.** Empirical null profile v1 and its diagnostic reports → step 3 (shrinkage), step 4
(tests).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Estimate the expected effect by shrinkage: an honest contraction</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Convert each raw score into a "cooled" expected effect, with a realistic uncertainty.

**Actions.**
- Define a contraction rule: weak evidence is pulled towards zero, strong evidence is penalised
  less.
- Produce, per variant: expected effect, uncertainty interval, shrinkage penalty (how much it was
  cooled).
- Log the assumptions: local [stationarity](../../glossary.md#stationarity), stability per regime.

**KPI.** Mean shrinkage ratio: it must cool without crushing, with a target such as 20–60%
depending on uncertainty. Error between predicted and realised, in retrospect: absolute gap ≤
threshold.

**Guardrail.** Against shrinkage that is too brutal or too weak: bias–variance curves and
time-ordered [cross-validation](../../glossary.md#cross-validation), then adjust the rule.

**Output.** Table of expected effects v1 (effect, uncertainty, shrinkage ratio) → steps 4 to 6,
S4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Test against the thesis: the relevant alternative</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Judge each variant against the alternative that matches its alpha mechanics: direction,
timing, activation conditions.

**Actions.**
- For each thesis, formulate the operational alternative: one-sided or two-sided, conditional, with
  a threshold.
- Compute a measure of evidence consistent with the learnt null: an evidence value, a local score
  of falseness.
- Consolidate by the condition frames (regimes, universes) declared in step 1.

**KPI.** Alignment between thesis and test: 100%. Simulated
[statistical power](../../glossary.md#statistical-power) under the operational alternative: at or
above its target.

**Guardrail.** Against a badly specified test: placebo controls and sign inversions; a positive
result on a placebo invalidates the test.

**Output.** Evidence table v1 (expected effect, evidence, conditions) → step 5, S4 (priority of
tests).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Control multiple testing, dependence included, and select</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Declare a set of discoveries while holding the targeted share of false ones, even when
the variants are correlated.

**Actions.**
- Fix a threshold of false findings, the target false discovery rate, overall and, where relevant,
  per family (groups of cousin variants). The count covers every trial in the dossier's trial
  registry, the lab's included.
- Apply a selection rule that tolerates dependence: groups, hierarchy, overlaps.
- Produce the list of discoveries, the estimate of expected false ones, and the alternative
  versions near the threshold (a waiting list).

**KPI.** False discovery rate realised in a controlled [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest) ≤ target + margin. Sensitivity to
dependence: the gap in selection between groupings ≤ threshold.

**Guardrail.** Against an explosion of false positives: relabel by families and analyse the
correlations; raise the threshold or regroup.

**Output.** Short-list v1 (discoveries, estimated false discovery rate, ranks) and the waiting list
at the threshold → step 6, S4 ([simulation plans](../../glossary.md#simulation-plan)).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Remove post-selection bias and finalise the candidate package</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Separate selection from measurement: re-estimate the effect of the discoveries without
recycling information, against [selection bias](../../glossary.md#selection-bias).

**Actions.**
- Re-measure the expected effects on a support not used for selection, or through a dedicated
  correction.
- Stress the stability (other periods, sub-universes, a time bootstrap) and rank by robustness.
- Standardise the package per strategy: net expected effect (after shrinkage and debiasing),
  uncertainty, risk of being false (global and local), robustness flags, notes on conditions.

**KPI.** Residual selection bias: the difference before and after debiasing ≤ threshold. Stability
of the top-k: ≥ x% kept across resamples. Rate of confirmations in S4, tracked with a lag.

**Guardrail.** Against leakage of information: strict separation of the supports and access logs;
blocked when a reuse is detected.

**Output.** [Candidate package](../../glossary.md#candidate-package) v1, in the format of section
4, and a complete log → S4 (execution simulation, cost analysis), S6 (monitoring), S7 (lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- "Common measure v1" specification: units, window, data schema, validations.
- Empirical null profile v1: estimate, diagnostics per regime, known limits.
- Table of expected effects v1: effect, uncertainty, shrinkage ratio, assumptions.
- Evidence table v1: a measure of evidence aligned with the thesis, activation conditions.
- Short-list v1 with its target false discovery rate and the estimate of expected false ones
  (overall and per family).
- Candidate packages v1 (format of section 4), with robustness notes and the decision log.
- KPI dashboard per step, with alert and [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) rules.
- Replacement process: how to retrain the null, adjust the shrinkage and revise the multiplicity
  thresholds without breaking the interfaces.
