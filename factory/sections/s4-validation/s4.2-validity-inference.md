# S4.2 — Validity inference & control of statistical illusions

## TL;DR

Gives each candidate a probabilistic verdict on whether its edge is real, repeatable and useful, after costs.
A pass needs a probability above the threshold of at least 95%, deflated for the number of trials at a false discovery rate of at most 10%, and a power of at least 80%.
The validity card feeds selection (S4.3), execution simulation and cost analysis (S4.6, S4.7) and the promotion gates (S4.10).

## Purpose

Give each candidate (a signal, a module, a strategy) a probabilistic verdict on whether its edge
is real, repeatable and useful. A fine [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest) becomes an identity
card of validity, the [validity card](../../glossary.md#validity-card): the size of the effect
after costs, its uncertainty, the probability that it beats a minimum credible threshold, and a
correction for [multiple testing](../../glossary.md#multiple-testing).

The count of trials is the whole [trial registry](../../glossary.md#trial-registry): the lab's
trials and section 3's, from the dossier, and those of S4.1.

**Success looks like:** a pass, retry or fail verdict, argued, traceable and replicable, that
feeds selection (S4.3), simulated execution and cost analysis (S4.6, S4.7) and the
[promotion gates](../../glossary.md#promotion-gate) (S4.10).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The out-of-sample pack brings the series per [fold](../../glossary.md#fold) (net or gross returns or P&L, with the cost
module used), the calendar of aggregation, the universe and the locked rules, with the metadata of
the experiments: [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), splits, dates of freeze, versions of [features](../../glossary.md#feature) and [labels](../../glossary.md#label). The trial
registry arrives from the dossier and from S4.1; this page consolidates and signs it, and S4.3
receives the signed version. The validity card gives S4.3 its flags of eligibility, and the
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) and cost analysis their priorities. Monitoring receives a [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) watchlist:
the metrics to watch, their alert thresholds and windows (S6.4 for drift). Lifecycle (S7) receives
the plan to extend the sample (data to collect, duration required) and the rules of re-test.

**Constraints.**
- Format: timestamped series, a declared time zone, one convention of aggregation, and metadata
  in a readable, structured form.
- Consistency: one scheme of annualisation and costs per evaluation; strict [versioning](../../glossary.md#versioning).

**Service levels:**
- A complete rerun of the inference within the next day, at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95), after new [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) data
  arrives; an alert when exceeded.
- Critical events (a change of market rules, a suspension, mass relistings): the validity pipeline
  re-executed within 48 h, at T95.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the anti-leakage protocol is validated (S4.1);
- the out-of-sample pack is complete;
- the trial registry is signed;
- the target effect is defined, net of costs;
- the base assumptions of execution are aligned (reference costs).

**Exit.**
- *Pass* when the probability above the threshold is ≥ 95%, the deflated probability ≥ 95% at a
  [false discovery rate](../../glossary.md#false-discovery-rate) ≤ 10%, the
  [statistical power](../../glossary.md#statistical-power) ≥ 80%, the width of the confidence
  interval ≤ 50% of the effect, and the minimum [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) holds.
- *Retry* when the evidence is promising but the power is insufficient or the sensitivity
  excessive: a plan to lengthen the sample or to simplify.
- *Fail* when the probabilities are insufficient after deflation, or the results are structurally
  inconsistent.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- significant new out-of-sample data arrives, or the [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes;
- monitoring (S6) sees a gap beyond its threshold, for example a live [Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#sharpe-ratio) more than 30%
  below the validity Sharpe ratio over the control window;
- market rules, microstructure or the universe change notably;
- a new upstream method changes labels or features: it is entered again in the trial registry.

Monitoring (S6) receives the drift watchlist: metrics to watch, alert thresholds, windows.
Lifecycle (S7) receives the plan to extend the sample and the rules of re-test.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Consolidate the inputs and the trial registry</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Lock the clean out-of-sample trajectories that S4.1's protocol produced, and count the
shots: every [variant](../../glossary.md#variant) tested.

**Actions.**
- Gather the out-of-sample series of P&L or returns per fold or path, with their metadata:
  horizon, timestamp, universe, costs used, frozen rules.
- Keep the trial registry: variants, windows, filters, thresholds, stopping rules; tell major
  ideas from minor tweaks; date each entry. The count covers the lab's trials and section 3's,
  from the dossier, with S4.1's.

**KPI.** Share of out-of-sample trajectories that can be traced: 100%. Completeness of the trial
registry: 100%.

**Guardrail.** The general [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail) below.

**Output.** Out-of-sample pack (series and metadata) and the signed trial registry → steps 3 and
4, S4.3 ([probability of backtest overfitting](../../glossary.md#probability-of-backtest-overfitting)).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Define the useful effect and normalise the measures</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Assess the right object: the net effect that can be exploited at the target horizon.
Setting the threshold is design; normalising the measures is execution.

**Actions.**
- Fix the minimum credible threshold, for example a target net Sharpe ratio at the horizon or a
  minimum net annual [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha), consistent with the
  [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) and [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) of S4.5.
- Normalise the calendar, the annualisation, the conventions of costs, the horizons and the
  aggregations.

**KPI.** The gap between the gross and net versions is documented, with one set of conventions
active.

**Guardrail.** The general guardrails below.

**Output.** Target effect (defined) and the net P&L series (net of costs, harmonised) → step 3
(inference), step 5 (power).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Infer the effect with a realistic uncertainty</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Measure the size of the effect and the probability that it is above the threshold,
with no naive assumption of normality.

**Actions.**
- Estimate the observed effect (for example the net [Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#sharpe-ratio),
  [information ratio](../../glossary.md#information-ratio) or alpha), with confidence intervals
  that respect fat tails.
- Compute the probability that the effect exceeds the threshold: a probabilistic version of the
  Sharpe ratio test that accounts for the length of the record, its skewness and its kurtosis,
  the [probabilistic Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#probabilistic-sharpe-ratio).

**KPI.** Probability above the threshold (of the probabilistic Sharpe ratio type) ≥ 95%; width of
the confidence interval ≤ 50% of the estimated effect.

**Guardrail.** The general guardrails below.

**Output.** Estimated effect with its confidence interval, and the probability above the
threshold → step 4 (corrections for multiple testing), S4.10 (the promotion gate).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Multiple testing and the deflated verdict</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Remove the illusion of the contest winner.

**Actions.**
- Quantify the effective number of trials from the trial registry, accounting for the
  correlations between variants.
- Apply a control of false discoveries, and produce a deflated effect and probability, of the
  [deflated Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#deflated-sharpe-ratio) type.

**KPI.** Deflated probability (of the deflated Sharpe ratio type) ≥ 95%, at a target false
discovery rate ≤ 10%.

**Guardrail.** The general guardrails below.

**Output.** Probability corrected for multiple testing, and the false-discovery report (the rate
of false positives tolerated and realised) → S4.3 (probability of backtest overfitting), S4.10
(the promotion gate).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Power, and whether the data is sufficient</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Decide whether "it does not pass" for lack of information, or because the edge does not
exist.

**Actions.**
- Draw the power curve against the duration or size of the sample, for the target effect: what
  length is needed to detect it 80% of the time or more.
- Conclude: sufficient; insufficient, so collect an extension; or pointless.

**KPI.** Power at the target edge ≥ 80%; otherwise, the length required, stated explicitly
(months, days, trades).

**Guardrail.** The general guardrails below.

**Output.** Power card (current power, length required, recommendation) → S7 (lifecycle, for the
plan of data), S4.10 (the promotion gate).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Minimum robustness and the operational verdict</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Make sure the conclusion holds under reasonable perturbations. The stress is design;
compiling the card and giving the verdict is execution.

**Actions.**
- Run a short stress: consistent resampling in time, modest perturbations of labels and features,
  tolerance to outliers.
- Compile the validity card and give the verdict, pass, retry or fail, with concrete
  recommendations: collect more data, simplify, stop.

**KPI.** Stability of the effect under stress: at most 20% variation of the key metrics.

**Guardrail.** The general guardrails below.

**Output.** Validity card (probabilistic and deflated Sharpe ratios, confidence interval, power,
trials, stress) and its verdict → S4.3 (selection and [overfitting](../../glossary.md#overfitting)), S4.6 and S4.7 (execution
simulation, cost analysis), S6 (monitoring), S7 (lifecycle).

</details>

**General guardrails.** Five failure modes are controlled across all the steps:

| Failure mode | Control |
|---|---|
| [Leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) and peeking | Every series comes from the declared out-of-sample folds, and the stopping period was not chosen after the fact (a signed trace). |
| Non-normal returns | Intervals and probabilities that respect extremes: no naive p-values, no variance assumed known. |
| Hidden multiple testing | The trial registry is mandatory; the sanction is a deflated Sharpe ratio and a false discovery rate. |
| A short sample | No pass with a power below 80%, except in a case argued explicitly in a waiver document. |
| Opportunistic tuning | Every change of parameter is entered again as a new trial. |

## Deliverables

- Validity specification: target effect, conventions, thresholds, false discovery rate.
- Out-of-sample pack (net series and metadata) and the trial registry, versioned and signed.
- Inference report: estimated effect, confidence interval, probabilities (raw and deflated), a
  summary of skewness and kurtosis.
- Power card: current power, length required, recommendation.
- Stress brief: robustness results, sensitivity, known limits.
- Validity card, in summary, with its verdict: pass, retry or fail.
- [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) dashboard, updated automatically with each new out-of-sample result.
- Replacement process: on retry, the actions; on fail, archive or stop; on pass, the handover to
  S4.3, the execution simulator and cost analysis.
