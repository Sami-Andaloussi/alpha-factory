# S4.1 — Experimental protocol & anti-leakage barriers

## TL;DR

Organises honest tests against the future: time is cut into partitions, leakage is blocked by purging and embargo, and every trial is run, logged and counted.
The result is a credible distribution of out-of-sample performance that covers the regimes and replays bit for bit from its experiment passport.
S4.2 reads the distribution; the execution simulator, cost analysis and paper trading replay the same partitions.

## Purpose

Organise honest tests against the future: cut time into partitions, put barriers against
[leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage), and run repeated, traceable trials, so as to produce a
credible distribution of [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) performance
that resembles live trading. The written rules form the
[experimental protocol](../../glossary.md#experimental-protocol), fixed before any result is read.

Section 4 never takes the dossier's results at face value. The dossier's
[trial registry](../../glossary.md#trial-registry) already holds the lab's trials and section 3's;
every trial run here joins it, and statistics are corrected for the total (S4.2, S4.3).

**Success looks like:** a protocol with no leakage, reproducible and covering the [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime), whose
out-of-sample outputs are ready for S4.2 and are reused by execution simulation, cost analysis
and [paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The sealed batches, read as of a date, bring the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) data, cleanly timestamped, with the
market calendars and the corrections of events; the gate report says what the batch was checked
for. The feature registry brings [features](../../glossary.md#feature) free of leakage, the [labels](../../glossary.md#label) with their horizon, and any
weights. The dossier and the simulation plan bring the hypothesis, the horizon and the assets
targeted, and what section 4 is to test. S4.2 receives the consolidated out-of-sample pack with
the map of splits, and the trial registry with this sub-section's trials added. The
[execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) (S4.6), cost analysis (S4.7) and paper trading (S4.8) [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) execution on the same
purged and embargoed partitions, which the experiment passport carries. Monitoring (S6) receives
a hygiene watchlist: [embargo](../../glossary.md#embargo) over label horizon, share of overlap purged, [reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility).
Lifecycle (S7) receives the [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) triggers.

**Constraints.**
- Clocks: everything in one consistent market time; no mixing of UTC and local time without a
  clear rule.
- Formats: split indices are immutable and addressable, by date or by [bar](../../glossary.md#bar).
- Annualisation: one convention (trading days or calendar days), documented here and reused by
  every later sub-section.

**Service levels:**
- Partitions updated after new data arrives: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 0.5 × the decision frequency; T99 ≤ 1 × the
  decision frequency.
- Critical events (IPO, suspension, delisting, change of trading hours): calendars and splits
  revised at T90 ≤ 1 × L, the label horizon; an alert when exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- point-in-time data is available, the labels (horizon L) are frozen, and the market calendar is
  validated;
- the experiment charter is signed (step 1);
- no dependency on the Strategy Lab is blocking.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the experiment passport is complete: splits, purge p, embargo e, warm-up w, logs, [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed);
- the consolidated out-of-sample pack holds a distribution and its regime coverage;
- the reproducibility test is passed.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- the data [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) structurally: lasting changes of volatility or [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity);
- market rules change: trading hours, tick size, microstructure;
- the hypothesis mutates: a new horizon L or holding period H, a new universe;
- monitoring fails: gaps between live and out-of-sample results beyond the tolerances set in
  section 6.

Monitoring (S6.1, and S6.4 for drift) receives alert thresholds on embargo over label horizon, on
the share of overlap purged and on drift of the regime coverage. Lifecycle (S7) receives the cadence of re-execution
(for example at each data roll or change of trading hours) and the criteria for retiring an old
passport.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Pre-register the experiment: the charter</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a frame, the split gets tuned after the fact, and that is a bias.

**Actions.**
- Fix the decision unit (the cadence), the label horizon (look-ahead L) and an average holding
  period (H).
- Define the market calendar (holidays, half days) and the granularity.
- Set the rules of annualisation, consistent across units and regimes.
- List what will be measured out of sample: performance metrics, stability, coverage.

**KPI.** Consistency of units: every metric expressed in the same base of time and asset.

**Guardrail.** A cross review of the charter before any run, with a checklist: calendar, L, H,
cadence.

**Output.** Locked experiment charter (hypotheses, units, metrics, rules) → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Cut time: walk-forward, time-series cross-validation, CPCV</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The split depends on L, H, the cadence and the metrics, so it comes after them.

**Actions.**
- Choose the family of partition: [walk-forward](../../glossary.md#walk-forward) to follow the
  live sequence; purged time-series [cross-validation](../../glossary.md#cross-validation) when
  several out-of-sample readings are needed;
  [combinatorial purged cross-validation](../../glossary.md#combinatorial-purged-cross-validation)
  ([CPCV](../../glossary.md#cpcv)) for many out-of-sample paths.
- Size in-sample and out-of-sample: in-sample long enough for several cycles of L and varied
  regimes; out-of-sample at least one significant decision window.
- Fix the rolling step (the stride) and the number of out-of-sample paths targeted.

**KPI.** Out-of-sample coverage, the share of the history that out-of-sample windows cover;
target: several identifiable regimes covered. Effective number of out-of-sample paths; target:
enough to read a distribution, not a single path.

**Guardrail.** Random cross-validation is forbidden: past and future are never mixed.

**Output.** Master partition plan (in-sample and out-of-sample indices, step, number of paths) →
step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Set the anti-leakage barriers: purging and embargo</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** [Purging](../../glossary.md#purging) and embargo apply to concrete splits, so they come once the splits exist.

**Actions.**
- [Purging](../../glossary.md#purging): remove from training every observation whose tail of
  information overlaps the test window. Practical rule: purge p ≥ max(L, H), in time or in bars.
- [Embargo](../../glossary.md#embargo): place a buffer e around each out-of-sample window, to cut
  latent spill-overs (announcements, open positions, chains of events). Practical rule: embargo
  e ≥ L, and ≥ the operational latency where relevant.
- Recompute the cleaned in-sample indices, after purging and embargo.

**KPI.** Share of overlap purged: about 100% of the theoretical overlaps. Embargo over label
horizon, e / L: ≥ 1; an alert below 1.

**Guardrail.** An infiltration test: shift the label artificially into the future; if in-sample
performance rises and out-of-sample performance does not, a leak is likely.

**Output.** Purged partitions and the embargo calendar → step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Run the trials and count them properly</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Run only healthy splits. Running and logging is execution; when a result puts in
doubt what counts as a trial, the work turns to design.

**Actions.**
- Warm-up: ignore the first points needed to fill the indicators (w, the lookback required).
- Seeds and logs: lock the random draws and log each split: timestamp, indices, versions of the
  data, labels and features.
- Trial counter: every trial counts, and none is removed after the fact. Each one joins the
  dossier's trial registry, after the lab's and section 3's.

**KPI.** [Reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility): the same splits and the same
[seed](../../glossary.md#seed) give the same outputs. Share of trials "consumed": 100% of the
planned splits run and counted.

**Guardrail.** A lock against post-hoc removal: no trial can be deleted because of its result.

**Output.** Out-of-sample trajectories per split, and the trial registry (the same splits give
the same outputs) → step 5, S4.2 (the count of trials).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Consolidate the out-of-sample results into a reading of stability</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The aim is the distribution, not a single value.

**Actions.**
- Aggregate the median, the dispersion and the tail of the distribution of the out-of-sample
  metrics.
- Check the coverage of [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime): rises and falls, high and low
  volatility, known shocks.
- Produce a map of the out-of-sample windows (when, how much, with which barriers) that the
  following sub-sections can read.

**KPI.** Out-of-sample dispersion: standard deviation or interquartile range of the key metrics.
Out-of-sample median against in-sample: a picture of the expected drop, not an acceptance
criterion here.

**Guardrail.** A coverage stress: if a major regime does not appear out of sample, the reading is
flagged "incomplete".

**Output.** Consolidated [out-of-sample pack](../../glossary.md#out-of-sample-pack) (distribution,
coverage, notes of reading) → S4.2 (validity inference); the [execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator), cost analysis
and paper trading replay its partitions (S4.6 to S4.8).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Freeze the traceability and the rules of rerun</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** What was really run is frozen last.

**Actions.**
- Issue the [experiment passport](../../glossary.md#experiment-passport): charter, final
  partitions, purge p, embargo e, warm-up w, logs, checks.
- Define the triggers of re-execution: new data, a change of L or H, calendar drift, incidents.

**KPI.** Integrity of the passport: every piece present, signed and timestamped.

**Guardrail.** Identical replay: a rerun from the passport reproduces the results bit for bit.

**Output.** Passport and requalification triggers → S4.9 (scientific governance), S6
(monitoring), S7 (lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Experiment charter: objectives, units, L and H, calendar, annualisation.
- Master partition plan: walk-forward, cross-validation or CPCV; in-sample and out-of-sample
  sizes; stride; number of paths.
- Documented barriers (purge p, embargo e, warm-up w), with proof that they were applied.
- Trial registry: seeds, versions of data, labels and features, timestamps, results per split.
- Consolidated out-of-sample pack: distribution, regime coverage, commented reading.
- Experiment passport, with everything needed for an identical replay, and a replacement
  checklist: how to issue a new passport when L, H or the universe change.
- Hygiene dashboard: the [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of steps 3 and 4, with green, orange and red lights.
