# S4.7 — Universal transaction cost analysis & execution attribution (closed loop)

## TL;DR

The factory's meter of ground truth between the intention (price and time of decision) and the last fill: every basis point lost or gained is measured, broken down and explained.
The anchor is implementation shortfall, split into timing, impact, spread, fees and opportunity with no residue above 1 bp at p95; VWAP is only informative.
It recalibrates the simulator (S4.6), steers execution, and derives the capacity that can be exploited, for S4.5, S4.8, the promotion gate (S4.10) and capital steering (S7.8).

## Purpose

Set up the meter of ground truth between the intention (the price and time of decision) and the
last fill. Universal [transaction cost analysis](../../glossary.md#transaction-cost-analysis)
measures, breaks down and explains each basis point lost or gained (timing, impact, [spread](../../glossary.md#spread), fees,
opportunity), then sends the information back to recalibrate the [execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator), to steer
execution (routing, algorithms, brokers, [venues](../../glossary.md#venue)) and to derive the [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) that can be exploited.
The anchor of truth is [implementation shortfall](../../glossary.md#implementation-shortfall), with
the distinction between effective and realised cost; VWAP is informative only.

**Success looks like:** costs measured cleanly, attributed without ambiguity, comparable
everywhere, and actionable: the simulator's parameters and the rules of execution updated.

<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

- Stable formats, [idempotent](../../glossary.md#idempotent) [replays](../../glossary.md#replay), strict [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time),

**Service levels:**
- Intraday metrics available ≤ 60 s after the fill at p90, ≤ 180 s at p95.

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the canon of cost analysis is validated (step 1), and the decision price is formalised as the
  anchor;
- the tables of fills and fees are reliable;
- a first calibrated version of the simulator exists;
- the clocks are checked, and the scope (strategies, assets) is defined.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the decomposition leaves no residue above 1 bp (p95);
- the simulator and cost analysis agree within their tolerances (step 4);
- the scorecards are robust and the routing policies published;
- the capacity curves are versioned;
- the stream to monitoring is connected, with its alerts active.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- the KPIs [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) beyond their threshold for 2 weeks;
- the microstructure changes: tick, fees, rules;
- new brokers, algorithms or venues arrive, or a new logic of execution;
- new [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime) of volatility appear;
- the gap between the simulator and cost analysis exceeds its tolerance.

Monitoring (S6) receives the live KPIs, the tolerances and the alerts. Lifecycle (S7) receives the
budgets of costs, the thresholds of promotion, reduction and stop, and the triggers of re-test.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · The canon of cost analysis and its anchors: schema and rules</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a common canon, comparing and attributing are impossible.

**Actions.**
- Define the universal grammar: identities of orders (parent and child), side, sizes and
  notional, timestamps (decision, sending, arrival, acceptance, entry into and exit from the
  queue, fill), the price of intention (the anchor of implementation shortfall), the pre-trade mid
  as benchmark, the mid at arrival, the context of the book where available, the tags of broker,
  algorithm and venue, fees and levies, rejections and cancels, and tags of latency.
- Set the rules of timestamping (UTC, maximum drift), the conventions (a buy cost is positive) and
  the mapping across assets.

**KPI.** Completeness of the critical fields ≥ 99.5%; maximum drift of clocks ≤ 5 ms within a
venue, ≤ 1 ms within a process; unique IDs 100%.

**Guardrail.** Consistency tests (side, sign of cost), monotonic timestamps, detection of a shifted
clock; events outside the window rejected or flagged.

**Output.** Specification of cost analysis, validators of data, and a "golden table" of normalised
events → steps 2 to 6, monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Measure implementation shortfall and break it down, cost by cost</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Explain the implementation shortfall, and prepare the attribution.

**Actions.**
- Compute the implementation shortfall from the decision to the fills, and separate: timing (the
  market moves by itself), impact (the order moves the price), [spread](../../glossary.md#spread),
  fees, opportunity (what was not executed), and latency or queue [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) where they can be
  observed.
- Also use the effective cost (against the pre-trade mid) and the realised cost (against a short
  post-trade mid) to frame the impact, which is the difference between them. VWAP and TWAP are
  informative only, never the anchor.

**KPI.** Median gap between effective and realised cost consistent with the expected impact;
residue of the decomposition (shortfall − the sum of the components) ≤ 1 bp at p95; a plausible
share of "negative effective spread" in fragmented markets.

**Guardrail.** Rules against double counting fees and taxes; bounds on the spread (≥ 0 when
quoted; the effective spread possibly < 0 with hidden price improvement); benchmarks anchored on
the decision price.

**Output.** Table of fills and cost components, per order and per execution → steps 3 to 6, the
simulator (S4.6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Conditional attribution: the market, and the choices of execution</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Know what to adjust: speed, participation, scheduling, the mix of venues.

**Actions.**
- Explain the costs by volatility, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity) and depth, time of day,
  relative size, participation, walking the book, and so on: the
  [execution attribution](../../glossary.md#execution-attribution).
- Separate temporary from permanent impact, and the share due to queues and latencies.
- Normalise by context, to avoid Simpson's paradox and to compare brokers, algorithms and venues
  at equal difficulty.

**KPI.** Stability of the effects per bin (Δ ≤ 20% between contiguous bins); explanatory R² of the
costs, targeted per strategy; residual variance under control; neutrality to the mix of orders.

**Guardrail.** Controls of fair comparison (matching by context); Simpson tests (signs inverted
after aggregation); a clean separation of timing and impact (the correlation of impact with size
and participation).

**Output.** Dataset of conditional attribution and contextual scorecards → step 5, section 5
(through S4.10).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · The closed loop between the simulator and cost analysis: calibration and V&V</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Closing the loop means no longer flying blind.

**Actions.**
- Compare the forecast (the simulator) with the realised (cost analysis) for each component
  (impact, spread, fees, opportunity): compute the errors of the model by context, and update the
  parameters, the budgets of costs and the tolerances.
- Re-simulate representative baskets, and validate the reconciliation.

**KPI.** |Relative error| from forecast to realised on the total impact ≤ 20% (p75) and ≤ 35%
(p95) after recalibration; convergence in ≤ 2 cycles on the validation sets.

**Guardrail.** Non-regression back-tests; a contextual hold-out set; alarms on an error rising for
2 weeks.

**Output.** Updated parameters of the simulator, report of
[verification and validation](../../glossary.md#verification-and-validation), budgets of costs per
strategy and regime → the simulator (S4.6), section 5 (through S4.10), section 7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Steering execution: comparisons and experiments</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Turn the measure into decisions of execution.

**Actions.**
- Build dashboards of brokers, algorithms and venues, conditioned on the same context.
- Run controlled tests (A/B tests, or contextual split routes) with criteria of success set in
  advance: basis points gained, net of the risk of opportunity.
- Produce routing policies per regime: participation, scheduling, mix of market and limit orders,
  dark or lit, venues.

**KPI.** Net gain per order (bps) against the control, significant, with a target
[statistical power](../../glossary.md#statistical-power) set in advance; hit rate of the
contextual policies; p95 implementation shortfall down, without the opportunity cost rising above
a threshold.

**Guardrail.** Randomisation or matching, a minimum sample per cell, peeking forbidden; client and
proprietary flows separated where relevant.

**Output.** Routing recommendations and production-ready scorecards → section 5 (through S4.10),
monitoring (S6).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Capacity and the rules of sizing: from cost to scale</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Scale without killing the [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha), and set budgets.

**Actions.**
- Convert the curve of total cost against participation, volume and time into a capacity curve:
  the level at which the net alpha falls to zero.
- Define the thresholds of deployment ([return on execution costs](../../glossary.md#return-on-execution-costs),
  minimum net [information ratio](../../glossary.md#information-ratio)) and the slopes of decay.
- Link them to the [fundamental law](../../glossary.md#fundamental-law-of-active-management):
  friction reduces the effective information ratio, and capacity is bounded by the gross alpha and
  the costs.

**KPI.** Return on execution costs ≥ its threshold per strategy; net information ratio ≥ the
minimum; slope of the costs (bps per point of participation) within bounds; the point of breakeven
identified and versioned.

**Guardrail.** [Hysteresis](../../glossary.md#hysteresis) of deployment, with no back and forth
around the threshold; a recomputation when fees, tick or venue change; a watch on post-trade
reversion, the sign of excessive impact.

**Output.** Capacity curves and the rules of sizing and promotion, gathered with the rest in the
[cost attribution report](../../glossary.md#cost-attribution-report) → lifecycle (S7.8), allocation
in section 5 (through S4.10).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Universal specification of cost analysis: schema, anchors, rules of computation, conventions.
- Log of choices: benchmarks, windows of "realised", rules of attribution, normalisations.
- Modules of measure and decomposition (implementation shortfall, effective and realised costs,
  timing, impact, spread, fees, opportunity, latency and queue), with unit tests.
- A datamart of cost analysis, ready for the simulator, section 5 and monitoring: normalised tables
  and aggregated views.
- Dashboards of brokers, algorithms and venues, conditioned on context, with an engine of
  experiments.
- Report of [calibration](../../glossary.md#calibration) between the simulator and cost analysis: parameters, verification and
  validation, tolerances.
- Capacity curves and rules of sizing: thresholds, hysteresis, runbook of escalation.
- Replacement process: [versioning](../../glossary.md#versioning), [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback), non-regression.
