# S4.10 — Promotion gates, risk tolerances & fallback mechanisms

## TL;DR

Decides go, go under conditions or no-go for a strategy ready for production, sets the risk envelope it may use, and prepares the plan of retreat if it deviates.
Five pillars are scored with their uncertainty (validity, selection, execution, stability, capacity), and no strategy turns green while a critical pillar fails, whatever the others show.
Its signed promotion record is section 4's single exit: section 5 deploys from it, monitoring watches it, lifecycle records it.

## Purpose

Decide go, go under conditions, or no-go for a strategy ready to enter production; define the
envelope of risk it is allowed; and prepare the plan of retreat if it deviates. This is the
factory's [promotion gate](../../glossary.md#promotion-gate) at the end of validation. Section 3
already read its own [simulation plan](../../glossary.md#simulation-plan), green, orange or red; section 4 reaches its own verdict on
its own evidence: S3 improves, S4 judges.

**Success looks like:** a traceable certification, with clear limits, a controlled ramp-up, stop
triggers, and clean bridges to section 5 (execution), section 6 (monitoring) and section 7
(lifecycle), gathered in the [promotion record](../../glossary.md#promotion-record).

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

From S4.1 to S4.9 come the [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) distributions
over many paths and the evidence against statistical illusions; the results of
[robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness) (stress on several axes, sensitivities, fragile points);
the [execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) (costs, impact, latency, queues,
walking the book) and cost analysis (realised costs, the extended
[implementation shortfall](../../glossary.md#implementation-shortfall)) with their
cross-calibration; [paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading) (logs of orders, latencies,
rejections, incidents, [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift) against the simulator and cost analysis);
and the metadata and versions: partitions, [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), signatures of datasets,
[labels](../../glossary.md#label), [features](../../glossary.md#feature) and code. The promotion
record is section 4's single exit: it carries the certification sheet (decision, limits, ramp-up,
triggers, who decides), the operating charter (budgets, rules, capacities), the ramp-up plan and the
specification of the instrumentation, and it references the frozen dossier of evidence of step 1:
section 5 reads the artefacts of S4.1 to S4.9 through it. Section 5 onboards and deploys from it
(S5.1, S5.9). Monitoring (S6.1) receives the metrics and alerts with their thresholds, and the
mapping from triggers to actions; live [execution fidelity](../../glossary.md#execution-fidelity)
(S6.2) the expectations of execution it judges the fills against; lifecycle (S7.6) the status and
the calendar of reviews, with the conditions of demotion (live to paper) and of re-promotion; the
system of record (S7.2) the evidence behind each decision of promotion;
[change management](../../glossary.md#change-management) (S7.5) the evidence of validation it links
to each [release package](../../glossary.md#release-package). The incident runbook goes to S6.7.

**Constraints.**
- Formats: every artefact readable by machines, with a stable schema, mandatory fields and
  explicit units.
- Consistency: the same conventions of time, assets and aggregation across [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest), simulator,
  cost analysis, paper trading and live.

**Service levels:**
- The metrics of tracking refreshed at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) within a fixed window, for example by the end of day D.
- Critical market events (IPO, suspension, delisting) integrated into the rules of risk and the
  charter at T90 / T95; an alert when exceeded.
- Dashboards available at T90 after a promotion or a [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the evidence is in: valid out-of-sample results, documented robustness, a calibrated simulator,
  cost analysis done, paper trading orchestrated with complete logs;
- the [traceability](../../glossary.md#traceability) is complete, and there is no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage);
- no critical issue is open, or it is accepted in the clauses of an "orange".

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the decision (go, go under conditions, no-go) is signed;
- the operating charter is complete, and the ramp-up plan defined;
- the incident runbook is published;
- the instrumentation is connected (section 6), and the status recorded (section 7).

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- costs or performance [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) against their tolerances, repeatedly or
  severely;
- the market changes (microstructure, rules, [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity)) or the
  universe does (new assets or [venues](../../glossary.md#venue));
- a new method or new data changes the key hypotheses;
- a major operational incident occurs, technical or with a broker.

Monitoring (S6) receives the list of metrics and alerts with their thresholds, and the mapping from
triggers to actions. Lifecycle (S7) receives the status, the calendar of reviews, and the
conditions of demotion (live to paper) and of re-promotion.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Consolidate the evidence and lock the frame</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** The results arrive: validity, robustness, simulator, cost analysis, paper. Before
judging, assemble and freeze the dossier, so that its reading cannot shift with the wind.

**Actions.**
- Gather into one dossier: out-of-sample distributions, the gaps from in-sample to out-of-sample,
  simulated against observed costs, [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity) and [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover), the logs
  of paper trading, the incidents.
- Check the integrity: partitions in time compliant, the count of trials, the metadata, the
  versions of data, labels, features and code.

**KPI.** Completeness of the dossier: the % of required pieces present.

**Guardrail.** Automatic rejection when a critical piece is missing, for example the out-of-sample
evidence, the real or paper costs, or the traceability of the partitions.

**Output.** Frozen dossier of evidence and an attestation of integrity → step 2 (scoring), S6 (the
[baseline](../../glossary.md#baseline) of monitoring).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Score validity on five pillars and map the uncertainty</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the dossier frozen, evaluate in a standard way on five pillars, to be fair between
ideas.

**Actions.**
- Build a certification matrix of five pillars:
  - validity: the size of the effect out of sample, its stability in time;
  - selection: the risk of over-selection, the gap to expect from in-sample to out-of-sample;
  - execution: real costs against budgets, the consistency of impact and [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage);
  - stability: sensitivities under stress, the fragilities identified;
  - capacity: the net [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) sustained at the targeted scale.
- For each pillar: a score, an uncertainty, and thresholds set in advance.

**KPI.** Share of the gaps explained: the variance of the score covered by identified factors.

**Guardrail.** When the uncertainty of a pillar exceeds its threshold set in advance, the decision
is blocked, or passes to "orange" at least.

**Output.** Certification matrix and the profile of uncertainty per pillar → step 3 (decision), S7
(the factory's institutional record).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · A three-colour decision, and the clauses of promotion</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The scores allow a decision: the analysis becomes a traffic light that can be acted on.

**Actions.**
- Green (go) when every pillar clears its threshold, with a contained uncertainty.
- Orange (go under conditions) when one or two pillars are borderline, with clauses: further
  tests, reinforced [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail), limits of size.
- Red (no-go) when a critical pillar fails, or the uncertainty is too high.
- Document the reasons for the decision, and the conditions of an orange.

**KPI.** Rate of contested decisions (re-examined) below a target threshold.

**Guardrail.** No green while a critical pillar fails, even with compensations elsewhere.

**Output.** Decision (green, orange or red) and its clauses → step 4 (envelope of risk), section 5
(the routing of execution).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Define the operational envelope of risk: the operating charter</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** A decision without limits of operation puts capital in danger.

**Actions.**
- Budgets: cost per unit of turnover, slippage per trade, tolerated
  [drawdown](../../glossary.md#drawdown), maximum exposure per asset or cluster, % of average daily
  volume, maximum concentration.
- Rules of operation: time windows, ticket sizes, allowed destinations of orders, thresholds of
  liquidity, behaviour in news and halts, tolerated latency.
- Capacity: the curve of size against degradation, dynamic limits by liquidity, guardrails against
  over-sizing, taken from the operating contract of S4.5.

**KPI.** Rate of breaches of budget: frequency and severity.

**Guardrail.** Fail fast: beyond N micro-breaches or a severity M, an automatic freeze and a
review.

**Output.** Operating charter (limits in numbers, with the logic of their update) → section 5
(execution, scheduling), S6 (surveillance).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · The ramp-up plan and the instrumentation of tracking</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Going live requires a controlled ramp-up, and sensors that check continuously.

**Actions.**
- A staircase of exposure (steps of size), with the conditions of promotion between steps: the
  gaps between simulated and real costs, the drift of performance or risk.
- Instrumentation: metrics of drift (paper against live at the same sizes), realised costs against
  budgets, operational health (rejections, latencies, suspensions).
- Runbooks of checks at each step: a short review, and a go or no-go for the step.

**KPI.** Speed of the ramp realised against the plan, and the gap between simulated and real costs.

**Guardrail.** When the gap exceeds the tolerance set in advance, the step is frozen and the work
returns to step 2, for a new evaluation.

**Output.** Ramp-up plan and the specification of dashboards and alerts → section 5 (progressive
deployment), S6 (dashboards).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Mechanisms of retreat: kill switch, rollback, quarantine</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** The day it deviates, a plan B is needed at once, with no ambiguity.

**Actions.**
- [Kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch), an automatic stop: major breaches (costs,
  drawdown, slippage, liquidity), technical anomalies, breaks in the data.
- [Rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback): a reduction of size, or a return to paper trading, with
  the criteria to unfreeze.
- [Quarantine](../../glossary.md#quarantine) and postmortem: freeze, a structured diagnosis
  (market, data, code, route), corrective actions, re-tests, and a final decision: repair,
  relaunch or retire.
- A clear [RACI](../../glossary.md#raci): who triggers, who validates, who executes.

**KPI.** Mean times to detect and to resolve ([MTTD and MTTR](../../glossary.md#mttd-mttr)).

**Guardrail.** Regular [drills](../../glossary.md#drill); when a drill fails, the strategy goes back
to orange.

**Output.** Incident runbook, decision trees and RACI → S6 (alerting and actions), S7
([requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) and status).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>7 · Handover, and the recording of the certification</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Without a formal transfer and a final log, the organisation forgets.

**Actions.**
- Produce the certification sheet: decision, limits, ramp-up, triggers, RACI, [KPIs](../../glossary.md#kpi) of tracking.
- Have the approvals signed: research, execution, risk; each signer first declares any conflict of
  interest ([principle 4](../../principles.md#4-the-producer-is-never-the-judge)).
- Record the status (paper, small live, live ramp, full live, quarantine, withdrawn) and the
  calendar of reviews.

**KPI.** Delay from decision to production below a target threshold.

**Guardrail.** No release to production while the sheet is incomplete or unsigned.

**Output.** Signed promotion record and the recording of its status → section 5 (its runbook), S6
(surveillance), S7 (governance).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Certification sheet (one page): decision, reasons, limits, ramp-up, triggers, RACI, KPIs of
  tracking.
- Operating charter: budgets, rules of orders, windows, capacities, updates.
- Ramp-up plan: steps, conditions, checklists per step, points of control.
- Incident runbook: kill switch, rollback, quarantine, postmortem, decision trees.
- Specification of instrumentation: metrics, frequencies, service levels, schemas of data, alerts.
- Log of decisions: reasons for the decision, gaps against thresholds, signatures, date and time.
- Tracking table: KPIs per step, drifts, breaches, times to detect and resolve, speed of the ramp,
  revisions.
