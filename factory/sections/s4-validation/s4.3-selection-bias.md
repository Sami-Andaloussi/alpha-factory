# S4.3 — Hierarchical selection & selection-bias risk (PBO)

## TL;DR

Chooses without illusions: a hierarchical tournament between families, variants and strategies, judged on many clean out-of-sample periods.
It measures the probability that the in-sample winner fails out of sample, which must stay at or below 15–20% for anything promoted.
Every candidate leaves with a robust rank, an out-of-sample confidence curve and a status: promote, retest or abandon.

## Purpose

Choose without fooling oneself. A hierarchical tournament is organised between families of ideas,
[variants](../../glossary.md#variant) and strategies, and judged on many clean [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) periods. It measures the
[probability of backtest overfitting](../../glossary.md#probability-of-backtest-overfitting) ([PBO](../../glossary.md#pbo)),
the probability that the in-sample winner fails out of sample, and the decay to expect from
in-sample to out-of-sample. Every trial counts, the lab's and section 3's included: the
[trial registry](../../glossary.md#trial-registry) holds them all.

**Success looks like:**
- a robust ranking;
- an out-of-sample confidence curve per candidate: the plausible range of its future
  performance;
- a decision status: promotable, to retest, or to abandon;
- the evidence that the winner is not a winner by chance.

- Formats: one timestamp, harmonised units (returns, [Sharpe ratio](../../glossary.md#sharpe-ratio), [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover)), a stable universe

- PBO computed after minor new data: [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) < 24 h, from the deposit to the decision sheet.
- Full tournament, for new candidates or families: T95 < 72 h.
- Critical market events that affect the universe (IPO, suspension, delisting, a change in an
  index's composition): the splits concerned replayed and the confidence curves republished at
  T90 24 h / T95 48 h.
- An alert when a service level is missed in more than 10% of cases over 30 days.

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- the point-in-time data is validated;
- the protocol of splits, with purging and embargo, is locked;
- the [trial budget](../../glossary.md#trial-budget) is fixed, clones are filtered, and the conditions of invalidation are known.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- PBO ≤ 15–20% for the promotable candidates;
- the median out-of-sample Sharpe ratio is ≥ 60–70% of the in-sample one;
- the stability of ranks is as required;
- the out-of-sample confidence curve is published and the decision sheet signed.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- new data covers ≥ 10% of the period of study;
- S6 detects [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): PBO rising, stability falling, costs changing;
- the market changes: microstructure, rules, trading hours, tick sizes;
- a credible new [variant](../../glossary.md#variant) appears in the same family;
- a real execution incident (S5, S7) contradicts the out-of-sample range.

Monitoring (S6) receives the alert thresholds on PBO and stability, the drift watchlist and the
logs of trials. Lifecycle (S7) receives the rhythm of requalification, the criteria of promotion
and demotion, and the legacy of decisions, kept as the factory's scientific record.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Define the hierarchical tournament and count the trials</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Structure the competition before any measure, to avoid lopsided comparisons, and count
every attempt; otherwise confidence is inflated.

**Actions.**
- Map the levels: family → variant → strategy.
- Define the criteria of membership of a family: [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) mechanism, horizon, universe, logic of
  position.
- Set the charter of eligibility: point-in-time data, minimum period, warm-up, stopping rules.
- Put in place a control of clones (similarity of signals and performance) and a rule of exclusion
  when two candidates are too close.
- Count the trials, every variant actually tested, and lock the tournament's
  [trial budget](../../glossary.md#trial-budget).

**KPI.** Effective diversity: the share of "very close" pairs below the similarity threshold; an
alert above 20% of clones. The trial budget is respected: overrun ≤ 0.

**Guardrail.** A cloning test (similarity of signals and performance) and a blacklist; refusal
when a rule is violated.

**Output.** Tournament charter (hierarchy, rules, trial budget, filters of clones) → step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Set clean partitions and play the matches</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** With the tournament defined, judge each candidate repeatedly on several out-of-sample
periods that do not leak. Setting the partitions is design; playing the matches is execution.

**Actions.**
- Define the time slots: in-sample and out-of-sample sizes, overlap, purging of dependencies and
  embargo against [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage).
- Multiply the combinations of splits, to obtain a distribution out of sample for each candidate,
  not a single score ([combinatorial purged cross-validation](../../glossary.md#combinatorial-purged-cross-validation)
  or [walk-forward](../../glossary.md#walk-forward)).
- Fix the warm-up of [features](../../glossary.md#feature) and positions, and consistent rules of annualisation.
- Play every match of the tournament, as the charter says.

**KPI.** Out-of-sample independence (no forbidden overlap); out-of-sample coverage ≥ 50% of the
total period; number of valid splits.

**Guardrail.** Checks of purging and embargo (zero [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage)); a test of
the stability of parameters during warm-up.

**Output.** Cohort of in-sample and out-of-sample results per split and per candidate
(trajectories, P&L, Sharpe ratio, [drawdowns](../../glossary.md#drawdown),
[turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover)…) → step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Rank each season and measure the stability of ranks</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** The matches produce performances; they are summarised by repeated rankings, and the
stability is tested: a truly good candidate stays good.

**Actions.**
- Produce an out-of-sample ranking for each split, on the chosen metric (for example the
  out-of-sample Sharpe ratio).
- Measure the stability of ranks between splits: rank correlation, rate of presence in the top k,
  rate of inversion.
- Identify the dominant families and the fragile variants, whose performance is volatile.

**KPI.** Rank correlation between splits ≥ 0.4 (target) for the top-k candidates; rate of
presence in the top k ≥ 60%.

**Guardrail.** An alert on mass inversions: more than 30% of the duels flip from one split to the
next.

**Output.** Table of repeated ranks and indicators of stability → step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Estimate PBO and the decay from in-sample to out-of-sample; draw the confidence curve</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** With repeated rankings, the illusion can be quantified: how far the in-sample winner
collapses out of sample.

**Actions.**
- Estimate PBO: the probability that the in-sample best underperforms out of sample (for example,
  falls at or below the out-of-sample median of the candidates) across the splits.
- Quantify the decay to expect from in-sample to out-of-sample: the ratio of out-of-sample to
  in-sample Sharpe ratio, the loss of return or of [information ratio](../../glossary.md#information-ratio).
- Build the out-of-sample confidence curve of each candidate: the plausible range of its future
  performance.

**KPI.** PBO of the winner ≤ 15–20%; median out-of-sample Sharpe over in-sample Sharpe ≥ 60–70%;
the width of the confidence curve contained.

**Guardrail.** A short-sample stress, by block resampling. An alert when PBO explodes as purging
and embargo are tightened.

**Output.** PBO map per candidate, out-of-sample confidence curve and table of decay → step 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Decide hierarchically and give each candidate a status</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** With the risk of illusion measured, decide without telling ourselves stories: promote,
retest or abandon.

**Actions.**
- Set decision thresholds: maximum PBO, maximum decay, minimum stability.
- Decide by family first (to avoid comparing apples and oranges), then by variant, then by
  strategy.
- Write a decision sheet for each candidate: strengths, weaknesses, conditions of invalidation,
  status.

**KPI.** Rate of acceptance under constraints (≤ x%, depending on the budget); hit rate after
[paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading) of the low-PBO candidates (target: above the
internal benchmark).

**Guardrail.** No optimisation on the out-of-sample periods: reruns are forbidden after the
decision. A family quorum: at least one candidate per family must pass the thresholds, otherwise
the family goes back into review.

**Output.** Batch of statuses and decision sheets → S4.6 and S4.7 (execution simulation, cost
analysis), and section 5 (aggregation, portfolio) through S4.10.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Traceability, against p-hacking, and the feedback loops</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Once the decision is taken, prove it and reuse it: audit, follow-up, requalification.

**Actions.**
- Lock the pre-registered protocol, the [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), the versions of data, labels and features, and the
  log of trials, with every attempt counted.
- Publish a dashboard (PBO, stability, decay, rates of acceptance and rejection) and alerts on
  drift.
- Define the triggers of [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification): new data, drift, a
  change of market rules, a promising new variant.

**KPI.** [Reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility): 100% (a rerun gives the same
results). Delay of requalification below its service level (see Interfaces).

**Guardrail.** Full control of versions: data, labels, features, hyperparameters. A "trial
counter" alert on repeated overruns.

**Output.** Complete audit dossier, dashboard and requalification rules → S6 (monitoring), S7
(lifecycle).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Tournament charter: hierarchy, rules, trial budget, filters of clones.
- Partition plan: in-sample and out-of-sample sizes, purging and embargo, warm-up, annualisation.
- Table of repeated ranks, with the indicators of stability.
- PBO map per candidate, the decay from in-sample to out-of-sample, and the out-of-sample
  confidence curve.
- Decision sheets: status, thresholds, conditions of invalidation, assumptions for the execution
  simulator and cost analysis.
- Log of trials: attempts counted, versions, seeds, data, labels and features locked.
- KPI dashboard (PBO, stability, decay, rate of acceptance, drifts) and the rules of alert.
- Requalification procedure: triggers, service levels, responsibility.
