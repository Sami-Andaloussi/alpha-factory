# S4.5 — Economic viability, capacity & the fundamental law

## TL;DR

Checks that a signal makes net P&L that can be exploited at size, once real costs, turnover and capacity are counted.
It fixes an operating size with its ramp, a budget of costs and turnover, a green, orange and red capacity curve, and the share of the theoretical edge that survives execution.
The operating contract goes to the promotion gate (S4.10) and to capital and cost steering (S7.8); live gauges go to monitoring.

## Purpose

Check that a signal generates net P&L that can be exploited at size, counting the real costs
(fees, [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage), [market impact](../../glossary.md#market-impact)),
the [turnover](../../glossary.md#turnover) and the [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity): the
capital and the participation that can be sustained. The [alpha](../../glossary.md#alpha) of the laboratory is translated into
a net [information ratio](../../glossary.md#information-ratio), a budget of costs, a capacity curve
and an operating point, recorded in the [operating contract](../../glossary.md#operating-contract).

**Success looks like:**
- an operating size, with its ramp;
- a budget of costs and turnover;
- a capacity curve in green, orange and red zones;
- an [execution transfer coefficient](../../glossary.md#execution-transfer-coefficient) between
  the theoretical alpha and the realised one.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The out-of-sample pack brings the series of the signal (predictions, positions, decisions); the
validity card, the selection report and the robustness report bring the estimates of the
[information coefficient](../../glossary.md#information-coefficient) and of the effective
[breadth](../../glossary.md#breadth) from S4.1 to S4.4. The execution profile of the simulator
(S4.6) and the cost attribution report (S4.7) bring the traces of orders and transactions, the
volumes and quotes, and the costs measured. The operating contract holds the size, the ramp, the
turnover and the budgets; the capacity curve, with the expected transfer coefficient and the
target [return on execution costs](../../glossary.md#return-on-execution-costs); and the safety envelope with its thresholds. Section 5 receives
it, as the constraints of allocation and execution, through the [promotion gate](../../glossary.md#promotion-gate) of
[S4.10](s4.10-promotion-gates.md); lifecycle's capital and cost steering (S7.8) receives the
triggers to scale up or down, the cadence of review and the criteria of
[sunset](../../glossary.md#sunset). Monitoring receives the live gauges (S6.2 for
[execution fidelity](../../glossary.md#execution-fidelity)), and S6.7 the runbook of adjustment. The gaps between forecast and realised costs go
back to the simulator and cost analysis (S4.6, S4.7) for recalibration, as feedback.

**Constraints.**
- Minimum granularity: decision → execution, timestamped [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time).
- Units: basis points, euros, annualised turnover, participation (%); clear conventions, such as
  the base price.

**Service levels:**
- Costs and gauges updated at [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) ≤ 15 min after each day's close (to adapt to the universe).
- Market incidents (IPO, suspension, delisting, a rule of microstructure): propagated to the
  capacity models within 1 business day, at T90 / T95; an automatic alert when exceeded.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- statistical validity out of sample is established (a clean protocol, no [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage)), with an
  information coefficient significantly above zero;
- the first measures of cost analysis and execution simulation are available;
- the definitions of cost and turnover are frozen.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the operating contract is validated;
- the net information ratio at the target size is ≥ the standard;
- the return on execution costs is ≥ its threshold;
- the capacity curve is signed;
- the safety envelope and its alarms are configured, and the handover to sections 5 and 6 is
  done.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Rerun the sub-section when:
- costs [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): the median gap realised − forecast exceeds X bps over N days;
- the [regime](../../glossary.md#regime) changes: [spreads](../../glossary.md#spread) or volumes move by more than Y standard
  deviations;
- a rule of microstructure changes: tick size, lots, priorities;
- the information coefficient or the breadth [drifts](../../glossary.md#drift): a persistent fall
  of either;
- a new method of execution, or a new market, is added.

Monitoring (S6) receives the live gauges: transfer coefficient, return on execution costs, drift
of costs. Lifecycle (S7) receives the triggers to scale up or down, the cadence of review, and the
criteria of sunset when the transfer coefficient or the return on execution costs stay red.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Map gross to net and fix the grammar of costs</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Put everyone on the same base: one definition of costs in basis points (commissions,
taxes, spread paid, slippage, temporary and permanent impact) and one measure of turnover, per
horizon and annualised. Listing the costs and their windows is design; building the counter is
execution.

**Actions.**
- List every line of cost that applies to the signal: per [venue](../../glossary.md#venue), per instrument, per size.
- Define the windows of measurement (before, during and after execution) and the conventions of
  computation, for example the decision price as base.
- Build a gross-to-net counter that returns, for each trade or day, the gross result, the cost in
  detail, and the net result.

**KPI.** Coverage of costs: the share of costs the measure captures, > 95% targeted; an alert
below 90%. Stability of turnover: relative standard deviation of the monthly turnover below an
internal threshold.

**Guardrail.** The risk is double counting, with benchmarks mixed. Test: an independent
reconciliation of gross − Σ costs − net, with a tolerance < 1 bp per day.

**Output.** Standard table "gross, costs per component, net", with the turnover per period →
step 2.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Estimate how costs grow with size and speed</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Capture the non-linearity: costs do not rise step by step, they run away. Building the
scenarios and fitting the elasticity is design; splitting fixed, variable and impact costs is
execution.

**Actions.**
- Build a grid of scenarios: capital, participation, speed (turnover), density of [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity).
- For each scenario, estimate the total cost in bps and extract an elasticity, for example an
  exponent α > 1 for a super-linear extra cost.
- Separate fixed from variable costs, and the share of impact, which is the most sensitive to
  size.

**KPI.** Cost exponent α below an operating threshold, for example α ≤ 1.2 in the green zone.
Error of the cost forecast, ex ante against ex post, below a threshold, for example 15% in median.

**Guardrail.** The risk is extrapolating outside the observed range. Test: a back-check by deciles
of size; on a systematic bias, narrow the domain of validity.

**Output.** Cost functions of capital, participation and turnover, and the cost exponent α per
universe → step 3.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Link the information ratio, the information coefficient and breadth</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Start from the theoretical alpha to estimate the information ratio before frictions,
then apply an execution transfer.

**Actions.**
- Measure, out of sample, the information coefficient (the correlation between forecast and
  outcome at the decision horizon) and the effective breadth (the number of independent bets per
  year).
- Compute the theoretical information ratio ≈ [IC](../../glossary.md#ic) × √breadth, the
  [fundamental law of active management](../../glossary.md#fundamental-law-of-active-management).
- Build a transfer coefficient τ that links the theoretical information ratio to the net one,
  through the functions of cost and turnover of step 2.

**KPI.** τ, the share of the information ratio kept after execution: green when τ ≥ an internal
threshold, for example ≥ 0.6. Slope of the net information ratio against cost: a controlled
decline, no early collapse.

**Guardrail.** The risk is an illusory breadth, through hidden correlations. Test: dimension
reduction or aggregation in time, to estimate an effective breadth, not a nominal one.

**Output.** The relation net [IR](../../glossary.md#ir) (capital, participation, turnover) = τ × theoretical IR → step 4.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Draw the capacity curve and choose the operating point</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Move from theory to the numbers of operation.

**Actions.**
- Generate the curve of net P&L and net information ratio against capital (and against
  participation), with zones: green (scalable), orange (caution), red (alpha destroyed).
- Define a criterion of optimum: for example the maximum net information ratio, the maximum net
  P&L per unit of risk, or a threshold of return on execution costs.
- Choose an operating point on the shoulder of the curve, not on its fragile summit, then a ramp:
  steps of size, each with a sanity check.

**KPI.** Net information ratio at the target size ≥ the internal standard, for example 1.0 or
1.5. [Return on execution costs](../../glossary.md#return-on-execution-costs) = net P&L / costs ≥
a threshold, for example ≥ 1.5. Marginal elasticity: marginal cost ≤ marginal gain in the green
zone.

**Guardrail.** The risk is over-exploiting illiquid pockets. Test: "step forward, step back" on
the ramp: increase, check, retreat on drift.

**Output.** Target size, target turnover, budget of costs (bps per day and % of gross), target
return on execution costs → step 5.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Stress the capacity and set the safety envelope</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Make sure the size holds outside ideal weather.

**Actions.**
- Stress the curve with market regimes (volatility, liquidity, spreads), rules of microstructure
  (suspensions, tick sizes) and latencies.
- Define a safety envelope: tolerances of degradation (for example +X bps of costs, −Y% of volume)
  that trigger slowing down or a reduction.

**KPI.** Gap between net and expected results, live or simulated, below a threshold. Drift of
costs (realised − forecast) per regime below a threshold.

**Guardrail.** The risk is reacting too slowly. Test: alarms in time (T90 / T95) when a threshold
is crossed, with predefined automatic actions.

**Output.** Table of tolerances (green, orange and red thresholds) and a
[runbook](../../glossary.md#runbook) of adjustment: reduce participation, slow down, withdraw a
universe → step 6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · The operating contract, and the handover to sections 5 and 6</b> — Design task + Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Freeze what is allowed, at what speed, under which [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail). Writing the contract is
design; defining the live gauges is execution.

**Actions.**
- Write the operating contract: target size, ramp, maximum turnover, budget of costs, expected τ,
  minimum return on execution costs, safety envelope, rules of retreat.
- Define the live gauges for section 6: realised against expected costs, the current net
  information ratio, τ live, the return on execution costs live, and an alarm when gross and net
  come apart.

**KPI.** Respect of the contract: the time spent in the green zone ≥ the target %. Reaction time,
from alert to action, within its service level.

**Guardrail.** The risk is slow drifts that no one sees. Test: periodic reviews, weekly or
monthly, and targeted [replays](../../glossary.md#replay) after incidents.

**Output.** Operating contract, [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) dashboard and [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification) procedure → S4.10 (the
promotion gate), S6 (live gauges), S7.8 (capital and cost steering).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Specification of costs and turnover: conventions, units, windows, quality checks.
- Functions of cost elasticity, with their domain of validity: capital, participation, turnover.
- Measures of the information coefficient and effective breadth, and τ, defined and traceable.
- Capacity curve (net P&L and net information ratio against capital and participation), with its
  zones.
- Operating contract: target size and ramp, maximum turnover, budget of costs, minimum return on
  execution costs, expected τ, safety envelope and runbook.
- KPI dashboard, live and backfilled, with its alarms and thresholds.
- Log of decisions (hypotheses, trade-offs, exclusions) and the requalification procedure.
