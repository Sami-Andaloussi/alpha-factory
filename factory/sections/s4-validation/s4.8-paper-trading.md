# S4.8 — Orchestrated paper trading & operational guardrails

## TL;DR

Proves that the whole chain, from signal to fill, lives connected to the market with no P&L at risk: controlled latencies, near-zero rejections, complete replayable logs, credible paper fills.
Success is a 20–30 day campaign over at least 2 regimes, with no kill switch fired, rejections below 0.1% and a drift against the simulator and cost analysis below its threshold.
This is the factory's campaign, distinct from the lab's short paper run; its stabilisation dossier goes to the promotion gate (S4.10).

## Purpose

Prove that the whole chain, from the signal to the fill, lives connected to the market without
putting P&L at risk: latencies under control, rejections close to zero, complete and replayable
logs, credible paper fills (queue, priority, latency), and an execution [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) in line with the
expectations that the [execution simulator](../../glossary.md#execution-simulator) and cost analysis set. This is the factory's orchestrated
campaign of [paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading), with [guardrails](../../glossary.md#guardrail), before any capital.
It is distinct from the lab's short paper run, which proves that a strategy behaves on fresh data as
in its [backtest](../../glossary.md#backtest).

**Success looks like:** a multi-day campaign that holds its service levels, with no
[kill switch](../../glossary.md#kill-switch) fired, and a
[stabilisation dossier](../../glossary.md#stabilisation-dossier) that authorises the promotion to
live.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The as-of API of section 1 brings the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) market data
(the ticks and aggregations the fill engine needs), with the [reference data](../../glossary.md#reference-data) of [venues](../../glossary.md#venue) and brokers:
states, reasons for rejection, trading hours, micro-rules. The selection report and the experiment
passport bring the strategy's frozen parameters: version, [seed](../../glossary.md#seed), configuration. The execution
profile (S4.6) and the cost attribution report (S4.7) are the truth of comparison: the expected
costs and profiles of impact. The stabilisation dossier holds the complete, replayable logs, the
[KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) dashboards, the root-cause analyses, the matrix of guardrails and the decision of the campaign;
section 5 receives its handover through the [promotion gate](../../glossary.md#promotion-gate) of [S4.10](s4.10-promotion-gates.md).
Monitoring receives the alerts "validated under fire" with their thresholds, the schemas of logs
and the templates of root-cause analysis; S6.7 keeps the runbooks. The gaps observed go back to the
simulator and cost analysis for recalibration (impact, [capacity](../../glossary.md#capacity), costs), as feedback.

**Constraints.**
- Consistency in time: one [reference clock](../../glossary.md#reference-clock), the maximum skew documented, timestamps at entry and
  at exit.
- Formats: a versioned schema of events; normalised states of orders; identifiers correlated from
  decision to order to fill.
- Security: the shadow chain is totally isolated from live, with "no live send" controls in place.

**Service levels:**
- Latency: an end-to-end service level, with P95 and P99 defined and watched per venue and [regime](../../glossary.md#regime).
- Market events: IPOs, suspensions and delistings integrated at T90 / [T95](../../glossary.md#t95); an alert when exceeded;
  a freeze when the state of the world is ambiguous.

</details>

## Gates

**Entry.** Start only when:
- S4.1 to S4.4 are validated: clean experiments, statistical evidence;
- the execution simulator and cost analysis are available and set the expectations;
- versions and parameters are frozen;
- the guardrails are defined, with dry tests.

**Exit.** Verdict *pass*, the promotion from paper to live, when all of these hold:
- a campaign of 20–30 days covering ≥ 2 [regimes](../../glossary.md#regime);
- P99 latency below its thresholds; rejections < 0.1%;
- 0 kill switch in normal conditions;
- the drift between paper and the simulator and cost analysis below its threshold (median and
  P95);
- 100% replayability of a witness day;
- alerts with a satisfactory ratio of signal to noise;
- the stabilisation dossier signed by sections 5 and 6 and by governance.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Run paper trading again when:
- the infrastructure changes: broker, venue, router, data;
- market rules change: tick, matching, trading hours;
- monitoring exceeds its thresholds: drift, latency, rejections, capacity;
- the model, its parameters or its [features](../../glossary.md#feature) change significantly;
- an operational incident occurs, or a new capacity (size, universe) is sought.

Monitoring (S6) receives the list of alerts validated under fire, their thresholds, the schemas of
logs and the templates of root-cause analysis. Lifecycle (S7) receives the criteria of
[requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification), the calendar of periodic tests, and the matrix "change → paper required".

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · Frame the campaign and freeze the versions</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Freeze the scope and the contract of performance before connecting anything.

**Actions.**
- Define the universe (instruments, venues), the time windows, the regimes targeted (calm,
  agitated) and the routes of orders targeted.
- List the metrics and their provisional tolerances: end-to-end latency (median, P95, P99),
  rejections, spam, drift of paper against the simulator and cost analysis, simulated capacity.
- Freeze the versions (code, reference data, parameters, seeds), with the rules of logging and of
  numbering versions.

**KPI.** Coverage of the cases ≥ the scope; residual ambiguities = 0; versions and parameters
frozen and traced.

**Guardrail.** A checklist of completeness, and a cross review before launch.

**Output.** Campaign charter and matrix of tolerances → steps 2 to 6, S6 (alerts).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · A shadow chain, instrumented bit for bit</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Run the same stack (decision → [pre-trade checks](../../glossary.md#pre-trade-check) → routing → acknowledgements) as a ghost,
with no real order, timestamped end to end and replayable to the millisecond: the chain runs in
[shadow mode](../../glossary.md#shadow-mode).

**Actions.**
- Connect the shadow order logic, isolated from the live book: no real channel is shared.
- Normalise the formats of orders and states; correlate decision, order and fill through a unique
  trace ID.
- Log everything: decisions, [snapshots](../../glossary.md#snapshot) and the useful deltas, acknowledgements and timeouts,
  network errors, versions, seeds, configuration.
- A contract of timestamps: one source of time, the maximum skew documented, a timestamp at entry
  and at exit.

**KPI.** Latency from decision to acknowledgement (median, P95, P99) stable; a schema of states
with no gap; completeness of the logs = 100% on the critical path.

**Guardrail.** Timestamp buffers at the boundaries; strict validation of schemas;
[fail-closed](../../glossary.md#fail-closed) when visibility is lost: when the chain can no longer
be seen, it is cut.

**Output.** Working shadow pipeline, schema of events and a tool of bit-for-bit
[replay](../../glossary.md#replay) → steps 3 to 6, S6, S7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · A credible engine of paper fills</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Simulate fills without cheating: queue and priority, available depth, round-trip
latency, partial fills, cancel and replace, suspensions and halts.

**Actions.**
- Feed the engine with the state of the market at time T (a snapshot, with the rules of priority
  and latency) and the microstructure of each venue (ticks, time steps, matching, suspensions).
- Model the waiting and the lifetime of orders, partial fills, and temporary and permanent impact
  where the evaluation of drift needs it, without guessing the future.
- Keep a journal of fill decisions: why an order was filled, and why not.

**KPI.** Share of credible fills ≈ 100% (queue and latency respected); distributions of lifetimes
and share of partial fills consistent with the observed [liquidity](../../glossary.md#liquidity);
structural gaps against the expected books explained.

**Guardrail.** Contradictory unit tests (priorities inverted, depth limited, latency injected); the
naive rule "price touched = filled" is forbidden.

**Output.** Calibrated engine of paper fills and journal of fills → step 4, S6, S7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Guardrails in numbers, and kill switches</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Put limits in numbers that can be measured, with their automatic actions.

**Actions.**
- Fix the budgets: [slippage](../../glossary.md#slippage) against cost analysis, drift of paper
  against the simulator and cost analysis (median, P95), end-to-end latency (median, P95, P99),
  rate of rejections, throughput of orders and anti-spam, drift of positions.
- Define the actions (slow down, isolate, cut) and a protocol of human re-arming: who, how, with
  what proof.
- Test the triggers dry: fire [drills](../../glossary.md#drill).

**KPI.** 0 kill switch in normal conditions; drifts and budgets under their thresholds; an
acceptable rate of false-positive alerts.

**Guardrail.** Sticky alerts, which need an acknowledgement; detection, decision and action kept
separate; a loop of audit.

**Output.** Matrix threshold → action → proof, and the results of the tests → step 5, sections 5
and 6.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Fire drills and tolerance to faults</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Prove that the chain degrades cleanly and alerts relevantly.

**Actions.**
- Inject faults: latency, micro-cuts, timeouts, mass rejections, suspensions, missing or late
  data, clocks out of line.
- Check detection, containment and recovery; score the relevance of the alerts.

**KPI.** Time to detect < its budget; time to recover < its budget; the volume of abnormal orders
contained; no loss of replayability.

**Guardrail.** An automatic quiesce mode on a loop of spam; a documented manual re-arming.

**Output.** Resilience report (tests, reactions, times of detection and recovery) → step 6, S6 (the
catalogue of validated alerts).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · The orchestrated multi-day campaign and its stabilisation review</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Run a campaign that covers several regimes, follow the KPIs continuously, compare paper
with the simulator and cost analysis, and decide: promote, extend or rework.

**Actions.**
- Orchestrate 20–30 days (or an equivalent) with varied regimes; keep the dashboards; run
  root-cause analyses on the drifts.
- Adjust where needed: recalibration, corrections of logs, thresholds.

**KPI.** P99 latency < its thresholds per venue; rejections < 0.1%; 0 gap in the logs; execution
drift under its threshold (median and P95); the limits of risk fully respected.

**Guardrail.** A persistent decoupling of paper from the simulator and cost analysis requires a
root-cause analysis, and a recalibration of the simulator and cost analysis before any new window.

**Output.** Stabilisation dossier (KPIs, drifts, root-cause analyses, decisions) and the sheet of
handover → section 5 (through S4.10), the initial settings of section 6.

</details>

## Deliverables

- Campaign charter: scope, objectives, tolerances.
- Schema of events and states of orders (versioned), the mapping of identifiers, and the contract
  of timestamps.
- Working shadow pipeline, with the proofs of its isolation from live.
- Documented engine of paper fills (rules, priorities, latencies), with its contradictory tests.
- [Observability](../../glossary.md#observability) kit: schemas of logs, trace ID, bit-for-bit replay (tool and runbooks).
- Matrix of guardrails and kill switches (threshold → action → proof), and the results of the fire
  drills.
- Continuous KPI dashboard: latency, rejections, drift, quality of fills, completeness of logs.
- Campaign reports: KPIs per day, drifts, root-cause analyses, remediations. They also document the
  structure of the experiments, their statistical significance and the handling of multiple tests.
- Stabilisation dossier and handover: contracts of latency and rejections, thresholds for
  monitoring, conditions of promotion and [rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback).
- Replacement plan: how to substitute a module of paper trading, fills or observability without
  breaking the chain.
