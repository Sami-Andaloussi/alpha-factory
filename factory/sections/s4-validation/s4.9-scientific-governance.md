# S4.9 — Scientific governance, traceability & reproducibility

## TL;DR

Makes every result provable, repeatable and defensible: declared before the test, traced during it, filed after it with a standard report.
The same test gives the same result within a known tolerance: any peer finds and replays a key result in 30 minutes at T95, with a reproducibility of at least 98%.
Its certification dossier feeds the promotion gate (S4.10) and gives monitoring its reference versions.

## Purpose

Make sure that every result can be proven, redone and defended. Before any test, what will be done
is declared: the [pre-registration](../../glossary.md#pre-registration). During the test,
everything is traced: data, versions, parameters, [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed), environments. After it, the artefacts are
filed properly, with a standard report. These rules hold for every page of section 4.

**Success looks like:** the same test gives the same result, within a known tolerance, found and
replayed in a few minutes by anyone in the factory.

<details>
<summary><b>What each flow carries, constraints and service levels</b></summary>

The batch registry brings the [point-in-time](../../glossary.md#point-in-time) datasets with their
manifests and their rules of revision (IPOs, suspensions, [corporate actions](../../glossary.md#corporate-action)); the feature registry
the versions of [features](../../glossary.md#feature) and [labels](../../glossary.md#label), with their [fingerprints](../../glossary.md#fingerprint) and descriptions (windows,
latencies); the dossier the hypotheses and plans of experiment to turn into pre-registrations; the
experiment passport the partitions, seeds and logs of each experiment. The rules set here
(pre-registration, identities, seeds and environments, [lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage), standard reports) apply to every
page of section 4: its [backtests](../../glossary.md#backtest), simulations, cost analyses and paper runs carry the manifests
and lineage defined here. The certification dossier goes to the [promotion gate](../../glossary.md#promotion-gate) (S4.10), and
section 5 receives it, with its conditions of use, through S4.10. Monitoring (S6.1) receives the
reference versions, the ground truth against which live and simulated results are compared, and
S6.4 the watchlist of [drift](../../glossary.md#drift): the variables and tolerances to watch.
Lifecycle (S7) receives the statuses (promotable, to review, rejected), the triggers of re-test, the criteria of demotion ([rollback](../../glossary.md#rollback)) and the
frequency of recertification.

**Constraints.**
- A minimal format of manifest, readable by people and by machines.
- One uniform timestamp.
- Tolerances documented in advance.
- No source that is not point-in-time is admissible for certification.

**Service levels:**
- Manifests updated after a critical event (IPO, suspension, a new rule): [T95](../../glossary.md#t95) 24 h; 2 h for a major
  critical event.
- A dossier published after the end of an experiment: T95 48 h.
- Latency of a [replay](../../glossary.md#replay) (step 6): ≤ 30 min at T95.
- A missed service level freezes promotion until the ground-truth reference is up to date; a broken
  format of manifest is rejected automatically at the entry of the [gates](../../glossary.md#gate).

</details>

## Gates

**Entry**, to launch a test. Start only when:
- a complete, timestamped pre-registration sheet exists;
- the upstream manifests (data, features and labels) are available in point-in-time versions;
- the tolerances of the metrics are fixed.

**Exit**, to close and certify. Verdict *pass* when all of these hold:
- the standard report is signed, with the opinions of two reviewers;
- a cold replay succeeds within tolerance;
- the certification dossier is complete: pre-registration, manifests, log of seeds, environment,
  report, reviews, results of the replay;
- the status is published, and sections 5, 6 and 7 are notified.

*Retry* and *fail* follow the [gate verdict](../../glossary.md#gate-verdict) rule.

**Requalification.** Test again when:
- the data changes: schema or source, latencies, massive corrections;
- the environment changes: a new major version of a component of execution;
- monitoring sees a [drift](../../glossary.md#drift) beyond its thresholds: persistent gaps from
  live to simulated;
- a new method affects the validated step, for example a new label or a new rule of aggregation;
- an audit fails: an imperfect replay, a collision of fingerprints, [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage) detected.

Monitoring (S6) receives the variables and tolerances to watch and the reference versions.
Lifecycle (S7) receives the criteria of demotion (rollback) and the frequency of recertification,
for example quarterly or on an event.

## Steps

<details>
<summary><b>1 · A charter of truth and the pre-registration</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Set the rules of the game: what must be written before an experiment runs.

**Actions.**
- Define one template of pre-registration: a falsifiable objective, the universe, the [in-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample)
  and [out-of-sample](../../glossary.md#in-sample-out-of-sample) periods, the metrics, the thresholds
  of acceptance and rejection, the count of trials, the rules of aggregation, and the assumptions,
  stated explicitly.
- Decide the tolerances of equality of results, for example a band on the metrics within which a
  rerun is accepted.
- Establish the rule "no test without pre-registration".

**KPI.** Share of pre-registrations made before execution ≥ 95%; mean gap between the declared
thresholds and the criteria actually used = 0.

**Guardrail.** A random weekly audit that compares the sheet with the real run; an alert when the
drift exceeds 5%.

**Output.** Timestamped, signed pre-registration sheet → the Strategy Lab (the frame of tests),
validation (all of section 4), internal peer review.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2 · Identity and fingerprint of the artefacts: ID, version, hash</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Guarantee that every piece of data, code, model, label and report has a stable identity
that can be checked.

**Actions.**
- Design a scheme of identifiers (experiment, dataset, features, labels, model, report) that
  includes a timestamp, a semantic version and a [fingerprint](../../glossary.md#fingerprint), a
  hash of the content.
- Define the rules for incrementing versions (major, minor, patch) and the link from parent to
  child, the logical [lineage](../../glossary.md#lineage).
- Impose the minimal packaging of an artefact: a readable manifest with its metadata, fingerprint
  and dependencies.

**KPI.** Coverage of fingerprints ≥ 99% of the artefacts; collisions of fingerprints = 0; complete
links from parent to child ≥ 98%.

**Guardrail.** A test of integrity: fingerprints recomputed at random; a failure blocks promotion.

**Output.** Registry of identities and format of manifest → all of section 4 (backtests, the
simulator, cost analysis), monitoring (reference truths), lifecycle (certification).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>3 · Controlled determinism: seeds and frozen environments</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Remove ghost effects: the same libraries, the same parameters and the same seeds give the
same results.

**Actions.**
- Formalise the policy of [seeds](../../glossary.md#seed): generation, locking, recording per
  component.
- Define the descriptor of environment (operating system, dependencies, numerically precise
  configuration) as a versioned artefact.
- Require every run to reference explicitly its seeds, its descriptor of environment and the
  manifest of its data.

**KPI.** Share of runs replayed identically, within tolerance, over 10 draws ≥ 98%; runs without a
log of seeds = 0.

**Guardrail.** A daily "mirror test": one run of the day relaunched cold; an alert when a gap in a
metric exceeds the tolerance.

**Output.** Log of seeds and versioned descriptor of environment per run → replay and audit (step
6), [paper trading](../../glossary.md#paper-trading) (simulated against live), monitoring.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>4 · Registry of artefacts and end-to-end traceability: data and model lineage</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Be able to explain who did what, when and with what, from the raw source to the report.

**Actions.**
- Establish a central registry of artefacts (point-in-time datasets, features and labels, code,
  models, reports), each carrying a manifest and its fingerprint.
- Define the minimal graph of lineage: each experiment points to its exact inputs (versions of
  data, features, labels) and to its outputs.
- Add a log of experiments (who, when, why) and a log of assumptions.

**KPI.** Completeness of the lineage per experiment ≥ 97%; experiments without a point-in-time
dataset = 0.

**Guardrail.** A test against [leakage](../../glossary.md#leakage): every source is checked
point-in-time; in doubt, the result is marked "not promotable".

**Output.** Browsable graph of lineage, and the logs → peer review (step 5), monitoring (reference
truths), lifecycle (the certification dossier).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>5 · Standard reports and peer review</b> — Design task</summary>

**Goal.** Force an honest reading: what was tested, how, the results, the limits, and what we would
do differently.

**Actions.**
- Design one template of report: the question, the protocol (copied from the pre-registration),
  the data and its version, the results, the uncertainties, the limits, a check of
  [robustness](../../glossary.md#robustness), the decisions.
- Set up a circuit of review: at least two outside readers, with defined roles (author, reviewer,
  approver) and a service level on the delay.
- Document the answers to criticism and the corrective actions, going back to step 1 or 4 where
  needed.

**KPI.** Median delay of review ≤ 3 business days; reports with a "limits" section = 100%; actions
after review closed ≥ 95%.

**Guardrail.** An occasional "blind test": a reviewer receives only the protocol and the numeric
outputs, without the storytelling.

**Output.** Signed report, opinions of review and list of actions → the gates of S4.10, section 5
(through S4.10), section 7.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>6 · Cold replay and the certification dossier</b> — Execution task</summary>

**Goal.** Prove that any peer can replay a key result without help and reach the same verdict.

**Actions.**
- Define the protocol of [replay](../../glossary.md#replay): from an experiment ID, find the
  manifests, rebuild the environment, relaunch, compare with the tolerances.
- Package everything into a [certification dossier](../../glossary.md#certification-dossier):
  pre-registration, manifests, log of seeds, descriptor of environment, report, opinions of review,
  results of the replay.
- Publish the status (promotable, to review, rejected), and notify monitoring (the ground-truth
  references) and lifecycle (the status and the conditions of use).

**KPI.** T95 time to find and replay a key result ≤ 30 min;
[reproducibility](../../glossary.md#reproducibility) ≥ 98% within tolerance; median gap from
backtest to rerun ≤ the tolerance fixed in step 1.

**Guardrail.** A monthly audit: a sample of dossiers replayed by a different team; a failure
triggers [requalification](../../glossary.md#requalification).

**Output.** Complete certification dossier and its status, public inside the factory → section 5
(handover, through S4.10), S6 (references of live against simulated), S7 (promotion or return).

</details>

## Deliverables

- Charter of truth, and a short, useful template of pre-registration.
- Scheme of identities and fingerprints, and the registry of artefacts.
- Policy of seeds, and the versioned descriptor of environment.
- Registry of lineage, log of experiments and log of assumptions.
- Template of the standard report, and the process of review: roles, service levels.
- Protocol of replay, with an automatic check of the tolerances.
- Model certification dossier, [KPI](../../glossary.md#kpi) dashboard (steps 1 to 6) and the procedure of requalification.
